# Content Engine

## Principle

Content is canon in motion.

Do not create disposable content.

Every asset should extract from the Living Canon and feed back into it.

## Core series

### The First Threshold

Eight Passages in Dream Coherence. Long-form public works, public-first and course-reading-second. Distinct from daily atomic recognitions. Governed by `canon/book/FIRST_THRESHOLD_PASSAGE_CYCLE.md`. Not produced through the weekly expansion flow.

### Dream Notes

Daily short recognitions.

### Threshold

Weekly contemplative short video/reel.

Name collision (OPEN): "Threshold" also names the iOS app and sits inside "The First Threshold." Decide whether this series is renamed.

### The Waking Dream

Daily life as dream practice.

### Night Practice

Sleep, dreams, recall, clear light, incubation.

### Golden Thread

Dharma, vocation, destiny, soul-song, contribution.

### Beauty Reveals Coherence

Aesthetic contemplations.

## Weekly minimum

- 5 short posts
- 3 reels
- 1 newsletter
- 1 longer essay or founder letter

## Weekly strong cadence

- 10 short posts
- 5 reels
- 2 carousels
- 1 newsletter
- 1 long essay
- 1 audio

## Founder input

One recognition per week.

- voice note,
- dream,
- field note,
