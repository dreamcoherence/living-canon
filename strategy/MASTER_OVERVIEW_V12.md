## The Seven Major v1.2 Changes

1. **Crystallizing language.** The "locked" framing is replaced throughout with crystallizing. Three classes of content (Recognition / Disciplines / Specifics) sit in the files as crystallizing / refining / forming. The architecture is alive. Structure and flow inform each other in a virtuous feedback loop.

2. **Outcome-anchored phases.** Phases no longer have calendar deadlines. Each phase has exit conditions that must be met. The orienting revenue arc: ~$50K by end of 2026, ~$300K annual run-rate by 2027, ~$1M annual run-rate by 2028, ~$10M cumulative or annual by 2030. "Calendar tyranny" named as a failure mode.

3. **Comprehensive lineage expansion.** The lineage map is restructured into four concentric circles (Spine / Primary Methodological Inheritance / Convergent Edge / Background Philosophical Ground). Jung, Steiner, Gurdjieff, Aurobindo and the Mother, the Hermetic tradition, and Corbin's imaginal tradition get full lineage figure treatments in Circle Two. Bergson, James, Plotinus, Whitehead get background mention in Circle Four.

4. **Subtle body as a working dimension.** A new file articulates the five-layer model (physical / etheric / astral / mental / witness/causal) drawing from Kashmir Shaiva, Steiner's fourfold human, Daoist neidan, Gurdjieff's kesdjan body, Hermetic correspondence, Tibetan dream yoga. The subtle body terminology is added to the lexicon and the five-layer principle is integrated into the field ontology.

5. **Astral body as the distinctive working territory.** Of the five layers, the astral body is named as Dream Coherence's most distinctive working territory. The dream practice that distinguishes the work from other contemporary contemplative protocols is fundamentally astral body practice. The work claims this middle territory deliberately.

6. **Discernment discipline named explicitly.** Working with the depth of lineage requires specific discernment. What is held / what is filtered / what is held with care, articulated explicitly for Steiner (without anthroposophy-as-system), Gurdjieff (without school-cult structure), Aurobindo (without sectarian ashram inheritance), Hermetic tradition (without occult-society apparatus), and the subtle body literature (without Victorian theosophy and New Age vagueness).

7. **The register of the work named.** The work operates at the register of Jung / Steiner / Gurdjieff / Aurobindo for a contemporary context. Every chapter, audio piece, essay, and offering is calibrated against this register. When work falls short, it is rebuilt.
