# Revenue Flywheel

## Purpose

Clarify how Dream Coherence becomes prosperous without making prosperity the public trunk.

## The clean flywheel

Recognition
→ Trust
→ Practice
→ Felt Change
→ Invitation
→ Purchase
→ Delivery
→ Testimony
→ Recurring Relationship
→ Revenue
→ Founder Freedom
→ Better Canon
→ Stronger Recognition

## Explanation

### Recognition

A person encounters a line, reel, essay, or audio and feels something true.

Recognition is the first conversion event.

### Trust

Recognition repeated over time becomes trust.

Trust is built through consistency, beauty, usefulness, restraint, and integrity.

### Practice

Trust deepens when the person tries a simple practice.

This is why the free lead magnet matters.

### Felt Change

Practice produces a shift: better dream recall, a clearer evening, a calmer morning, a sense of orientation, an insight, a symbolic connection.

Felt change is the bridge from audience to buyer.

### Invitation

The paid offer appears as the next natural threshold.

No pressure.

No hype.

The person has felt enough to want the deeper container.

### Purchase

Purchase is reciprocity formalized.

The person exchanges money for a structured transformation.

### Delivery

The product must overdeliver.

A beautiful promise with weak delivery destroys the field.

### Testimony

Real transformation produces language, screenshots, testimonials, referrals, and trust assets.

### Recurring Relationship

After the first product, the person should have a next home:

- membership,
- deeper cohort,
- retreat,
- advanced stewardship.

### Revenue

Revenue becomes stable when relationship continues.

### Founder Freedom

Revenue buys time, support, tools, design, production, and space.

### Better Canon

Founder freedom returns to deeper writing, practice, and vision.

### Stronger Recognition

A deeper canon produces stronger recognitions, and the cycle rises.

## Founder workload strategy

AI and systems handle multiplication.

Founder handles recognition, voice, teaching, and approval.

The business becomes lighter as recurring relationship increases.
