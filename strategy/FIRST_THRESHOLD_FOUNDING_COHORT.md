## Promise

Learn to recognize the dreamlike nature of experience, cultivate attunement across day and night, and begin discerning the Golden Thread of your life.

## Format recommendation

Hybrid.

- 4 live sessions
- self-paced audio practices
- dream journal
- weekly prompts
- private community or comment thread
- founder transmission
- testimonials collected throughout

## Curriculum

### Week 1: Recognition

Reality as dreamlike. Awareness as first fact. Perception as participation.

### Week 2: Attunement

Body, attention, state, identity. How life changes as perception changes.

### Week 3: Night

Dream recall, evening threshold, morning bridge, lucid dreaming as laboratory.

### Week 4: Golden Thread

Dharma, destiny, contribution, flourishing, participation, next life architecture.
