# Weekly Recognition Engine

## Principle

One recognition becomes many expressions.

Nothing is created once.

## Weekly flow

### Founder input

One recognition, story, dream, or field note.

### AI expansion

- 1 essay
- 1 newsletter
- 1 podcast/audio outline
- 3 reels
- 2 carousels
- 5 X posts
- 5 Instagram captions
- 1 practice
- 1 course fragment
- 1 repository update

## Weekly cadence

Monday: recognition and essay.

Tuesday: short posts.

Wednesday: carousel.

Thursday: reels.

Friday: practice/audio.

Saturday: newsletter.

Sunday: review, metrics, repository update.

## Founder time

Target: 10–60 minutes per week.

## AI instruction

Do not ask for seven ideas.

Ask for one true seed.
