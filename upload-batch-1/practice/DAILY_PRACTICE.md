# Daily Dream Coherence Practice

## Minimum viable practice

Less than five minutes.

### Morning

Before the day takes possession, return to the inner state.

Ask:

What is the Golden Thread asking today?

### Midday

At one charged or vivid moment, recognize:

This is a dream.

Then ask:

What becomes available when this is met lucidly?

### Evening

Before sleep, ask:

What entered the field today?

Choose three charged or meaningful moments.

Feel beneath the story.
Set them down.
Enter the night clear.

## Fuller practice

### Morning

- Dream capture.
- Body arrival.
- Witness practice.
- Identity alignment.
- Golden Thread inquiry.

### Midday

- Reality check.
- Attention discernment.
- One aligned action.

### Evening

- Day review.
- Revision.
- Dream incubation.
- Sleep of Clear Light.

## Principle

Consistency over intensity.

The minimum protects continuity.

The fuller practice grows when capacity grows.
