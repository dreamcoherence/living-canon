# Practice OS

## Purpose

Dream Coherence must become a real method, not a vibe.

## Five practice stages carried from V1

### 1. Body Coherence

Stabilize the vessel.

Sleep, breath, body scan, downshift, food, light, movement.

### 2. Attention Coherence

Train lucidity.

Witness practice, reality checks, attention redirection, thought observation.

### 3. Identity Coherence

Repattern the assumed self.

I Am inquiry, assumption practice, revision, future-state embodiment, self-concept audit.

### 4. Dream Coherence

Bring sleep dreams into the path.

Dream journaling, incubation, reality checks, symbol tracking, lucid dreaming preparation.

### 5. Reality Coherence

Live from the integrated field.

Synchronicity tracking, aligned action, decision by body and field, artistic transmission, wealth embodiment.
