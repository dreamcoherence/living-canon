# Dream Yoga Protocol

## Purpose

Translate the dream yoga inheritance into Dream Coherence practice without falsely claiming lineage authority.

## Stage 1: Recall

Train the morning bridge.

Before movement, speech, or phone, turn toward the night.

Write image, feeling, fragment, symbol, or absence.

## Stage 2: Day Lucidity

Use waking experience to train dream recognition.

At vivid moments:

This is a dream.

## Stage 3: Evening Clearing

The day becomes the seed of the night.

Review charged moments.
Rename what can be renamed.
Set down what can be set down.

## Stage 4: Intention

Before sleep:

May I recognize the dream as dream.
May I remember what matters.
May the night show what is ready to be seen.

## Stage 5: Lucid Dream Practice

When lucidity arises:

- stabilize,
- look at hands or ground,
- remember the dreamlike nature,
- ask the dream what it is showing,
- practice shaping gently,
- orient toward alchemy, beauty, and Golden Thread, not mere control.

## Stage 6: Clear Light Orientation

Advanced.

Between waking and dream, or dream and dream, there may be image-free awareness.

Do not force.

Incline, soften, release.

## Safety

Do not overemphasize advanced sleep practices for beginners.

Begin with recall, body, grounding, and simple continuity.
