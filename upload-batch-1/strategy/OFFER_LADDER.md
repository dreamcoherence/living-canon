## Product law

No product should be built unless it answers:

1. What threshold does this help someone cross?
2. What does the buyer experience in 7 days?
3. What does the buyer experience in 30 days?
4. How does it connect to the larger method?
5. Can AI produce 80% of the operational material?
6. What must the founder personally supply?
