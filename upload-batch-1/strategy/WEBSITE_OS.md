# Website OS

## Purpose

The website should be a threshold, not a brochure.

## Homepage job

Within seconds, a visitor should feel:

I have found something unlike ordinary spiritual content.

## Homepage structure

1. Hero recognition.
2. Brief orientation.
3. What Dream Coherence is.
4. The Golden Thread.
5. First practice / lead magnet.
6. Founding Cohort waitlist.
7. Canon / essays.
8. Newsletter.

## Current recommendation

Launch simple.

Do not wait for a massive site.

The homepage, newsletter capture, lead magnet, and waitlist matter most.

## Website voice

Brand Voice, with occasional Source Transmission.

Not too much explanation.

The site should make the field felt.
