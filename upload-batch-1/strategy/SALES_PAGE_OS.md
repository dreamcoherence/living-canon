# Sales Page OS

## Purpose

Turn an offering into a dignified invitation.

## Sales page structure

1. Recognition opening.
2. Name the longing.
3. Name the confusion or gap.
4. Introduce Dream Coherence.
5. Name the threshold.
6. Describe the transformation.
7. Show the curriculum.
8. Show what is included.
9. Explain who it is for.
10. Explain who it is not for.
11. Include safety and integrity notes.
12. Name price.
13. Invite enrollment.
14. Answer objections.
15. Close with beauty.

## Tone

- clear
- grounded
- elegant
- confident
- invitational
- never desperate
- never hype-driven

## Core rule

People should not feel sold.

They should feel recognized, oriented, and invited.
