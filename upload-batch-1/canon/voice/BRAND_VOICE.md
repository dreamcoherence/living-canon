# Brand Voice

## Formula

Brand Voice = lucid + exact + contemplative + practical + quietly mythic.

## What it is

- calm
- precise
- direct
- grounded
- elegant
- intimate without oversharing
- mystical without vagueness
- practical without becoming ordinary

## What it is not

- hyperbolic
- guru-like
- academic
- therapeutic jargon
- manifestation influencer copy
- generic meditation app copy
- motivational speaking
- AI-generated mysticism

## Three modes

### Public Entry

Simple, accessible, quotable.

### Inner Teaching

Newsletter, course, audio scripts, deeper essays.

### Source Transmission

Atmospheric, slow, symbolic, contemplative. Used sparingly.

## Transmission standard

Every piece of public writing must do at least one:

1. Clarify reality.
2. Shift state.
3. Name a pattern.
4. Give a practice.
5. Open perception.
6. Increase agency.
7. Return attention to the body.
8. Point toward the dream.

## Brand aphorisms to preserve

- The dream is not elsewhere.
- The body is the gate.
- Attention is the first instrument.
- What visits you in sleep may be what governs you by day.
- The future enters first as a state.
- Lucidity is intimacy without captivity.
- The symbol repeats until it is received.
- Beauty is coherence made visible.
- Magic begins where self-deception ends.
- Sleep is not absence. It is rehearsal.
- The body must learn the future before life can confirm it.
