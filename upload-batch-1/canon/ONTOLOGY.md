# Ontology

## Foundational view

Reality is not encountered as a dead material object.

Reality is encountered as experience: symbolic, responsive, participatory, consciousness-saturated, shaped by attention, interpreted through identity, stabilized through the body, and disclosed through state.

Dream Coherence begins from the view that life is not merely happening to the practitioner. Life is something the practitioner is participating in.

## Dream principle

Dream does not mean unreal.

Dream means a world made of experience and lived from within.

The waking world and sleeping dream are not identical, but they share structural features:

- both appear within awareness,
- both are shaped by attention,
- both are interpreted symbolically,
- both are colored by emotion,
- both are organized through identity,
- both contain recurring motifs,
- both become more workable when recognized as dreamlike.

## Awareness principle

All states appear within awareness.

Waking appears.
Dream appears.
Sleep appears as remembered absence or clear presence.
Emotion appears.
Thought appears.
Identity appears.
World appears.

The practitioner learns to recognize the field in which the state appears, not only the state itself.

## Identity principle

Identity selects perception.

What one assumes oneself to be determines what is noticed, ignored, possible, threatening, natural, and confirmed.

Identity is not merely belief. It is a configuration of body, attention, memory, emotion, imagination, expectation, and action.

## Body principle

The body is the vessel of dream participation.

A dysregulated body cannot reliably hold subtle perception, identity shift, creativity, prosperity, intimacy, or awakening.

Body coherence is not optional. It is the ground.

## Five-layer model

Dream Coherence operates across five integrated layers:

1. Physical: sensation, posture, breath, sleep, movement.
2. Etheric: vitality, life-force, energetic coherence.
3. Astral: dream, emotion, symbol, vision, imagination.
4. Mental: thought, meaning, belief, interpretation, articulation.
5. Witness/Causal: awareness that knows every layer.

The astral layer is one of Dream Coherence's most distinctive working territories. This is the layer where dream, vision, emotion, symbol, and disciplined imagination operate.

## Goal

The goal is not escape.

The goal is lucid participation.

To live as if the world is real enough to love, dreamlike enough to transform, sacred enough to steward, responsive enough to engage, and mysterious enough to remain humble before.
