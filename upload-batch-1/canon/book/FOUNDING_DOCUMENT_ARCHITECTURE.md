# Founding Document Architecture

## Purpose

The founding document opens the field.

It does not need to contain everything.

It must make Dream Coherence unmistakable.

## Required effect

After reading, someone should feel:

- oriented,
- awake,
- curious,
- inwardly stilled,
- drawn toward practice,
- trusting of the field,
- willing to join the first threshold.

## Recommended form

Shorter than the full Path of Clear Light.

Long enough to establish:

1. Reality as dreamlike.
2. Attunement as faculty.
3. Golden Thread as central inquiry.
4. Day/night continuity.
5. Practice as threshold.
6. Participation as authorship.
7. Invitation into First Threshold.

## Relation to course

The founding document should lead naturally into the 10/10 Founding Cohort.

It is the field-opening artifact.

The course is the structured crossing.
