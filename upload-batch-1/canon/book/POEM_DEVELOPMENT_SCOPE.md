# Dream Coherence. The Poem. Development Scope.

**Version 1.2. May 2026. The first public artifact of the field, positioned as the bridge between the book's interior register and the field's bolder register.**

The poem is the field's first public transmission. It is not a fragment of the book. It is not a sample. It is a standalone artifact — a single, beautifully made piece of writing that exists at its own register and serves a specific function: be the first thing released that signals what the work is.

This file scopes the poem's development: what the poem is, what it does, how it is produced, how it is released, and what its place is in the architecture.

Note on timing: an earlier version of this scope positioned the poem as released "4-8 weeks before book launch" to seed the field for launch. The current architecture is book-launch-agnostic (per `25_STRATEGIC_ARCHITECTURE.md`). The poem releases when it is ready, in the period when the field is established enough to receive it. The book's release happens when it happens. The poem and the book do not depend on each other to land; each is its own complete artifact.

---

## What the Poem Is

A single long-form poem. Working title held open until written. Approximately 60-120 lines, possibly more if the form requires.

The poem transmits the central recognition of Dream Coherence at the highest contemplative register the work allows. It sits in the Sovereign Voice register, possibly more compressed than even the book allows because poetry can do what prose cannot.

The poem is not narrative in conventional sense. It does not tell a story with characters and arc. It is closer to a Rilke *Duino Elegy*, a Mary Oliver meditation, a Rumi recognition rendered in modern English. The reader undergoes something across the read. The poem's structure is the reader's progression through a recognition.

---

## What the Poem Transmits

The poem holds the work's central recognition in compressed form. The reader encounters:

- The opening seeing — what the moment is
- The dream principle — the recognition that experience is dreamlike
- The threshold — the moment of waking inside the dream
- The body — the gate of recognition
- The practitioner's posture — what it is to live from this
- A closing arrival — what is possible from here

The poem can hold the entire arc of the work in 100 lines. This is the poem's particular power.

---

## Voice Register

Sovereign Voice. Possibly the highest register the work ever publishes. The poem follows all the voice axioms (`13_VOICE_AXIOMS.md`) but with poetic license to compress, to use line breaks for rhythm, to repeat for emphasis, to use image where prose would explain.

What the poem does not do:
- Rhyme in a heavy-handed way (occasional rhyme acceptable, scheme rhyme rejected)
- Use forced meter (free verse with internal rhythm)
- Reference specific traditions by name (the recognition is the inheritance of all of them and belongs to none)
- Sound like translated mystical poetry (avoids the Rumi-pastiche register that floods the contemporary contemplative space)
- Include em dashes (per voice axioms)

What the poem does:
- Hold each line so it can stand alone
- Move the reader through a recognition by accumulation
- Use image rather than abstraction
- Use the body's sensations as anchor points
- Carry weight in a few words
- Land at close with a line that can be quoted, remembered, returned to

## Drafting Process

The poem is drafted by the founder, supported by Claude in the project. The process:

**Stage One. The Surfacing.** The founder produces several drafts across weeks, not in a single sitting. The poem surfaces from the founder's lived practice. Claude assists in finding what wants to be said.

**Stage Two. The Compression.** The drafts are compressed. Each line is tested: can it stand alone? Does it earn its place? Long sections are cut. The poem moves toward its final length.

**Stage Three. The Tuning.** The poem is read aloud at every revision. Lines that don't survive being spoken are rewritten. The Voice Guardian agent runs against each pass.

**Stage Four. The Lock.** The founder declares the poem locked. From here it does not change.

Total drafting time: 6-12 weeks. Not rushed.

## Production Forms

The poem releases in three forms, each with its own production standard.

### Form One. Free reading.

The poem is published on dreamcoherence.com on its own page (`/the-poem`). Anyone can read it. Free. No gate.

Production: clean web typography in the brand register. EB Garamond Italic, generous spacing, ample white space around the lines. A single audio link to the reading (Form Two). The page is the most contemplative page on the site.

### Form Two. Audio reading.

The poem is recorded as a single 5-10 minute audio piece. Read aloud — by the founder, or by a chosen narrator with a voice the work resonates with. Production at the highest standard the budget allows. Quiet ambient bed (if any) or pure spoken-word. Released on Spotify, available on all streaming.

The audio is free. The audio file is also embedded on the poem's web page.

### Form Three. Limited edition print.

A small print run of the poem as a physical artifact. 500-2000 copies, depending on demand signals. Hand-bound or premium production. Letterpress or quality offset printing. Heavy paper. Brand colors. The poem set in EB Garamond Italic at the deepest typographic care.

The physical edition is sold at $50-$150 per copy. This is the first paid revenue artifact of Dream Coherence, possibly arriving even before the first paid course offering.

The limited edition is collectible-grade. Once sold out, it is not reprinted (unless a new edition is commissioned later as an artifact distinct from the original).

## Release Timing and Arc

The poem releases when it is ready and when the field is established enough to receive it. Most likely in Phase 1 (Months 9-12), after the website v2 is live, the newsletter has 1K-3K subscribers, and the audio surface has at least 2-3 pieces published.

The release arc spans approximately 4-6 weeks:

**Week 1.** The poem releases on dreamcoherence.com (Form One). No marketing push. A single newsletter announces it: a 200-300 word note saying the poem is live and a link.

**Week 2-3.** The audio release (Form Two) drops. Newsletter announces. Spotify and other audio platforms ingest. The poem begins circulating in the audio surfaces.

**Week 4-6.** The limited edition (Form Three) opens for pre-order. Newsletter announces. Order fulfillment ships approximately 2-3 weeks after pre-orders open.

The book's release happens whenever it happens, independent of the poem. If the book releases shortly after the poem, the two reinforce each other in the field. If the book releases months later, the poem has already established the work's depth and the book arrives into a field that knows what to expect.

The poem and the book are companion artifacts that do not depend on each other.

## Strategic Function

The poem does several things at once.

**It establishes register.** Anyone encountering the poem before the book sees immediately what the work is: contemplative, serious, the inheritor of a contemplative literary tradition, not commercial spirituality. The poem sets expectations correctly.

**It tests the field's response.** The reception of the poem — newsletter forwards, social shares, audio plays, limited edition sell-through — gives data on whether the audience is ready for the book.

**It generates a first artifact for the audience to share.** The poem is more shareable than book chapters can be (the chapters do not exist outside the book). The poem can move freely. People share it. The work spreads.

**It produces a small but real first revenue.** The limited edition generates $25K-$300K depending on print run and pricing. This is the first proof that the work can carry an economy.

**It creates a beautiful first object.** Practitioners who own the limited edition own the first physical artifact of Dream Coherence. This is a long-term brand asset.

## The Discipline

The poem must be excellent. Not "good enough to release." Not "a fine first try." Excellent. Worthy of standing next to the contemplative poets the work resonates with.

This means: the founder writes it. Claude assists with revision and pressure-testing but does not draft the poem. AI-drafted poetry at this register reads as AI poetry. The poem must come from a person who has lived the recognition. Claude's role is to help the founder do what only the founder can do.

If the poem is not excellent when the founder reads it aloud and feels the recognition land — it is rewritten. If it never reaches excellence, it is held until it does. The poem releases when it is ready, not on a schedule.

The poem's quality is the work's calling card to the world. Calibrate accordingly.

---

## Pre-release Discipline

Before the poem releases publicly:

- The poem is locked by the founder
- The audio recording is produced at high standard
- The physical edition is designed and a proof copy is in hand
- The web page is built and tested
- The newsletter announcing it is written and scheduled
- Pre-order systems are tested for the limited edition

No release happens until all of these are verified.

---

## Aftermath

The poem stays live on the website. It remains free. The audio remains free. The limited edition closes when sold out and is not reprinted.

If the poem lands well, additional poems may follow over the years. A collected edition could eventually become a small book of poems (held for Phase 3 or Phase 4).

The poem becomes a touchstone the field can return to. Practitioners revisit it. New visitors encounter it. The work continues to enter the world through it.

---

## The Promise of the Form

The poem is small. The poem is brief. The poem is the first thing released.

By Phase 4 of the build, when the architecture has matured and the wager is in its final reckoning, the poem will still be the same 60-120 lines it always was. New work will have accumulated around it. The book will be in its print runs. The flagship will have run a dozen times. But the poem will still hold the central recognition in the smallest possible vessel.

This is what compressed forms do. They outlast.

The poem is the field's first transmission and possibly its most enduring.
