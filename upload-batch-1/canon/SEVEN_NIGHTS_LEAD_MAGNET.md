# Seven Nights of Dream Coherence

## Purpose

Primary recommended lead magnet.

It creates an immediate practice experience and leads naturally into First Threshold.

## Promise

In seven nights, begin remembering the night, clearing the day before sleep, and recognizing the continuity between waking and dreaming.

## Format

- PDF
- 7 emails
- 3 short audios
- dream journal template
- morning recall sheet
- evening threshold practice

## Seven nights

1. Remember the night.
2. Set the day down.
3. Name the dream signs.
4. Ask the night a question.
5. Recognize the waking dream.
6. Meet the symbol.
7. Carry the thread forward.
