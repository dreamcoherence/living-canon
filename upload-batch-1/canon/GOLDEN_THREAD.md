# The Golden Thread

## Central question

> How does reality uniquely wish to become itself through this life?

## Meaning

The Golden Thread is not generic purpose.

It is the distinct movement of reality through one incarnation.

It may appear as dharma, calling, soul-song, creative necessity, life direction, vocation, service, destiny, or divine consignment.

## The human promise

Dream Coherence helps people become more awake to the unique life they are here to live.

This includes:

- realizing their nature,
- discerning their distinct dharma,
- recognizing the song of their soul,
- finding the work only they can tend,
- participating in the alchemy that gives rise to the greatest grace and flourishing of their incarnation.

## How it is found

Not by force.

Not by branding oneself.

Not by choosing a purpose from the mind.

It is found through attunement: noticing what repeats, what opens, what carries energy, what returns in dreams, what life keeps asking, what beauty reveals, what the body knows, what the heart cannot abandon.

## Product implication

Every major offering should help someone move from confusion to orientation.

Dream Coherence competes with confusion.

Its product is orientation.
