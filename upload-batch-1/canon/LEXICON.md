# Dream Coherence. Lexicon.

**Version 1.3. June 2026. The working definitions of the key terms the work uses. The standalone reference for content production, course material, the book's glossary, and anyone outside the project trying to understand the work. Crystallizing — terms refine as the work matures. v1.3 re-roots Coherence as graded lucidity, the standing degree of lucidity in the waking dream, with the five domains as where it is raised and their alignment as its signature.**

The lexicon holds the operational meanings of the terms the work uses. Each term has a Brand Voice definition (for public-facing surfaces) and, where the term needs more precision, a Sovereign Voice definition (for the book and the deepest transmissions). The two are coherent; they differ in density and register, not in meaning.

Terms are listed alphabetically. The list is not exhaustive — terms are added as the work names new ground. Subtle body terminology is articulated in detail in `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`; brief lexical definitions appear here for cross-reference.

---

## Astral Body

**Brand Voice.** The dream body. The emotional and imaginal body. The layer of the practitioner at which dream, vision, desire, emotion, and imaginative cognition operate. The most distinctive working territory of Dream Coherence.

**Sovereign Voice.** The astral body is the layer of consciousness that experiences the dream, that perceives the imaginal, that holds the emotional field, that participates in symbolic communication. The astral body is what becomes lucid in dream; the astral body is what perceives in vision; the astral body is the substrate of disciplined imagination. See `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md` for the working articulation.

---

## Attention

**Brand Voice.** The instrument by which awareness touches what awareness becomes aware of. The first practical instrument of the work. What attention repeatedly touches becomes more real in experience.

**Sovereign Voice.** Attention is creative because it stabilizes worlds. Untrained attention drifts. Trained attention directs. The discipline of attention is the discipline of what one's life becomes.

---

## Awareness

**Brand Voice.** The screen on which everything appears. The seeing within which thought, sensation, image, and world become noticeable. Not produced by the brain; the medium within which brains appear.

**Sovereign Voice.** Awareness is what is meant by the word *consciousness* when consciousness is recognized as fundamental. Not a function. Not a property of matter. The ground itself.

---

## Body

**Brand Voice.** The first instrument of the practice. The gate through which recognition becomes lived. A dysregulated body cannot hold subtle perception. Practice begins here.

**Sovereign Voice.** The body is awareness embodied as a specific vessel, capable of holding and transmitting what is being recognized. The body is not separate from awareness; the body is one of the forms awareness takes.

---

## Capacity

**Brand Voice.** The cultivated ability of the practitioner to hold, embody, and express the recognition in increasing dimensions of life — presence, perception, embodiment, energy, expression, livelihood, relationship.

**Sovereign Voice.** Capacity is the difference between recognition and lived recognition. Recognition is the seeing. Capacity is the vessel that can hold the seeing across time, body, relationship, and circumstance.

---

## Coherence

**Brand Voice.** Your standing degree of lucidity in the waking dream. From Latin *cohaerere*, to cling together. Not alignment as a static state but lucidity raised into a level of being: how awake you are within the dream of your life. It is built across body, attention, identity, dream, and action, and felt as their alignment. The dream grows responsive to you in proportion to your coherence.

**Sovereign Voice.** Coherence is lucidity that has become a standing condition rather than a passing flicker. The five domains are where it is raised. Their alignment is its signature. The responsiveness of the field is its fruit, and the field answers to being, not to wishing. Coherence is therefore not a switch between asleep and awake but a degree raised one increment at a time, from wherever one stands. It is, in the language of dream, what older work named a level of being.

---

## Dharma

**Brand Voice.** The particular shape the recognition takes in the form of a specific incarnation. The gift the life is here to express. Not the same as career or purpose in the conventional sense; closer to the specific dharma each life is.

**Sovereign Voice.** Dharma is what the one consciousness is doing as this particular form. The recognition that the one is the many, and that each many is a specific expression the one is making. To live one's dharma is to participate consciously in what is already being done through one.

---

## Dream

**Brand Voice.** The total field of lived experience — waking life, sleeping dreams, symbolic pattern, identity, and the responsiveness of the world. Not "the dream world" as opposed to "the real world"; the recognition that experience itself has the structure of dreaming.

**Sovereign Voice.** Dream is the form awareness takes when awareness becomes content. Both waking and sleeping dreams are awareness dreaming itself, at different intensities of identification.

---

## Etheric Body

**Brand Voice.** The energy body. The life-force layer that interpenetrates the physical body and gives it its livingness. The bridge between the physical body and the astral body.

**Sovereign Voice.** The etheric body is the energetic dimension of the physical body, the felt energetic field that practitioners perceive as the work matures. In Daoist terms, the qi body. In Indian terms, the prāṇamaya kosha. See `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`.

---

## Field

**Brand Voice.** The living context in which experience appears and responds. The whole of what is, considered as a single responsive presence rather than as separate objects.

**Sovereign Voice.** The field is awareness as the medium of all appearance. Not background. Not container. The very thing within which and as which all experience occurs.

---

## Identity

**Brand Voice.** The lived assumption of what one is, what is possible, and what life is allowed to become. Not only a belief; a configuration of body, attention, memory, emotion, imagination, and expectation that selects what is perceived.

**Sovereign Voice.** Identity is awareness self-localized as a specific position from which the dream is being experienced. The position is not fixed; identity can revise. Revision of identity is one of the work's primary disciplines.

---

## Lucidity

**Brand Voice.** The capacity to recognize experience while participating in it. Not detachment. Not dissociation. The middle state in which the dream is recognized as the dream and entered fully anyway. Lucidity comes in degrees: a first flicker of recognition, then steadier presence, then agency within the dream. A level raised, not a state switched on.

**Sovereign Voice.** Lucidity is intimacy without captivity. The seeing that does not stop participation. The participation that does not lose the seeing. Lucidity is graded, and its standing degree, raised across the domains of a life, is what the work names coherence.

---

## Practice

**Brand Voice.** The method by which recognition becomes lived. The repeatable discipline that turns insight into capacity. Daily, weekly, lifelong.

**Sovereign Voice.** Practice is the deliberate cultivation of the conditions in which recognition can stabilize. Practice does not produce the recognition; the recognition is already the case. Practice produces the body that can hold it.

---

## Recognition

**Brand Voice.** The seeing of what is already the case. Not learning something new. Not acquiring a state. The noticing of what is already true about the nature of experience.

**Sovereign Voice.** Recognition is the moment awareness sees itself. The moment is structurally simple, but the conditions under which it can occur and stabilize are the work of a life.

---

## State

**Brand Voice.** The integrated condition of body, attention, identity, and feeling at a given moment, from which perception and action arise. Not a passing mood; the configuration the life is operating from.

**Sovereign Voice.** State is the dream's current calibration. What is possible from one state is not possible from another. The cultivation of state is the cultivation of what life can become.

---

## Subtle Body

**Brand Voice.** The layers of the practitioner beyond the gross physical body. In Dream Coherence's working model: etheric, astral, mental, and witness layers, with the physical body as the foundation. The subtle body is real, perceptible to the cultivated practitioner, and work-able through specific practice.

**Sovereign Voice.** The subtle body is the integrated organization of the practitioner across five layers. Different lineages articulate the layers differently; the convergence across traditions on the existence of a subtle dimension is one of contemplative work's most striking confirmations. See `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`.

---

## Subconscious

**Brand Voice.** The dream-engine beneath ordinary waking control. Not merely hidden memory; the active layer that produces nighttime dreams, emotional triggers, repeating patterns, bodily contraction, and symbolic recurrence.

**Sovereign Voice.** The subconscious is the layer of awareness that continues to dream while the waking mind is focused elsewhere. It carries unresolved pattern and unfinished material. The work brings the subconscious into conscious relationship.

---

## Superconscious

**Brand Voice.** The higher ordering intelligence that discloses pattern, direction, and insight beyond the ordinary mind. The layer through which synchronicity, intuition, and recognition arrive.

**Sovereign Voice.** The superconscious is the layer of awareness that knows what the waking mind has not yet seen. It is not separate from the practitioner; it is the practitioner not yet identified with the waking position.

---

## Threshold

**Brand Voice.** A transition point where identity, perception, or state can shift. The moment between waking and sleeping, between one life chapter and another, between one identity and another.

**Sovereign Voice.** Threshold is where the dream's seams become visible. The places where awareness can be noticed because the usual continuity has loosened. Practice often happens at thresholds.

---

## Witness

**Brand Voice.** The awareness that sees thought, emotion, and image without being consumed by them. The internal observer that can be cultivated as a stable position.

**Sovereign Voice.** Witness is awareness recognizing itself as the seer rather than as the seen. The witness is not separate from what is witnessed; the witness is the awareness in which both seer and seen appear.

## Nahua Lineage Terms

These are source-tradition terms the work now holds, drawn from Nahua philosophy (see `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md`). They are reference terms, not Brand Voice terms. The work names them precisely rather than under the marketed umbrella "Toltec."

**Teotl.** The single, self-generating sacred power in ceaseless motion that the Nahua sages held as the nature of reality, continually arranging itself as every form. Not a god. The Mesoamerican convergence with *spanda*, the doctrine of vibration, and with the work's term **Awareness** as the ground in self-expression.

**Nepantla.** The middle, the between. In Nahua metaphysics, where reality is woven from complementary polarities. The indigenous articulation of the work's recognition of awareness as the seeing in the middle between seer and seen. Cross-references **Witness** and **Field**.

**In xochitl in cuicatl.** "Flower and song." The Nahua doctrine that the rooted truth can be spoken only through metaphor, poetry, and paired image. The lineage precedent for the work's discipline of transmission before argument, and for the recognition that words are spells.

**Tlamatinime.** "Those who know." The Nahua sages whose recorded testimony, in the Florentine Codex and the flower-and-song manuscripts, is the source of what survives of this philosophy.

## Cross-references

Some terms relate closely:

- **Awareness** and **Field** name the same recognition from two angles: awareness as the screen, field as the responsive presence.
- **Astral Body**, **Etheric Body**, and **Subtle Body** are articulated in detail in `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`. The lexicon gives the brief definition; the full working articulation lives in the dedicated file.
- **Subconscious** and **Superconscious** are not separate territories; they are layers of the same awareness, differentiated by what they hold (unresolved pattern below, higher ordering above). Both operate within the astral and mental body layers.
- **Dream** and **Coherence** combine in the project's name: the recognition that experience is dreamlike (Dream), and the standing degree of lucidity one holds within it (Coherence).
- **Lucidity** and **Coherence** are root and measure: lucidity is the recognition itself, flickering or sustained; coherence is lucidity raised to a standing level of being across body, attention, identity, dream, and action. Lucidity is the root; coherence is how much of it stands.
- **Practice** and **Recognition** are not the same. Recognition is already the case. Practice cultivates the conditions for the recognition to stabilize.
- **Identity** and **State** are related; identity is the longer-arc configuration, state is the moment-to-moment expression.
- **Dharma** and **Capacity** combine: dharma is what is to be expressed, capacity is what allows the expression.
- **Witness** is the awareness operating at the deepest layer of the subtle body model — Layer Five, the causal/witness layer that observes the activity of all other layers.

## How This Lexicon Is Used

For content production: any term used in brand or book writing is calibrated to its definition here. Drift in usage is corrected.

For course material: the lexicon becomes the glossary practitioners reference.

For the book: the lexicon contributes to a final glossary appendix.

For external use: when someone outside the project asks "what do you mean by [term]?", the lexicon is the source-of-truth.

---

## The Discipline

Precision in terms is precision in transmission. Drift in terms is drift in recognition. The lexicon is the calibration point.

The lexicon grows as the work names new ground. The lexicon does not shrink — terms that have been named are held even when the work moves to less reliance on a specific term.
