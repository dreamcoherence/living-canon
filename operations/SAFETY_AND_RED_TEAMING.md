# Safety and Red Teaming

## Purpose

Dream Coherence touches identity, sleep, dream, imagination, subtle body, and metaphysical perception.

These are powerful territories.

The work must be held with care.

## Universal disclaimer

Dream Coherence is contemplative practice.

It is not medical care, psychiatric care, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

Anyone in acute psychological crisis should seek qualified professional support.

## Safety principles

- Do not destabilize the practitioner.
- Do not promise outcomes.
- Do not encourage dissociation.
- Do not inflate dream content into certainty.
- Do not replace clinical care.
- Do not claim supernatural authority.
- Do not push advanced practices prematurely.

## Warning signs

Pause practice and seek support if there is:

- persistent dissociation or derealization,
- severe mood destabilization,
- distressing dream escalation,
- inability to function,
- obsessive interpretation,
- manic inflation,
- crisis-level despair.

## Product safety

Entry products must be grounding.

Advanced products require stronger framing, screening, or live containment.

## Red Team prompt

Before launching any practice or product, ask:

1. Where could this be unsafe?
2. Where could this be overclaimed?
3. Who is this not for?
4. What warning signs need naming?
5. What support path is needed?
6. Does the promise remain honest?
