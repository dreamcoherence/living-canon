# Safety and Red Teaming

## Purpose

Dream Coherence touches identity, sleep, dream, imagination, subtle body, and metaphysical perception.

These are powerful territories.

The work must be held with care.

## Universal disclaimer

Dream Coherence is contemplative practice.

It is not medical care, psychiatric care, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

Anyone in acute psychological crisis should seek qualified professional support.

## Safety principles

- Do not destabilize the practitioner.
- Do not promise outcomes.
- Do not encourage dissociation.
- Do not inflate dream content into certainty.
- Do not replace clinical care.
- Do not claim supernatural authority.
- Do not push advanced practices prematurely.
- Do not certify liberation or attainment.

## Identity and awareness work

Healthy loosening should increase:

- embodiment;
- relational availability;
- discernment;
- ordinary functioning;
- responsibility;
- flexibility.

It should not produce or validate:

- emotional numbing;
- detached superiority;
- loss of personhood;
- derealization or depersonalization;
- exemption from accountability;
- certainty about other minds;
- cosmic specialness;
- supernatural authority.

What loosens is enclosure, not personhood. Boundaries, memory, judgment, consent, history, and responsibility remain. Detachment, derealization, grandiosity, emotional numbing, and diminished functioning are not signs of realization.

Self-remembering and witnessing practices must not install a second observer, inner surveillance, or a dissociative split.

## Shared-world safeguards

- Dream interpretation never overrides consent.
- Metaphysical unity never erases interpersonal boundaries.
- No teaching may imply suffering was deserved, chosen, or unconsciously selected.
- Illness, poverty, trauma, disability, and oppression are never read as incoherence or low vibration.
- Wealth is never presented as proof of alignment.
- Relational correction is necessary when metaphysical certainty intensifies.
- Necessary protection, refusal, departure, or boundary-setting need not wait for contemplative clarity.

## Calling and vocation

Grandiose vocation is a warning sign, not a milestone. Watch for a calling that inflates the self, requires others to organize around it, arrives with urgency and total certainty, or is described as election, destiny, or a highest timeline, especially alongside reduced sleep or escalating interpretation. Calling never justifies abandoning obligations or overriding consent.

## Energetic-practice safeguards

- Spanda, Shakti, qi, prana, and channels are not biomedical claims.
- Entry material excludes forceful breath retention, extreme sleep disruption, sexual-energy techniques, forced circulation, initiatory practices, and energetic escalation.
- Stronger energetic practices require separate safety review and qualified containment.

## The shadow of coherence

Coherence can harden into rigidity, or one person's order can be imposed on others. That is force, not coherence. Any increase in agency that diminishes love, freedom, humility, or answerability is not greater coherence. Coherence coordinates difference; enforced smoothness and emotional suppression are failures of it.

## Authority

- No teacher, including the founder, is presented as spiritual master. Fellow pupils, not masters.
- No obedience, shock methods, or humiliation.
- No personality cult structures.
- No lineage realization is conferred.

## Warning signs

Pause practice and seek support if there is:

- persistent dissociation or derealization,
- severe mood destabilization,
- distressing dream escalation,
- escalating sleeplessness,
- inability to function,
- obsessive or compulsive interpretation,
- paranoia,
- manic inflation or grandiose certainty,
- crisis-level despair.

## Product safety

Entry products must be grounding.

Advanced products require stronger framing, screening, or live containment.

Before any curriculum ships, run a content-to-safety review. Identity loosening, dream-state practice, sleep disruption, central-channel language, intense affect, and altered-state material require explicit contraindications, grounding, warning signs, stop conditions, screening proportional to intensity, and facilitator containment.

## Red Team prompt

Before launching any practice or product, ask:

1. Where could this be unsafe?
2. Where could this be overclaimed?
3. Who is this not for?
4. What warning signs need naming?
5. What support path is needed?
6. Does the promise remain honest?
7. Could this reduce other people to symbols or imply private authorship of shared reality?
8. Could this read suffering as deserved or as incoherence?
9. Is any lineage claim unattributed or any register transition concealed?
