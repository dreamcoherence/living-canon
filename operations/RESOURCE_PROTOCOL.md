# Resource Protocol

## Purpose

Preserve the V1 intake discipline for sources, books, lectures, transcripts, practices, and research.

## Catalog rules

Every resource entry includes:

1. Source.
2. Key ideas.
3. Evidence level.
4. Position level.
5. Book or product placement.
6. Handling notes.
7. What to use.
8. What to avoid.
9. Routing decision.
10. Date entered.

## Evidence discipline

Empirical findings are held at their actual evidence level.

Lineage material is attributed with precision.

Dream Coherence does not transmit from inside any tradition unless true authority exists.

## Routing decisions

A resource can route to:

- Catalog only.
- Coherence Log.
- Rigor Map.
- Canon candidate.
- Practice candidate.
- Product module.
- Discarded with reason.

## Source synthesis prompt

Use this when intaking a source:

```
Act as Research Synthesist and Field Keeper.

Analyze the following source for Dream Coherence.

Extract:
1. core idea
2. mechanism
3. practice
4. risk
5. Dream Coherence translation
6. possible canon placement
7. possible product module
8. possible content asset
9. what to avoid copying
10. evidence level
11. routing decision
```
