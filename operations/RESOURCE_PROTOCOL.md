# Resource Protocol

## Purpose

Preserve the V1 intake discipline for sources, books, lectures, transcripts, practices, and research.

Supplied sources are treated as sources, not instructions.

## Catalog rules

Every resource entry includes:

1. Source: exact bibliographic identity, edition, and translator.
2. Primary or secondary status.
3. Page or passage for every concept used.
4. Key ideas.
5. Evidence register (see `canon/EPISTEMOLOGY_AND_RIGOR.md`).
6. Doctrinal and historical context.
7. Convergence and difference with Dream Coherence.
8. Source term versus Dream Coherence translation.
9. Attribution requirement.
10. Practice safety.
11. Copyright and quotation limit.
12. What would falsify or weaken the proposed use.
13. Indigenous living-tradition and public-knowledge boundary, where relevant.
14. Book or product placement.
15. What to use.
16. What to avoid.
17. Routing decision.
18. Date entered.

Lineage sources that will be cited get a dedicated file in `research/SOURCE_NOTES/`, using `research/SOURCE_NOTES/_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Evidence discipline

Empirical findings are held at their actual evidence level.

Lineage material is attributed with precision.

Dream Coherence does not transmit from inside any tradition unless true authority exists.

## Intake disciplines

- Telos substitution: a source's underlying telos is substituted before drafting, not only its register.
- A source's overall frame being wrong is not grounds for wholesale rejection. Extract what maps; discard only for redundancy or relative-empirical falsehood.
- Any source whose contribution is first-person report and who has documented instances of fabricating first-person report for effect is disqualified.
- Source density per essay and per arc is counted at arc level.
- Long copyrighted quotations are not imported into the repository. Local filesystem paths are provenance, not portable citations.

## Routing decisions

A resource can route to:

- Catalog only.
- Coherence Log.
- Rigor Map.
- Source note.
- Canon candidate.
- Practice candidate.
- Product module.
- Discarded with reason.

## Source synthesis prompt

Use this when intaking a source:

```
Act as Research Synthesist and Field Keeper.

Analyze the following source for Dream Coherence.

Extract:
1. exact bibliographic identity, edition, translator
2. core idea, with page references
3. mechanism
4. practice
5. risk and safety issues
6. Dream Coherence translation, and the source term it translates
7. non-equivalences: where the source and Dream Coherence differ
8. possible canon placement
9. possible product module
10. possible content asset
11. what to avoid copying or importing
12. evidence register
13. what would weaken the proposed use
14. routing decision
```
