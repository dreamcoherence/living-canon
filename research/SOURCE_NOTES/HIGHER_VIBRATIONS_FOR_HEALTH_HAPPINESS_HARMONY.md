# Source Note: Higher Vibrations for Health, Happiness and Harmony

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. Supplied EPUB. TO VERIFY: author, publisher, edition before any citation. Treated as a register-risk source.

## Retained (ordinary, testable insights only)
- State influences attention, interpretation, behavior, persistence, and relational availability.
- Repeated patterns become easier to enact.
- Feelings carry information.
- Gratitude, generosity, presence, compassion, and purposeful action can alter lived state.
- Desire can be examined for the qualities of being it seeks.

## Rejected
Invisible frequencies mechanically attracting events; health, wealth, relationship, or safety guarantees; difficult emotion as spiritual inferiority; emotional suppression disguised as positivity; victim-blaming; moral ranking of people by vibration; metaphysical speculation presented as science.

## The four senses of "vibration"
| Sense | Dream Coherence treatment |
|---|---|
| Measurable physical oscillation | Scientific claim requiring evidence and measurement |
| Felt tone or pattern of a state | Phenomenologically available when defined precisely |
| Shaiva spanda | Attributed metaphysical and contemplative doctrine of dynamic awareness |
| New Thought law of vibration | Not adopted as causal law |

Spanda is not the law of attraction. Vibration names the lived pulse and organization of a state. It does not rank human worth or guarantee external outcomes.

## Routing
Redundancy discard for its causal frame; retained insights are already held more rigorously elsewhere. Kept as a negative-calibration source.
