# Source Note: Advaita and Jivanmukti

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. Primary sources to identify.

## Translatable contributions
Nonseparation is not acquired in time. The deepest identity is not manufactured by personal history.

## Required doctrinal correction
In classical Advaita, the identity of Atman and Brahman is recognized as already the case. Atman does not become or attain Brahman as something separate.

## Non-equivalences
Advaita and nondual Shaivism are not collapsed; their world, power, manifestation, and liberation grammars differ. "Advaita says the world is unreal" is too crude. Treating all nondual traditions as doctrinally identical is equally inaccurate.

## Jivanmukti
Varies across traditions. "Liberated While Living" (Passage VIII) resonates with it as a horizon. It is never promised as a product outcome or certified.
