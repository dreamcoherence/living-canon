## Historical and doctrinal context
Early Spanda materials (Spandakarika and commentaries) are distinct from later Pratyabhijna-inflected syntheses. Dyczkowski's reading of that relationship must be recorded, not assumed.

## Translatable contributions
- The Ground is not opposed to movement. Current is the Ground differentiating as this living moment.
- Contraction makes perspective, intimacy, memory, commitment, and finite action possible. It becomes binding when a contracted standpoint mistakes itself for the whole.
- Recognition in stillness matures as recognition through manifestation.

## Non-equivalences
Spanda is not measurable physical vibration, emotional positivity, high-frequency status, quantum mechanics, or a law of attraction. Spanda is not the New Thought law of vibration.
