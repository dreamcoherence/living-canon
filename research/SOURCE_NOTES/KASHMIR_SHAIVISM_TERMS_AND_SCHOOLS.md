## Historical and doctrinal context
"Kashmir Shaivism" is an umbrella, not a single system. Spanda, Pratyabhijna, Trika, Krama, and Kaula are not interchangeable. Trika is not a synonym for all nondual Shaivism.

## Translatable contributions
- Prakasha (luminosity, appearing) and vimarsha (self-knowing, expressive reflexivity): awareness is not a passive container. Dream Coherence translation: awareness is not only where experience appears. It is the living knowing of appearance.
- Pratyabhijna: recognition is recovery rather than acquisition.
- Manifestation as real expression: the world is real enough to love, consequential enough to answer for, and dreamlike enough to disclose more than its surfaces first reveal.
- Iccha, jnana, kriya: backstage structure for orientation, attunement, participation.
- Grace and means: we prepare the conditions, listen for what exceeds our plans, act with full participation, and release the claim to control the whole.

## Non-equivalences
Shiva is not the empirical ego. Shakti is not a generic energy substance. The empirical pronoun "I" does not prove the universal aham. A moment of self-presence does not establish Shaiva metaphysics.

## What must not be imported
Initiatory ritual, mantra, advanced subtle-energy practice, sexual or transgressive rites, guru doctrine, siddhi claims.
