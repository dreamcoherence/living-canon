# Source Note: Gurdjieff and Self-Remembering

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. TO VERIFY: which formulations come through Ouspensky (In Search of the Miraculous) versus Gurdjieff's own writings.

## Role
Attentional methodology, not Dream Coherence's ontology.

## Translatable contributions
- Becoming present to oneself while perception and action continue.
- Observation of automaticity.
- Integration of thought, feeling, body, and action.
- Practice within ordinary life.
- Knowledge becoming embodied capacity.

Preferred public translations: "Include yourself in the moment you are experiencing." "Become present to the life through which this moment is being met."

## Non-equivalences and cautions
- Self-remembering must not create a second observer, inner surveillance, or a dissociative split. Inclusive attention includes what is happening, the body-feeling-thought response, one's participation, and the relational field.
- "Divided attention" and the "double arrow" require careful attribution, especially through Ouspensky. Not used as public terminology.
- Mechanicality is translated as automaticity, unattended patterning, or fragmentation. People are never called asleep, mechanical, or inferior.
- The three centers are a practical heuristic, not anatomy.

## What must not be imported
Personality cult and authoritarian school structures; humiliating or manipulative shock methods; speculative cosmology; higher-body claims as fact; contempt for "sleeping humanity"; spiritual superiority; the idea that ordinary personality has no value.
