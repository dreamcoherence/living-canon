# Source Note: Corbin, Alone with the Alone

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. Supplied PDF. TO VERIFY: edition (Princeton/Bollingen), translator (Ralph Manheim), pages.

## Translatable contributions
- The imaginal as a disciplined middle position between reductive fantasy and literalized revelation. An image can be more than decoration without becoming proof of an external being or event.
- Imagination can disclose, configure, rehearse, and bring implicit meaning into encounterable form, tested through embodiment, ethics, relationship, consequence, and time.
- Relational nonduality: unity need not abolish the I-Thou structure. The deepest unity need not abolish relation.
- Creative prayer: prayer is not only asking. It is becoming capable of relation. Supports Communion.
- Divine Names resonate with the Golden Thread as metaphysical-poetic analogy only.

## Non-equivalences and filters
- The mundus imaginalis is not presented as empirical fact and not casually equated with the astral register.
- Dream figures are not treated as external entities by default.
- The personal Lord is not converted into a generic higher self.
- No insider speech for Ibn Arabi, Sufism, or Islamic esotericism.
- Intensity does not establish election. An inner image grants no authority over others.
