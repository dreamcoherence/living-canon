# Source Note: Meditations on the Tarot

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. Supplied PDF. TO VERIFY: edition and translator (anonymous author, commonly attributed to Valentin Tomberg; English translation by Robert Powell). Attribution of authorship stated as commonly attributed, not asserted.

## Translatable contributions
- Concentration without force. Attention becomes effortless when the being no longer has to force itself in one direction against the momentum of another.
- Symbol as operation rather than decoration. A true symbol changes the way experience can be met.
- Experience, understanding, ethical enactment, and articulation held together. Experience without understanding becomes intoxication; understanding without embodiment becomes abstraction; action without either becomes technique severed from wisdom.
- Analogy as method rather than proof.
- Effort and grace. Practice prepares the conditions. It does not command the revelation.
- Love above power. Any increase in agency that diminishes love, freedom, humility, or answerability is not greater coherence.
- Fellow pupils rather than spiritual masters.

## What must not be imported
Specifically Catholic and Christian Hermetic theology as doctrine; ceremonial occultism; hierarchy; supernatural causal claims; gendered metaphysics; obedience that could support submission to a teacher.
