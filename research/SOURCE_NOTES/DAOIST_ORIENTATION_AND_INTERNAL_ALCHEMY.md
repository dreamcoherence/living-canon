# Source Note: Daoist Orientation and Internal Alchemy

**Status:** SOURCE-DEPENDENT. Primary and secondary sources to identify.

## Translatable contributions
- Embodiment within realization. The body is not what realization leaves behind. It is where coherence becomes conduct.
- Refinement as return and integration.
- Responsive, unforced action. Unforced action is not action without discipline. It is action no longer divided against the conditions it must meet.
- Timing as part of wisdom; receptivity and action as complementary; yielding as intelligence rather than passivity.

## Non-equivalences
The microcosmic orbit is not the Indic or Tibetan central channel. Qi is not prana. Daoist channels are not nadis or tsa. Jing-qi-shen is not a universal ladder. Structural resonance does not prove anatomical identity.

## What must not be imported
Wu wei as justification for passivity or acting only when inspired. Neidan taught without sufficient study and authority.
