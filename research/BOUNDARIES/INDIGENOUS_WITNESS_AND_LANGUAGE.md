# Boundary: Indigenous Witness and Language

**Status:** Required audit boundary.

## Rule
"Great Spirit" does not become Dream Coherence vocabulary. It suggests a single pan-Indigenous doctrine, detaches sacred language from land and community, and can borrow authority without relationship or permission.

## The usable intuition, in Dream Coherence language
Becoming available to a living whole; service beyond ego; land, ancestry, community, and more-than-human relation; reciprocity rather than extraction; humility before intelligence no individual owns.

Use: the living whole, sacred more-than-self, life moving through this life, becoming available to service.

## Requirements for any lineage-specific use
A named people; a precise public source; linguistic and historical context; clarity about authority; ideally actual relationship rather than textual extraction.

## Retroactive audit (required)
This boundary applies to material already in the canon, not only to new language. The following are routed through the same provenance, public-knowledge, living-tradition, and relationship audit:

- Nahua philosophy (teotl, nepantla, flower and song) in `LINEAGE_MAP_FORTIFIED.md` Circle One and the Lexicon's Nahua Lineage Terms.
