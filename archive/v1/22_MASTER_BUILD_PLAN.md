## The Compressed Working Model

For each chapter, in order, the working sequence runs:

1. **Spine.** Thesis, beats, voice texture, lineage and science load summary, practices, transitions, dharma thread, candidate central distillation sentence. Locked at the spine level.
2. **Lineage and science load specified.** Which lineages named in which beats with which source-material weight. Which scientific frameworks referenced. Which luminaries surface in the prose, briefly. Which figures held entirely for later chapters.
3. **Central distillation work.** The single sentence — or small cluster of sentences — that lands the chapter's face of the recognition as transmission. Drafted, tested, refined. The sentence belongs in the chapter's spine and operates as the chapter's pivot during drafting. *The one dreaming is the one reading* is Chapter 1's central distillation. Each chapter has its own.
4. **Draft.** Full prose. Multiple passes: structural, voice, friction-and-precision, read-aloud-in-founder's-voice, recognition-and-transmission test. The fifth pass either locks the chapter or sends it back to the second pass.
5. **Lock.** The chapter enters the project as a locked artifact.
6. **Integration check.** Did anything in drafting surface insights that affect earlier chapters? File Coherence Log entries. Integrate now if essential, hold for later integration pass if not.
7. **Next chapter begins.**

Chapters are worked sequentially. No parallel drafting. Each chapter inherits the deepening of the chapters before it.

**At the close of each movement, before the next movement begins:** a blank-canvas review. The work is set aside and the whole movement is reviewed as if encountered for the first time. The standard is tested: does this movement, read fresh, move a reader from jiva to jivanmukti at the depth this movement is responsible for? Gaps surfaced enter the Coherence Log or are integrated immediately if essential.

## The Two-Month Sprint (Active Cadence)

This is the founder-directed compressed cadence, active as of 2026-06-17. It overrides the open-ended "speed of quality" pace for the duration of the sprint while preserving every quality gate. The target is **first-locked-draft state** — all thirteen chapters plus Preface drafted and locked to the tuning fork. External editorial and the publication pathway are month three onward.

The map front-loads all architecture so that once drafting begins it never stalls waiting for a spine. Eight weeks of work with a ninth as buffer.

- **Week 1.** Finish Chapter 2 (The Convergence) to lock on the critical path. In parallel, batch-spine Movement Two (Chapters 4 through 10).
- **Week 2.** Draft Chapter 3 (Conscious Participation) and the Preface to lock. Movement One coherence check. Batch-spine Movement Three (Chapters 11 through 13). All thirteen spines locked.
- **Week 3.** Chapter 4 (Arrival into Presence) to lock. Begin Chapter 5 (Body Coherence).
- **Week 4.** Chapter 5 to lock. Chapter 6 (Attention Coherence) to lock.
- **Week 5.** Chapter 7 (Identity Coherence) to lock. Chapter 8 (Dream Coherence) to lock.
- **Week 6.** Chapter 9 (The Bardo of Dream) to lock. Chapter 10 (Reality Coherence) to lock. Movement Two coherence check.
- **Week 7.** Chapters 11, 12, 13 drafted (The Synthesis, The Frontier, The Threshold).
- **Week 8.** Movement Three locked. Whole-book read-through. Coherence Log integration pass.
- **Week 9 (buffer).** Second read-through. First-locked-draft declared.

Two binding facts. The pace holds only at roughly five to six sessions a week with fast founder review turnaround; the rate-limiter is the review-and-lock gate, not drafting speed. The strain zone is weeks three through six, where the heaviest chapters cluster (Arrival, Body, Identity, Dream Coherence, the Bardo of Dream); the Fork B addition of Chapter 8 tightened this cluster and the week-nine buffer. The standing rule under compression: slip a chapter before dropping the bar. The bar is what makes the manuscript worth finishing.
