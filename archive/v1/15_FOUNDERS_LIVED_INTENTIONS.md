## Important Note on Practice

The daily, weekly, and monthly protocols at the end of this file are flagged as a component the founder has not yet fully registered into lived experience. They are aspirational at present. The honest truth is that any practice held in mind is more powerful than no practice, and a small daily practice held consistently is more powerful than an elaborate one abandoned after three days.

The protocol below is therefore offered in two forms: a minimum viable practice that takes less than five minutes a day and is genuinely livable, and a fuller practice that scales as the capacity to hold it scales. Begin with the minimum. Let it deepen on its own as the recognition deepens.

What is asked is consistency, not intensity.

## Minimum Viable Practice (the version that gets done)

This is the version designed to be genuinely sustainable. Less than five minutes a day. Designed to be impossible to skip.

**Morning, sixty seconds.** Open this file. Read the section titled *Inner State*. Close the file. Begin the day.

That is the entire morning practice. Sixty seconds. No journaling, no logging. Just a single re-inhabitation of the state.

**Evening, sixty seconds.** Before sleep, ask one question silently: *what entered the field today?* Notice whatever comes. No need to write it down.

That is the entire evening practice. Sixty seconds.

**Weekly, ten minutes.** One day a week (Sunday is recommended), open this file and ask: *what is changing? what is consolidating? what is asking to be acted on?* Write a few sentences in the log section below. No more.

**Monthly, twenty minutes.** Once a month, review the weekly notes. Ask: *what changed across the wagers?* Write a brief monthly note.

If the minimum is sustained for ninety days, the fuller practice (below) becomes available naturally. The capacity has been built.

## Fuller Practice (when capacity has been built)

This is the version to grow into, not to begin with.

**Morning, ten minutes.** Read the wager sections (Livelihood, Body, Relationships, Circumstances). Sit briefly with each. Re-inhabit the state. Notice the body. Notice what wants to be done today from this state.

**Evening, ten minutes.** Log briefly: what entered the field, what state was inhabited, what body shifts occurred, what synchronicities appeared.

**Weekly review, thirty minutes.** Read the week's evening logs. Identify patterns. Note opportunities. Update what needs updating.

**Monthly review, sixty minutes.** Comprehensive review across all wagers. What changed, what is consolidating, what needs deeper work, what is accelerating, what needs adjustment.
