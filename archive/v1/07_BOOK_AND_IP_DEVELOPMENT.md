## The Book's Role in the Field

The book is the foundational artifact of Dream Coherence. The field exists around it. Every offering downstream legitimizes itself through the book. The book also exists in its own right as a serious contemplative work that can be read, studied, and re-read across decades.

The book is not a marketing artifact. It is a body of work that stands on its own. Treated as a marketing artifact, it would have been written differently.

## Publishing Decision

Whether to publish traditionally or self-publish is decided in late polish. Each has tradeoffs:

**Traditional publishing.** More legitimacy and broader discoverability. Royalty rate of 10-15% on hardcover, ~25% on ebook. Distribution and editorial support. Slower (12-18 months from manuscript to bookstores). Less brand control.

**Self-publishing.** Higher royalty (40-70% depending on platform). Total brand control. Faster (3-6 months from manuscript to release). Less third-party legitimacy. Distribution requires more direct effort.

For Dream Coherence, the right answer is likely traditional publishing if a serious literary publisher (Riverhead, Knopf, FSG, Penguin Press, or a serious independent like Counterpoint or Coffee House or Graywolf) wants the book. If not, self-publishing through a quality channel (Bookbaby, IngramSpark, or direct print-on-demand with strong design) preserves the work without compromising it.

The decision is made when the manuscript is ready, based on what relationship serves the work best.

## Book to Field Conversion

Once the book is locked, each chapter becomes the source for multiple field artifacts. This is the natural extension of the book into the field's content engine.

For each chapter, the conversion produces:

- One or more newsletter issues drawing from the chapter's recognition
- A short-form content cluster (5-10 X posts and Instagram pieces) carrying chapter quotes and aphorisms
- One or more audio essays giving voice to the chapter's recognition (calibrated to Sovereign or Brand Voice depending on the piece)
- One or more practice worksheets drawn from the chapter's practice work (used in the first offering, the flagship cohort, and the membership)
- One or more course modules in the flagship that teach the chapter's practice in depth

The book is the source. The field artifacts are extensions that bring the book's recognition into multiple forms.

This conversion does not happen during the book's drafting. It happens after the book is locked, run through the AI infrastructure with the founder's review.

## IP Protection

The book is registered for copyright at publication. The brand name *Dream Coherence* and the spiral mark are trademark candidates; consultation with IP counsel happens in Phase 1 to determine whether to register and in which jurisdictions.

The methodology itself cannot be copyrighted, but the specific articulation of it (the protocol, the specific practices, the central distillations) is the book's IP. The protocol's name (Body Coherence, Attention Coherence, Identity Coherence, Dream Coherence, Reality Coherence as the five-stage protocol) may be trademarkable as a phrase associated with the brand.

The IP discipline is to protect the brand's identity, not to defend against people doing similar work. Other practitioners will develop adjacent methodologies; that is fine. What is defended is the specific brand, the specific phrasing, the specific work.

## Translations and Foreign Rights

If a traditional publisher is involved, foreign rights typically remain with the publisher who may sub-license. If self-published, foreign rights remain with the founder and translations can be commissioned later.

Priority languages for eventual translation (when scale supports it):
- Spanish (large potential audience)
- Portuguese (Brazilian market)
- German
- French
- Possibly Japanese, Chinese (mainland or traditional), and Korean depending on the work's reception in Asia

Translations are not a Phase 1-2 concern. Held for Phase 3+.

The audiobook of the book is a separate artifact and an important one. Decisions:

The audiobook is likely the highest-impact extension of the book. Audio is also a primary surface for the brand. The audiobook is built with serious production discipline.
