# Doorway Content Map. Months One Through Three.

**Version 1.0. May 2026. Working Voice register. The first-quarter operational content slate for the field launch. Sits alongside `26_AUDIENCE_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md`, which holds the strategic engine; this file holds the specific twelve-piece slate the engine produces in months one through three.**

This file specifies the twelve pieces of content that constitute the first quarter of the field launch. Each piece is identified by surface, register, length, working title, central move, and calibration notes. Each piece will be drafted in a separate session in Brand Voice. This file is the planning artifact. The drafts are the work.

---

## Operating Principle

Each piece is a doorway. Someone arrives without context, encounters the piece, and the recognition lands or does not. The piece does not promise depth ahead. The piece is depth, available immediately, transmitting the work itself.

The twelve pieces compose a three-month arc that takes a newcomer from first contact with the work through the door of the first threshold offering. The arc is not a funnel. The pieces are doorways. Some people enter, walk through the first essay, and never return. The work has shipped. Others stay through all twelve and arrive at the threshold ready. Others read one piece and remember it for six months before the moment is right.

---

## Month One. Establishing the Recognition.

The opening month introduces what the work is and what it transmits. No selling. No funnel. The recognition, given.

### Piece One. Long-form essay. Website and newsletter.

**Working title:** The dreaming has already begun.

**The move:** Open with the recognition that experience is occurring as a dreaming, here, now, in the awareness reading the sentence. Trace the convergence (Shaiva, Dzogchen, Hermetic, the contemporary edge) without performing scholarship. End in the practical: the practice begins where you are.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice at near-Sovereign register. 1,800 to 2,200 words. The website's first long-form piece. Sets the tone for everything that follows.

**Why first:** It is the doorway that locates the work. Anyone who reads it knows whether the work is for them.

### Piece Two. X thread, five posts.

**Working title:** What you are calling life is a continuous dreaming.

**The move:** Compressed version of the essay's opening recognition. Each post stands on its own. Builds to the practical: notice now.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice with the highest density per word. The thread is the recognition in five tap-stops.

### Piece Three. Instagram carousel, seven slides.

**Working title:** The four registers of one practice.

**The move:** Visual articulation of the four registers (infinite ground, instrument, ensouling, operating system). Each slide one register. Final slide names the four as one. Atmospheric image on each per brand system.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice in overlays. EB Garamond Italic per `23_BRAND_SYSTEM.md`. Image atmospheric, not literal.

## Month Two. The Mechanism.

The second month moves from recognition into mechanism. How the dreaming works. What participation looks like.

### Piece Four. Long-form essay.

**Working title:** The day is a file being saved.

**The move:** The Tenzin Wangyal file-tagging mechanism rendered in Dream Coherence's voice. Most experience is being tagged unconsciously, which is why night returns the day's debris untransformed. Conscious tagging is the practice. Open with the recognition. Close with the four exercises transposed into the work's language.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice. 1,500 to 1,800 words. Includes a practice the reader can begin tonight.

**Note:** This piece draws directly from `RESOURCE_TENZIN_WANGYAL_DREAM_YOGA_SYNTHESIS.md`. One of the strongest first-touchpoint pieces because it is operationally clear and immediately deployable.

### Piece Five. Audio piece, ten to fifteen minutes.

**Working title:** Pre-sleep transmission. Five minutes before the night.

**The move:** Guided practice based on Tenzin Wangyal's pre-sleep meditation, adapted to Brand Voice. Body, throat, heart. Stillness, silence, spaciousness. Fall asleep from that ground. Practice piece that lives on Spotify and the website's audio library.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice. Audio register more spacious than text. Pacing slow. Not hypnosis (per brand discipline against the wellness-hypnosis content pattern). A contemplative practice piece anchored in dream yoga tradition.

### Piece Six. X thread, seven posts.

**Working title:** How a day becomes the dream that night.

**The move:** The file-saving mechanism in seven posts. Sharpens the essay's recognition into transmissible aphorisms.

## Month Three. The Lived Practice.

The third month brings the work into the days. What practice looks like over time. What the four registers mean lived.

### Piece Seven. Long-form essay.

**Working title:** The instrument first. Why the body is the foundation of every recognition that lasts.

**The move:** The instrument register articulated. Why nervous-system regulation, breath, posture, energetic vitality are not separate from the contemplative work. They are the ground that lets the recognition hold. Touches the etheric layer without naming it as such. The body as the alchemical vessel.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice. 1,500 to 1,800 words.

### Piece Eight. Instagram single posts, three released across the month.

**Working title:** Three aphorisms on the ensouling work.

**The move:** Single line per image. The slow disclosure of what it means to bring recognition into the specific life that is being lived. Image atmospheric.

### Piece Nine. Audio piece, fifteen to twenty minutes.

**Working title:** Spanda. The pulse beneath the days.

**The move:** A transmission piece, not a guided practice. Spoken essay on the Kashmir Shaiva recognition of spanda, the subtle vibration by which awareness becomes form. Calibrated to listener attention. The first piece that names a lineage term and lets it open into experience.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice with depth toward Sovereign on the most condensed passages.

The closing pieces of month three position the first threshold offering. No hard pivot. The work has been transmitted for three months. People who want to go deeper now have somewhere to go.

**Calibration:** Brand Voice.

## What This Map Does

Twelve pieces. Three months. One arc that takes someone from never having heard of Dream Coherence to standing at the door of the first threshold, having received three months of transmission that was itself the work.

The cadence is sustainable. Three to four pieces per month is the floor, not the ceiling. As the field matures, cadence increases per `26_AUDIENCE_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md` (5 to 10 X posts per week, 3 to 5 Instagram pieces per week, one newsletter per week, one audio piece per month growing to one per week by Phase Three).

This file holds the spine pieces. The higher-cadence surface posts (daily X, weekly Instagram, weekly newsletter) ride on top of the spine pieces, drawing from them, sharpening individual recognitions into shorter forms.

## Production Sequence

Recommended order in which to draft the twelve pieces:

1. Pieces One and Two together. The long essay and the X thread are sibling pieces. Drafting them in the same session keeps the recognition coherent across both forms.

2. Piece Three. The carousel, calibrated against the locked v2 of the four-register articulation.

3. Pieces Four and Six together. Same sibling pattern. The Tenzin Wangyal mechanism piece and its X-thread compression.

4. Piece Five. The audio piece. Requires both drafted script and recording session.

5. Pieces Seven through Nine. The third-month content.

6. Pieces Ten through Twelve. The threshold bridge content, drafted closer to the actual opening of the first threshold.

Each drafting session focuses on one piece (or one sibling pair). Each piece is held to the voice axioms and the locked Brand Voice register. Each piece is reviewed before shipping.
