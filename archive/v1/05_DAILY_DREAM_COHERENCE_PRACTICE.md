# Daily Dream Coherence Practice (Founder)

**Version 1.3. June 2026. Working Voice register, with the Sleep of Clear Light practice text held in its own practice-transmission register where marked. The founder's daily practice. Private working document. Not for publication.**

This file directs the founder's daily practice. The project must be lived before it is taught. Dream Coherence is not only a brand. It is a way of relating to life. The lived practice is the deepest validation any offering will ever rest on.

This file is structured in two parts. **Part One** is the minimum viable practice that is genuinely sustainable on any day. **Part Two** is the fuller practice that scales as capacity scales. The fuller practice is to grow into, not to begin with.

The fuller protocol below is also the working source from which the eventual practitioner-facing first offering and flagship cohort are designed. Founder lives it first, refines it through living, then teaches it.

---

## Part One. Minimum Viable Practice.

Less than five minutes a day. Designed to be impossible to skip. This is the version that gets done on hard days, busy days, traveling days, sick days.

### Morning, sixty seconds.

Open `15_FOUNDERS_LIVED_INTENTIONS.md`. Read the section titled *Inner State*. Close the file. Begin the day.

### Evening, sixty seconds.

Before sleep, ask one question silently: *what entered the field today?* Notice whatever comes. No need to write it down.

Then, lying down, the seed of the Sleep of Clear Light. This is the minimum-viable form of the full practice held in Part Two:

> Set the day at the threshold. Three breaths out, and it is gone. Soften the body into the awareness that meets it. Silence at the throat. Open sky at the heart. Sleep as the openness, awake as the awareness that knows it.

### Weekly, ten minutes.

One day per week (Sunday recommended), open `15_FOUNDERS_LIVED_INTENTIONS.md` and ask: *what is changing? What is consolidating? What is asking to be acted on?* Write a few sentences in the log section.

### Monthly, twenty minutes.

Once a month, review the weekly notes. Ask: *what changed across the wagers?* Write a brief monthly note.

If the minimum is sustained for ninety days, the fuller practice below becomes available naturally. The capacity has been built.

## Part Two. Fuller Practice.

This is the version to grow into. It is also the source from which the offerings will eventually be designed.

### Morning Protocol

#### 1. Dream Capture

Before phone, speaking, coffee, or action:

Write what was dreamed. The images remaining. The emotions remaining. The symbols repeating. What the dream showed. What this connects to in waking life. What instruction might be hidden here.

If nothing is remembered, write: *No dream recalled. I remain available.*

This trains continuity.

#### 2. Body Arrival (5-10 min)

Feel feet, pelvis, belly, heart, throat, face. Let the body become safe enough for the day.

The body is the gate. Enter here.

#### 3. Witness Practice (10-20 min)

Sit. Notice thought, sensation, image, memory, mood. Do not control. Do not solve.

Ask: *what is aware of this?*

Then rest.

#### 4. Identity Alignment

Write one sentence: *Today I live as the one who...*

Examples:
- Today I live as the one whose work is inevitable
- Today I live as the one whose body can hold prosperity
- Today I live as the one whose attention serves the highest dream
- Today I live as the one whose presence is the work
- Today I live as the one already living from the recognition

Then ask: *what would be natural from that state?*

#### 5. Dharma Question

Ask: *what is the dream beneath the dream today?*

Do not force an answer. Let one direction emerge.

### Midday Protocol

#### 1. Reality Check

Ask: *am I dreaming?*

Then look: what is the state of the body? What story is running? What is being assumed? What is life mirroring? What would lucidity change here?

#### 2. Attention Discernment

Ask: *what am I illumining in the dark?*

Then choose one: continue, redirect, release, act, wait.

#### 3. Action from State

Take one action that belongs to the desired life. Small but real.

Examples: outline a product, write a page, clean the workspace, send the email, move the body, refine one asset, rest instead of forcing.

### Evening Protocol

Done sitting and writing, while there is still attention for the page.

#### 1. Day Review

Write briefly: where was I lucid? Where was I asleep? What pattern repeated? What did life mirror? What did I avoid? What did I honor? What belongs in tomorrow's practice?

#### 2. Revision

Choose one moment from the day that felt misaligned. Revise it in imagination. Not to deny it. To repattern the nervous system and the subconscious.

Ask: *how would this have unfolded from the highest version of me?*

Feel that scene. Let the body learn it.

#### 3. Dream Incubation

Before sleep, write: *Tonight, show me what I need to see about...*

Choose one: money, body, love, work, destiny, fear, creativity, Dream Coherence as work, the next offering, the next chapter, what is asking to be expressed.

Then say internally: *I will remember what matters.*

### The Sleep of Clear Light

The protocols above are done sitting and writing. This one is done lying down, as sleep approaches, and again on waking. It is the contemplative spine of the practice, the place where the recognition meets the night directly and the day is carried across the threshold of sleep and returned in the morning.

The Day Review and Revision can fold into the Evening Review below, or precede it. On hard nights, the seed in Part One is enough. The Morning Review flows directly into Dream Capture.

The text that follows is the practice itself, read, recalled, or moved through from memory. It is held in the practice-transmission register, not in Working Voice.

#### Evening Review

> Before sleep, return to the day.
> Let its events arise on their own.
> Notice what still carries charge. A conversation, a message, a meeting, an emotion.
> Choose three.
>
> For each, descend to the ground beneath the story.
> Stillness in the body. Silence in speech. Spaciousness in the heart.
> From there, look again.
> Hold the experience in awareness rather than in the story it tells.
>
> If it releases, release it.
> If it does not, give it a new relationship. Rename it. Set it in a larger frame.
> Then set it down.
> The day's residue stays at the threshold.
> Enter the night clean.

#### Breath

> Five rounds.
> Inhale through the nose. At the top, one final sip of air. Hold in fullness. Exhale slowly through the nose.
> Each exhale carries the day out.

#### Body

> Scan from the soles to the crown.
> Where there is tension, contraction, holding, bring awareness there.
> No resistance. No analysis.
> What is held dissolves in the awareness that meets it.

#### Throat

> Rest attention at the throat.
> Find the silence. Not the absence of sound, the silence itself, present and alive.
> Every voice of worry, memory, and unfinished speech returns to it.

#### Heart

> Rest attention at the heart.
> A clear sky opens within. Vast. Boundless. Untouched.
> Wound, burden, story, identity, all of it dissolves into that space.
> Nothing held. Nothing lacking. Awareness and openness, and nothing between them.

#### Sleep

> From this ground, let sleep come.
> Rest as the openness. Rest as the awareness that knows it.
> This is the sleep of clear light.
> Clear, because nothing obscures the space. Light, because awareness stays present within it.

#### Morning Review

> On waking, remember the night.
> Notice what arose in dream, and what it taught.
> If lucidity came, honor it. If it did not, renew the intention.
> No judgment. No disappointment. Only practice.
> Awareness is learning to recognize itself.
>
> The morning feeds the day. The day prepares the night. The night deepens the recognition.
> And it continues.

## Weekly Deep Practice (60-90 min)

Once per week:

### Dream Review

Read the week's dreams. Extract symbols, characters, settings, conflicts, gifts, repeated emotions, unresolved themes.

### Waking Dream Review

Read the week's events. Extract synchronicities, frustrations, opportunities, patterns, resistance, intuitive signals.

### Coherence Map

Where are waking and sleeping dreams saying the same thing? Where do they contradict? What is the field asking to integrate?

## The Discipline

Do not turn daily practice into performance.

The point is not to appear mystical. The point is to become lucid, coherent, and capable.

The fuller practice grows on its own as the recognition deepens. The minimum protects continuity. Either is sufficient. Inconsistency between them is the failure mode to avoid.

## Practice Within Partnership and Fatherhood

The work is not done in monastic isolation. It is done within partnership with Lalita and fatherhood with Satya. The lived practice is the work as it actually happens in this specific life, not the practice as it would happen if the founder were alone.

This means: the practice integrates with relationship rather than competing with it. The morning protocol may be shorter on some days because Satya wakes early and needs attention. The evening protocol may happen in a different shape because partnership requires presence. The weekly deep practice may be done with Lalita's awareness and support rather than as a solo retreat from family life.

**The principle.** The practice serves the life that includes partnership and fatherhood. The life is not interrupted by the practice; the practice is woven into the life.

**The relational practice.** Partnership and fatherhood are themselves practice surfaces. The work of being a partner who can be present with a partner whose own life is real, the work of being a father who can hold a small consciousness without dominating it, are the practice. The same recognition that the work transmits is being tested in these relationships continuously.

**The dharma question.** What the founder is here to do as a partner and a father is part of the founder's dharma, not external to it. The work being built and the family being raised are one dharma, taking two visible forms.

**The discipline of integration.** The practice does not require the family to bend around it. The work does not require the family to be background. The integration is the discipline: how does this practice serve the life including partnership and fatherhood? When it does not, the practice adjusts.

**The teaching that emerges.** What surfaces from doing this work inside this specific life is teaching that does not exist in monastic literature. The work has access to a territory most contemplative work cannot reach because most contemplative work is done in isolation from sustained partnership and active parenting. This is a content territory of unusual specificity.

The teaching, when it appears in the work, follows the discipline named in `28_FOUNDER_PRESENCE_PROTOCOL.md`: personal story enters only when the lesson translates and the teaching becomes better as a result. The specific people are not named publicly. The events are not made the subject. The recognition is the subject; the lived ground enters as the precision that makes the recognition transmissible.
