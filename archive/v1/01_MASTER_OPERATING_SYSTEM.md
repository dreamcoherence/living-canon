# Dream Coherence Master Operating System

## What Dream Coherence Is

Dream Coherence is a method for becoming lucid inside life.

It treats waking experience, sleeping dreams, subconscious patterning, nervous system state, imagination, and identity as interdependent layers of one living field of awareness.

The project has two simultaneous purposes:

1. **Inner purpose.** To live the recognition. To inhabit the dreaming with grace, lucidity, and full capacity.

2. **Outer purpose.** To create books, audio, products, and other artifacts that transmit the recognition and its practice, generating meaningful recurring revenue while serving real transformation.

## The Founder Path

The founder is the first laboratory.

The work begins as personal recognition, becomes practice, becomes method, becomes transmission, becomes artifact, becomes field.

The book is the founding artifact. Everything else flows from the book and the practice that produces the book.

## The Core Equation

Dream Coherence is the alignment of awareness, body, attention, identity, dream life, and aligned action such that life becomes the lucid participation of consciousness in its own dreaming.

## Primary Aim

To live and to transmit the recognition that life is awareness's dreaming of itself, and to demonstrate, through the lived case study of the founder and the practice of readers, that this recognition transforms what becomes possible in a life: livelihood, vitality, relationships, creative work, the texture of the days.

## The Build Sequence

The book is being built in the following order. Each phase is locked before the next begins.

**Phase 1 — Position.** Locked. See `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md`.

**Phase 2 — Voice.** Locked. See `13_VOICE_AXIOMS.md` and `12_OPENING_LOCKED.md`.

**Phase 3 — Experiential Arc.** Locked. See `17_EXPERIENTIAL_ARC.md`.

**Phase 3.5 — Rigor Map.** Current. The stress-testing of every major claim the book makes, named and bounded, before architecture rests on them. Will lock as `18_RIGOR_MAP.md`.

**Phase 4 — Architecture.** After the Rigor Map. The chapter design that serves the arc and stands on the tested ground.

**Phase 5 — Lineage and Science Map.** After architecture. The detailed engagement with each tradition and each scientific framework, calibrated to what the chapters need.

**Phase 6 — Chapter-Level Detail.** After the lineage map. The thesis, references, practices, and turning points of each chapter.

**Phase 7 — Drafting.** Slow, disciplined, one chapter at a time.

The current phase is Phase 3.5.
