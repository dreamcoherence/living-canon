# Dream Coherence. Quality, Safety, and Red Teaming.

**Status: refactored at v1.0 (May 2026). Working Voice register. The safety architecture of the offerings and the brand's quality discipline. Updated as practice work scales.**

The work touches identity revision, sleep states, dream practice, manifestation, and the dissolution of inherited frames of self. These have real psychological power, which is the point. They also carry real risk for some practitioners. This file holds the safety architecture that lets the work be done seriously without producing harm.

The brand's "no medical claims" line is necessary but not sufficient. This file specifies the operational architecture that protects practitioners while letting the work do what it is meant to do.

---

## The Frame

The work is not therapy. The work is contemplative practice in the lineage of serious traditions that have always been done with care. The safety architecture mirrors how serious lineages have always handled this: screening at the threshold, structured containers that hold the practitioner, named warning signs, clear referral paths, and a non-negotiable principle that no offering's revenue is more important than a practitioner's wellbeing.

The architecture has three layers:

**Layer One. The threshold.** Who is admitted into the work. What is screened for at intake.

**Layer Two. The container.** What disciplines hold practitioners during the work. What warning signs surface.

**Layer Three. The referral path.** What happens when a practitioner needs more than the work can offer.

Each layer is specified per offering below.

---

## Universal Disclaimers (All Offerings)

Every offering states clearly, in the practitioner-facing materials, what the work is not:

- The work is not medical or psychiatric treatment.
- The work does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition.
- The work is not a substitute for therapy, medication, or qualified mental health care.
- The work makes no guarantee of specific outcomes (financial, relational, spiritual, or otherwise).
- The work is contemplative practice and is undertaken at the practitioner's own discernment.

This language is in the website terms of practice, the welcome materials of every offering, and the application process of the deeper containers.

---

## Free Transmission Layer (Layer 0)

The free layer reaches anyone. Most readers self-select with no risk. For the small number who may be in a fragile state when they encounter the work, the architecture provides:

**On the website.** A footer link to a brief "If you are in crisis" page that names: this work is not crisis support; if you are in active crisis, here are resources (988 Lifeline in the US, equivalent in other countries, link to find-a-therapist directory).

**In the newsletter welcome sequence.** The second or third welcome email mentions briefly that the work is contemplative practice, not therapy, and includes a link to the crisis resources page.

**In audio pieces that touch potentially destabilizing territory** (dream work, identity revision, deep states). A brief verbal note at the open: this practice is contemplative work and is undertaken at your own discernment; if you are in active crisis, this is not the resource for you.

**In substantive correspondence with newsletter subscribers** that surfaces signs of crisis. The founder or whoever responds on behalf of the brand uses a templated kind response that names: this is beyond what we can support; here are resources; you matter and there are people equipped to help you well.

## First Threshold ($297-$497, 30-45 day container)

The first paid offering. Lower stakes than the deeper layers but the architecture still holds.

**Threshold (intake).**

The enrollment page asks one screening question (self-attested):

> Are you currently in acute psychological crisis (active suicidal ideation, recent psychotic episode, severe untreated bipolar mania, active substance crisis), or under active psychiatric care that your provider has not been informed about regarding contemplative practice?

If yes, the enrollment defers the practitioner to: complete current clinical work first; consult their provider about whether contemplative practice is appropriate; refund or future-credit if they have already purchased.

The screening is honest-system. The architecture does not require clinical evaluation. The screening filters out the small minority who would be harmed and self-recognize this.

**Container.**

The practice work is structured (daily audio, written practice materials) so practitioners are not left in unstructured states. The container has a clear beginning, middle, and end. There is no "advanced practice" that destabilizes earlier than the practitioner is ready for.

Three explicit warning signs are named in the welcome materials. If any surface, the practitioner is asked to pause:

- Persistent dissociation or derealization lasting more than a few hours after practice
- Mood destabilization (sustained mania, sustained severe depression) that did not exist before beginning
- Increased dream content that becomes distressing rather than instructive

The practitioner is invited to email the support contact if any of these surface. Standard response: acknowledge, recommend pausing the practice, recommend consulting a qualified mental health professional, offer refund or pause-and-resume.

**Referral path.**

The brand maintains a short list of trusted referral resources: psychotherapy directories, crisis lines, and (eventually) a few specific practitioners (somatic therapists, psychiatrists familiar with contemplative practice) who can be named when needed. The list is updated quarterly.

## Flagship Cohort ($1,497-$2,497, 8-12 weeks)

Higher stakes. Live cohort container, more sustained engagement.

**Threshold (intake).**

Enrollment includes a short pre-enrollment questionnaire (not application; cohort is open enrollment with screening):

- Self-report on prior contemplative experience
- Self-report on current psychiatric care, medications, and conditions
- Self-report on current life stability (active crisis, recent major loss, etc.)
- Acknowledgment of the universal disclaimers

The questionnaire is not a barrier; it is a calibration. The output triggers one of three paths:

- Standard enrollment (majority)
- Enrollment with a flagged note for the facilitator (e.g., active psychiatric care; the facilitator knows to check in with this practitioner)
- Deferred enrollment with a recommendation to address the current situation first

The deferred path includes a personal note from the brand (not a template) acknowledging the work matters and the timing is what is being adjusted.

**Container.**

The cohort runs in small groups (12-20 per facilitator) so each practitioner is known. Weekly group sessions and a community space provide visibility.

Facilitators (the founder in early cohorts; trained facilitators by Phase 3) are trained to recognize:

- Withdrawal from the cohort (someone going dark)
- Concerning content in shared writing or speaking
- Signs of acute distress in live sessions
- Mismatch between practitioner readiness and where the cohort is going

Each cohort has a designated mental health consultant on-call — a therapist or psychiatrist familiar with contemplative practice who can be reached for facilitator consultation (not direct practitioner care). The consultant is a paid retainer relationship and is named in the facilitator training.

Three explicit cohort-level warning signs trigger active outreach:

- Practitioner unresponsive in cohort space for more than 2 weeks
- Concerning content in writing or speaking that suggests crisis
- Practitioner self-reports they are struggling

Active outreach is a personal communication (not an automated check-in), from the founder or a senior facilitator, acknowledging the difficulty and pointing toward the referral path.

**Referral path.**

The cohort's referral list is the brand's list plus the cohort consultant's recommendations. If a practitioner needs care beyond what the cohort offers, the cohort pauses for that practitioner with refund or future-credit; clinical care comes first.

## Field Library Membership ($30-$50/month)

The membership is a less-intensive container. The architecture is lighter.

**Threshold.** No application. Standard enrollment. Universal disclaimers in the welcome materials.

**Container.** Less acute than the cohort because the work is self-paced and the community is less continuous. Risk is correspondingly lower.

**Referral path.** Standard brand referral resources.

## Deep Container ($10K-$25K, 6-12 months)

The highest-stakes offering. Application-only. Founder's direct involvement. Safety architecture is most rigorous here.

**Threshold (application).**

The application includes:

- Comprehensive self-report on contemplative history, depth of prior practice, prior teachers
- Comprehensive self-report on current mental health status, current care, current medications
- Two references (people who can speak to the applicant's stability and readiness)
- A statement on why they are applying and what they are seeking
- Explicit acknowledgment that this is contemplative work, not therapy

The application is reviewed by the founder and (by Phase 3) a senior facilitator. Some applications are accepted. Some are deferred ("come back when X is true"). Some are declined ("the work is not for you at this moment"). Declined applications receive a kind, brief note. Deferred applications get a specific reason and an invitation to apply again.

A clinical consultant reviews applications where any flag surfaced. The consultant is a paid relationship; their input is part of the screening discipline.

**Container.**

The container has the highest-touch architecture of any offering:

- Bi-weekly small group sessions where each practitioner is known
- Monthly individual check-ins (with founder or senior facilitator)
- Active monitoring by the founder for signs of difficulty
- A standing relationship with the clinical consultant who is available for practitioner referral when needed
- Clear protocols for what happens if a practitioner shows signs of crisis (immediate consultation, possible pause of practitioner's container, referral to appropriate care, refund or credit as appropriate)

**Referral path.**

The deep container's referral path includes named clinical professionals familiar with contemplative work. Some are paid retainers, available for direct referral when a practitioner needs clinical care.

## What the Founder Does Not Do

Important boundary clarifications:

**The founder does not provide clinical care.** Not therapy, not counseling, not diagnosis, not medication consultation. The founder's role is contemplative teaching and stewardship of the practice work. When clinical care is needed, the referral path is used.

**The founder does not diagnose conditions** in any practitioner, even casually. The brand does not say things like "you might have anxiety" or "this sounds like trauma." The brand may say "what you are describing is beyond what we can support; here are resources that can help well."

**The founder does not become a parental or therapeutic figure** for practitioners. The work is teacher-student in the contemplative-teacher sense, not therapist-client. The discipline of professional distance is held even in the deep container.

**The founder declines to take on practitioners who need clinical care first.** When the application process surfaces this, the decline is honest and kind.

## Quality Discipline (Brand-Level)

Beyond practitioner safety, the brand maintains quality disciplines that protect the work's integrity:

**The Voice Guardian runs on every public output.** Per `27_AI_INFRASTRUCTURE.md`, every piece passes register check before release. The Voice Guardian also checks for: hyperbolic claims, outcome-guarantees, urgency manipulation, scarcity manufacturing, and any language that promises what cannot be honored.

**The brand truth test.** Every line of brand copy is tested: stripped of styling, would this be true and useful? Pieces that fail are rebuilt.

**The Red Team agent.** For any major decision (new offering, pricing change, content territory expansion, founder appearance opportunity), the Red Team runs the decision through stress-testing: where is this false, unsafe, commercially weak, or spiritually inflated?

**Quarterly quality audits.** Once per quarter, a sample of published content is audited against the brand and safety disciplines. Drift is named and corrected.

## Lineage Provenance Discernment

Two worked examples anchor a standing discernment the brand applies to all lineage material. Carlos Castaneda's don Juan corpus and Kilton Stewart's Senoi dream-control material both present twentieth-century construction as ancient indigenous transmission; both carry usable phenomenology (dreaming attention in the first, dream-incubation framing in the second); both fail scholarship on provenance (de Mille and Fikes on Castaneda; the ethnographic record on the Senoi). The discipline: a fabricated lineage claim does not void usable content, and usable content does not launder a fabricated lineage claim. The brand draws on such material the way it draws on Neville, for phenomenology and not for provenance, names the construction for what it is, and never repeats a borrowed-ancient lineage claim in its own voice. This protects the work from the most common failure mode in the adjacent field: inherited false authority.

## Annual Safety Review

Once per year, the safety architecture is reviewed:

- What did practitioners report?
- What incidents (if any) occurred?
- What in the screening missed something?
- What in the container missed something?
- What in the referral path could be improved?
- Does the clinical consultant relationship need to expand?

The review produces specific updates to this file. The architecture is treated as a living document.

---

## What This File Does Not Pretend To Do

This file does not make the work risk-free. No contemplative work is risk-free, just as no medication is risk-free. The architecture is designed to: minimize the small subset of practitioners who would be harmed by the work; recognize warning signs early; provide clear paths to better care when needed; and protect the work's integrity by being honest about what it is and is not.

The architecture also does not pretend that all risk can be screened for at intake. Some practitioners will only reveal what they carry once they are in the work. The container discipline (active facilitation, named warning signs, established referral paths) is the second line of protection after threshold screening.

---

## The Closing Frame

The work matters. The work has real power. Held with safety architecture, the work serves the practitioners it is for and protects those it is not for. The discipline is what makes serious contemplative work possible in a moment when most contemplative work has abandoned the discipline.

Safety architecture is not a constraint on the work. Safety architecture is what lets the work be what it is.
