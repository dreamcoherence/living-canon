## The Two Pillars

Dream Coherence is taught and lived across two pillars. The pillars are one work. Most practitioners enter through one based on what their moment is calling for. Deep work integrates both.

### Pillar One. The Nature of Reality.

The view. The understanding of the realm itself. The infinite field of consciousness. The holographic and prismatic structure. How awareness becomes form. How the practitioner perceives and operates within what is.

What this pillar teaches:
- The dreamlike structure of experience
- Consciousness as the ground of all appearance
- The convergence of the contemplative streams on this single recognition
- How attention, state, identity, and resonance constitute the dream
- How the practitioner participates consciously in what is being dreamed
- The mechanics of synchronicity, manifestation, and the field's responsiveness
- The far edge of what stabilized recognition makes available

What practitioners come to this pillar for:
- Worldview clarification
- Resolution of the question of what is real
- Understanding why their practice or their life has been disclosing what it has been disclosing
- The intellectual and contemplative ground to live from
- Integration of fragmented spiritual exposure into a coherent whole

### Pillar Two. The Nature of the Self.

The dharma. The individuated path. The recognition that the same one consciousness is having its experience as a specific incarnation, and that the gift of the life is the precise dharma the one is taking, in this body, in this name, in this hour of the world.

What this pillar teaches:
- Individuation as the path of the recognized life
- The distinction between trauma-driven desire and dharma
- The cultivation of capacity. Presence, perception, embodiment, energy, expression
- Identity revision. The work of becoming the one through whom the dharma can flow
- The harmonization of the inner life with the outer expression
- The mastery of livelihood, relationship, body, creativity, contribution as the dharma matures
- Self-realization as the integration of recognition with embodied capacity

What practitioners come to this pillar for:
- Clarity on who they are and what they are here to do
- Reorientation after the collapse of inherited identity
- The cultivation of capacity to live the life that wants to be lived through them
- Integration of the recognition into the texture of their days

## The Three Layers of the Field

Each pillar moves through three layers. The layers are not levels of advancement. They are different modes of meeting the work.

### Layer One. The Recognition.

The view. The foundational orientation. The book. The long-form essays. The deepest transmissions. The poem. The opening of the website.

This layer transmits the recognition itself. It does not teach practices in detail. It establishes the ground from which practice becomes meaningful.

### Layer Two. The Practice.

The protocol. The method. The daily and weekly disciplines. The course. The membership. The audio practices. The deeper containers.

This layer turns the recognition into living practice. The five stages of the protocol (body, attention, identity, dream, reality) live here. The two pillars both move through this layer with their own practice work.

### Layer Three. The Field.

The larger ecosystem. The audience. The community. The lived case study. The arc of the work across decades. The economic infrastructure that sustains the work.

This layer is where the work matures into a sustained body. The case study deepens. The methodology refines. The community of practitioners forms around the work.

## The Offerings Ladder. Structural Level.

The full ladder is operationalized in `29_OFFERINGS_LADDER.md`. Structural summary here.

**Layer Zero. Free Transmission.**
The website. Essays. Audio. Social posts. The poem (when released). The newsletter. Everything that is given without exchange.

Purpose: build the field, transmit the recognition, establish trust, find the practitioners the deeper work is for.

**Layer One. First Threshold.**
A small first paid offering. $200 to $600. A 30 to 45 day practice container, audio plus written, that does one specific piece of work. Probably enters either through the dream-recall threshold or the evening identity-alignment threshold.

Purpose: prove the architecture, validate the brand-to-paid bridge, identify the practitioners ready for the deeper work.

**Layer Two. Flagship Cohort.**
The Dream Coherence Protocol taught across 8 to 12 weeks, twice yearly, $1,500 to $2,500. Cohort grows from 50-100 in early runs to 200-400 by mature runs.

Purpose: the engine. The first vehicle that produces meaningful revenue. Practitioners become real here. High-end candidates surface here.

**Layer Three. The Field Library (Membership).**
Monthly recurring at $30 to $50. Audio library, written archive, monthly transmissions, possibly a community layer. Built on the accumulated body of work.

Purpose: recurring revenue spine. Long-term practitioner relationship. The work continues to deepen for those who have done the cohort.

**Layer Four. The Deep Container.**
Application-only. $10,000 to $25,000 per practitioner for a 6 to 12 month container. Twenty to fifty people maximum at any time. The founder's direct work, supported by infrastructure.

Purpose: the deepest work. The high-end revenue layer. The container where the founder's presence is the point.

**Layer Five. The Limited Edition.**
Selected artifacts produced at the highest standard. The poem in printed limited edition. Audio albums on vinyl. Beautifully-bound editions of the book. Possibly other artifacts as they emerge.

Purpose: collectible-grade artifacts that contribute to brand depth and modest revenue.

**Layer Six. Retreats and In-Person.**
Optional. Decided as the field matures. $3,000 to $10,000 per practitioner. Two to four times yearly. Small, premium, selective.

Purpose: in-person depth, additional revenue, community formation. Added if and when the founder wants the in-person dimension.

## The Build Phases

The wager is ~$10M revenue by 2030. The architecture is built to make this real through outcome-anchored phases, not calendar-anchored ones.

The orienting outcome arc:
- ~$50K net by end of 2026 (~Phase 1 mid-point)
- ~$300K net annual run-rate by 2027 (~Phase 1 exit / Phase 2 entry)
- ~$1M net annual run-rate by 2028 (~Phase 2 exit / Phase 3 entry)
- ~$10M cumulative or annual at sustainable run-rate by 2030 (~Phase 4)

These are orientations. Phases exit when their conditions are met.

### Phase One. Foundation.

**Outcome.** Build the foundation. Book reaches skeleton-and-blood form, then late polish. Free transmission engine launched. First small offering scoped privately. AI infrastructure operational. Brand fully deployed across web, newsletter, social.

**Revenue during phase.** $0 to ~$50K. Mostly from the poem if released as limited edition, possibly a small first offering at the end of the phase.

**Founder time.** 20 to 30 hours per week.

**Phase exit conditions (all four):**
1. Book is locked (in late polish or published)
2. Free transmission running consistently at cadence
3. Signal list at 3K-8K
4. AI infrastructure operationally validated; first offering ready for release

Calendar orientation: most likely 12-18 months. May take longer if conditions need more time.

### Phase Two. First Transmission.

**Outcome.** Book becomes available. First paid offering live. Flagship cohort in late development.

**Revenue during phase.** $50K to ~$400K cumulative within phase. Annual run-rate by phase exit: ~$300K. Revenue does not depend on book launch spike.

**Founder time.** 25 to 35 hours per week.

**Phase exit conditions (all four):**
1. Book is publicly available
2. First offering has run 2-3 cohorts with practitioner validation
3. Flagship is in beta or ready for first run
4. Signal list at 10K-30K

Calendar orientation: most likely 12-18 months following Phase One.

### Phase Three. Architecture.

**Outcome.** Flagship cohort launches at scale. Membership launches. High-end container begins.

**Revenue during phase.** $400K to ~$3M+ cumulative within phase. Annual run-rate by phase exit: ~$1M-$3M. The architecture is earning.

**Founder time.** Stabilizes at 20 to 30 hours per week. Team begins to form.

**Phase exit conditions (all four):**
1. Flagship running at sustainable scale (200+ per cohort, twice yearly minimum)
2. Membership stabilized at 1K+ paying members
3. High-end work with first cohort completing
4. Team has formed (operations lead, editor, customer experience, clinical consultant retainer)

Calendar orientation: most likely 18-24 months.

### Phase Four. Stewardship.

**Outcome.** Every offering mature. Founder transitions from builder to steward. Wager is fulfilled or in clear final approach.

**Revenue during phase.** $3M+ cumulative within phase, approaching $10M cumulative across the full arc.

**Founder time.** Drops toward 10 to 20 hours per week.

**Phase characteristics.** Architecture mature. Team handles operations. Founder focuses on strategic direction, voice, deep containers, possibly second body of work.

Calendar orientation: by 2030 or shortly after.

## The Frame for Every Field Decision

When evaluating any field decision, the question is: does this serve the recognition, the brand, the long arc, and the wager, in that order of priority?

If a decision serves the wager but not the recognition, the decision is rejected.

If a decision serves the recognition but breaks the brand, the decision is restructured.

If a decision serves the long arc but produces no short-term value, the decision is held against the realistic budget of founder energy.

The order matters. Recognition before brand. Brand before arc. Arc before wager. The wager is reached by holding the order.
