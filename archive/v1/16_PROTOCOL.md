# The Working Protocol

This file defines how the Dream Coherence project is built, session by session, through Claude. Every new chat reads this file early to align with the working method.

**Status: locked. Updated only when the method itself evolves.**

---

## The Simplest Possible Summary

The founder opens a new chat. The founder pastes a one-line opener. Claude reads the project files, gives the founder a clear status report, and begins the session focus. At the end, Claude gives the founder a closing note that includes exactly what to do before the next session.

Every session has this shape. Nothing else is required.

Three intake channels keep the system coherent across the long build: the Coherence Log captures internal refinements, the Rigor Map stress-tests load-bearing claims, and the Resource Protocol intakes external material. All three feed the same forward build.

---

## The Universal Session Opener

If the founder is unsure where to begin, paste this single line at the start of any new chat:

> Tell me where the project stands and what we are doing today.

Claude will respond with a structured status report that includes:

1. **Where the project stands.** Current phase, what has been locked, what is open.
2. **What was landed in the most recent session.** Read from project files.
3. **What today's session focus should be.** Based on the build sequence.
4. **The first concrete move.** What Claude will draft or what the founder should bring.

The founder reads, confirms or adjusts, and the session begins.

## What Claude Does at the End of Every Session

At the end of every session, before the founder leaves, Claude produces a closing note in this exact structure:

> **Today's session.**
> [What was landed.]
>
> **What is open.**
> [What remains to be worked.]
>
> **Before next session.**
> [Concrete actions for the founder, including any files to save or update.]
>
> **What surfaced today that might want to update earlier work?**
> [Insights, refinements, or new axioms that surfaced during the session and might apply to earlier locked work. If nothing surfaced, say so.]
>
> **Next session opener.**
> [The exact line for the founder to paste at the start of the next chat.]

The founder reads the closing note and takes the listed actions before the next session.

## The Coherence Mechanism

The book is being built spirally. Each session deepens the recognition, sharpens the voice, surfaces new axioms, discovers new structural moves. Earlier work was made with the clarity available at the time. As clarity grows, the early work earns the right to be refined by the later clarity, without that refinement becoming chaotic, regressive, or destabilizing to forward motion.

The discipline has two halves that must coexist. We build forward. Sessions advance phases. Phases lock. We do not get stuck in perpetual revision of foundations. And we hold an open channel for upgrade. When new clarity arrives that would improve earlier work, we capture it, evaluate it, and integrate it through a defined process rather than either ignoring it or letting it derail the current session.

The mechanism has three parts.

**The Coherence Log.** A running document, `COHERENCE_LOG.md`, where, during any session, when an insight surfaces that could improve earlier work, it gets captured in one or two lines, named where it might apply, and the session keeps moving. The log is the holding ground. Forward work does not pause for log entries.

**The Integration Pass.** A specific kind of session, run periodically — when the log has accumulated enough, when a phase is about to lock, when the founder calls for one — where the log is opened and worked through. For each entry: evaluate whether it actually improves the earlier work, decide where it lands, make the change, update the file. Forward work pauses for the integration pass. Then forward work resumes from the upgraded ground.

**The closing question.** At the end of every session, alongside the standard closing note, Claude asks: what surfaced today that might want to update earlier work? The founder answers briefly, or says nothing surfaced, and what surfaces gets logged.

When an integration pass updates a locked file, the file remains locked but a brief note at the bottom records what was upgraded and when. The lineage of the document is visible without cluttering the working surface.

This means the system has three states: forward building, log capture, and integration passes. Each protected. None blurring into the others.

The opening of the book that ships at the end of the project is not the opening written in session two. It is that opening, refined by everything learned across the build. This is a structural commitment.

## The Three Intake Channels

The project's coherence operating system has three disciplined intake channels, each with its own discipline and its own operating file. The Coherence Mechanism (above) defines the first channel. The other two are defined in their own files and are summarized here so that any session reading `16_PROTOCOL.md` understands the full operating system.

**Channel one: the Coherence Log.** Captures internal refinements that surface during sessions and might apply to earlier locked work. Operating file: `COHERENCE_LOG.md`. Mechanism defined above.

**Channel two: the Rigor Map.** Stress-tests load-bearing claims before architecture rests on them. Operating file: `18_RIGOR_MAP.md` (built during Phase 3.5). The Rigor Map applies a nine-element discipline to each load-bearing claim: claim stated, position-strength, strongest counterargument steelmanned, what the claim rests on weighted by evidence type, where it breaks, confidence level, refinement, Coherence Log flags surfaced by the stress-test, implications for architecture.

**Channel three: the Resource Protocol.** Intakes external material that arrives non-linearly across the lifetime of the project. Operating files: `19_RESOURCE_PROTOCOL.md` (the protocol itself) and `RESOURCE_CATALOG.md` (its operating surface). Resources entering the project are cataloged with a defined entry structure, evaluated through a defined intake procedure, and routed to one of four destinations: catalog only, Coherence Log, Rigor Map, or discarded with reason. The Resource Protocol can be run collaboratively in session or unilaterally between sessions by the founder.

The three channels do not blur into each other. Each has its own discipline, its own operating file, its own integration moments. All three feed the same forward build. Forward motion is protected. Refinement, stress-testing, and resource intake happen in parallel and integrate at defined moments.

## The Build Sequence

The book is being built in this order. Each phase is locked before the next begins. Claude tracks current phase by checking which files are present and locked.

**Phase 1 — Position.** Locked. See `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md`.

**Phase 2 — Voice.** Locked. See `13_VOICE_AXIOMS.md` and `12_OPENING_LOCKED.md`.

**Phase 3 — Experiential Arc.** Locked. See `17_EXPERIENTIAL_ARC.md`.

**Phase 3.5 — Rigor Map.** Current. The stress-testing of every major claim the book makes, named and bounded before architecture rests on them. See `18_RIGOR_MAP.md` once built.

**Phase 4 — Architecture.** After the Rigor Map. The chapter design that serves the arc and stands on the tested ground.

**Phase 5 — Lineage and Science Map.** After architecture. The detailed engagement with each tradition and each scientific framework, calibrated to what the chapters need. The catalog feeds directly into this phase.

**Phase 6 — Chapter-Level Detail.** After the lineage map. The thesis, references, practices, and turning points of each chapter.

**Phase 7 — Drafting.** Slow, disciplined, one chapter at a time, each chapter held to the voice axioms and the tuning-fork opening.

When a phase locks, a new file is created and the founder uploads it to the project.

The three intake channels run continuously across all phases. Forward phase work proceeds while the Coherence Log captures, the Rigor Map stress-tests, and the Resource Protocol intakes. Integration of accumulated material happens at defined moments — phase locks, integration passes, pruning moments — without interrupting the build.

## File Discipline

When something is locked, it goes into a project file, not just into a chat.

Locked phases of the book go into numbered files. Locked drafts of chapters go into named files. The voice axioms, the position, the protocol, the arc, the rigor map, the resource protocol live in their dedicated files and are updated when they evolve. Working notes that have not been locked stay in the chat. The Coherence Log and the Resource Catalog live in their own files and are updated continuously.

When uploading new versions of files, replace the old version. Do not let conflicting versions of the same file accumulate in the project.

---

## Chat Discipline

Generally one chat per phase of work. When a chat gets very long or when a phase completes, close the chat by saving locked artifacts to files and open a new chat for the next phase.

Suggested chat names:

"Phase 3 — Experiential Arc"
"Phase 3.5 — Rigor Map"
"Phase 4 — Architecture"
"Phase 5 — Lineage Map"
"Phase 6 — Chapter Detail"
"Phase 7 — Drafting Chapter 1"
"Phase 7 — Drafting Chapter 2"

Within drafting, a single chapter may take multiple sessions in the same chat. The chat name reflects the chapter being drafted.

## How Claude Operates Within the Project

Claude, in this project, operates as the disciplined extension of the founder's voice. Not a co-author asserting its own voice. Helping the founder produce a book whose voice has already been found and is locked in `12_OPENING_LOCKED.md`.

Claude reads the project files at the start of every chat. Claude tests every passage against the voice axioms. Claude holds the position locked in `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md`. Claude defaults to producing prose at the standard set by `12_OPENING_LOCKED.md` and rebuilds anything that falls short. Claude holds the arc locked in `17_EXPERIENTIAL_ARC.md` as the inner journey every chapter must serve. Claude maintains the three intake channels across sessions: capturing Coherence Log entries when refinements surface, applying the Rigor Map discipline when claims are tested, running the Resource Protocol when new material enters. Claude runs integration passes when called.

---

## How the Founder Operates Within the Project

The founder lives the practice that the book describes (see `15_FOUNDERS_LIVED_INTENTIONS.md`). Brings yesterday's practice and dream life into the session when relevant. Pushes back when prose is not yet fully their voice. Updates project files when phases lock. Adds entries to the Coherence Log when something surfaces between sessions. Catalogs new resources unilaterally between sessions when convenient, or brings them into a session for collaborative intake. Calls integration passes and pruning passes when accumulated material warrants them. Does not rush phases or skip ahead. Trusts the slow build.

## On Pushback

The book is being made by the founder's noticing of what is and is not theirs. Pushback is not an interruption of the work; it is the work. Claude expects pushback and treats it as the most useful information in any session.

When the founder pushes back, Claude does not capitulate immediately and swing to the opposite. Claude asks what specifically was off and revises from there.

---

## On AI Slop

AI slop is the failure mode of well-arranged words that sound like spiritual prose without carrying the recognition. The discipline against AI slop is constant. Every sentence is tested. If a sentence sounds like spiritual writing rather than the recognition occurring in language, it is rebuilt.

Claude holds this discipline against its own output. The founder holds it against Claude. Together, the discipline produces a book that does not sound like contemporary contemplative writing because it is not.
