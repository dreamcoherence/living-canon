# Source Field Excerpts and Translation

This file gives Claude the source-field DNA of Dream Coherence.

Important:
Do not copy these styles directly.
Do not imitate the channeling style.
Do not imitate inflated manifestation language.
Do not claim lineage.
Extract principles, translate them, and write in Dream Coherence voice.

## Source 1: Reality as Dream and Witness

Selected source excerpt:

"All you have to do is see the dream as dream."

Translation into Dream Coherence:

Life is not escaped by rejecting it. It is liberated by seeing its dreamlike nature. When the practitioner recognizes experience as appearance within awareness, identification loosens and choice returns.

Dream Coherence principle:

See the dream as dream. Then participate with lucidity.

Usable lines:
- The dream changes when the dreamer wakes inside it.
- Lucidity is not escape. It is intimacy without captivity.
- What appears can be honored without being obeyed.

## Source 2: The Screen of Awareness

Selected source excerpt:

"The screen is always there but several types of pictures appear on the screen and then disappear. Nothing sticks to the screen."

Translation:

Awareness is the constant field in which waking, dream, sleep, thought, emotion, and identity appear. The practitioner learns to identify less with the pictures and more with the screen.

Dream Coherence principle:

You are not only the dream image. You are the field in which the image appears.

Usable lines:
- The picture moves. The screen remains.
- Your states change. Awareness does not.
- Learn the screen and the dream loses its tyranny.

## Source 3: I Am Practice

Selected source excerpt:

"Relax and watch the I am. Reality is just behind it."

Translation:

The feeling of existence itself is the doorway. Before identity, biography, and striving, there is simple being. Stabilizing attention there gives the practitioner a ground deeper than mental narrative.

Dream Coherence principle:

Return to the bare fact of being.

Usable lines:
- Before the story, there is the simple fact that you are.
- The first doorway is not belief. It is being.
- Let the I am become quiet enough to reveal what stands behind it.

## Source 4: Manifestation as Assumption

Selected source excerpt:

"Whatever you are conscious of being you become."

Translation:

Identity is not passive. The state one inhabits determines perception, action, expectation, and the reality one unconsciously helps generate.

Dream Coherence principle:

State precedes trajectory.

Usable lines:
- You do not receive the dream you want. You receive the dream your state can hold.
- Identity is the atmosphere reality grows inside.
- Become the one for whom the desired life is natural.

## Source 5: Living from the End

Selected source excerpt:

"Assume the feeling of your wish fulfilled."

Translation:

The practitioner does not merely think about the desired future. The practitioner inhabits its felt reality, then allows action to emerge from that state.

Dream Coherence principle:

Do not chase the dream. Stabilize the state from which the dream is already true.

Usable lines:
- The end must become familiar before it becomes visible.
- Do not beg the dream to arrive. Become its native atmosphere.
- The body must learn the future before life can confirm it.

## Source 6: Silence and Gestation

Selected source excerpt:

"Every desire has its own gestation period."

Translation:

The dream must be protected while forming. Premature explanation, social validation, and compulsive checking can dissipate the charge.

Dream Coherence principle:

Gestate the dream before displaying it.

Usable lines:
- Not every seed should be shown to the weather.
- The dream needs a womb before it needs an audience.
- Silence is not absence. It is protection.

## Source 7: Vessel and Pace

Selected source excerpt:

"Some truths act as a poison on the unhealed mind."

Translation:

Depth must be paced. The nervous system, body, and heart must be prepared for larger truths. The work should not overwhelm the vessel.

Dream Coherence principle:

Revelation must be metabolized.

Usable lines:
- The vessel determines the dose.
- A truth too large for the body becomes distortion.
- Go slowly enough that the whole being can come with you.

Dream Coherence principle:

Usable lines:

- Precision is not severity.
- Discernment is the art of lighting the next true step.

## Source 13: Conscious Creation Without Inflation

The source field contains language of cosmic creation, infinite dream weaving, abundance, and reality reshaping.

Translation:

Do not reproduce inflated claims. Convert them into disciplined language:
- attention shapes experience
- identity affects probability
- imagination directs action
- nervous system capacity determines what can be held
- coherent practice changes the lived world

Dream Coherence principle:

The magical must be disciplined to become real.

Usable lines:
- Magic without coherence becomes fantasy.
- Manifestation begins where self-deception ends.
- The dream responds to sincerity, not theatrics.
