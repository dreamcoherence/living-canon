Sits alongside `26_AUDIENCE_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md` (the strategic engine) and `26a_DOORWAY_CONTENT_MAP_MONTHS_1_TO_3.md` (the twelve-piece spine). ## The Channels

Four primary channels in the first 90 days. Audio enters in month two and ramps to weekly. Two adjacent channels held for later phases.

**Primary channels (active in 90-day rollout):**
1. The website (`dreamcoherence.com`)
2. The newsletter (Beehiiv)
3. X / Twitter
4. Instagram

**Channel entering and ramping in month two:**
5. Audio (Spotify and website audio library)

**Channels held for later phases:**
6. YouTube (potential, held for Phase 1 exit; expensive to produce at the brand's quality bar)
7. Self-hosted podcast (held for opportunistic interviews only in Phase 1)

The discipline: build deep on four channels. Do not spread thin. As the field matures, audio expands and YouTube may enter. Until then, the four channels carry the work.

## Audio Is the Underweighted Power Channel

A specific calibration note worth holding at the top of the file.

The practitioner the work is for — mid-life, contemplatively experienced, intellectually serious — consumes audio more than they read essays as often as the work writes them. They listen on walks, while driving, during retreat, before sleep. Audio is the highest-leverage format for this audience.

The earlier scaffolding of this rollout undercounted audio at two pieces in 90 days. The revised cadence (this file) ramps audio to one piece per week starting month two. Pieces can be short (5–8 minutes) when the longer form is not earned. The discipline is consistency, not length.

The audio library becomes the work's primary growing asset alongside the essay archive. The essays are the deepening; the audio is the daily companion.

---

## The Quality Override

Better to ship two great essays in 90 days than three with one undercooked. The brand's signal is seriousness. When a piece is not ready, the piece does not ship.

The override applies most strongly to long-form essays and audio pieces, where quality and depth are the brand's recognizable signal. The override applies less strongly to X and Instagram surface posts, where consistency of presence at the brand's voice register is the discipline.

The discipline: never feed the schedule at the expense of force. The schedule below is the target. The quality is the non-negotiable.

---

## The Flex Clause

If founder production fatigue threatens the cadence, the order of reduction is:

1. Instagram cadence reduces first (from 3–4 per week to 2–3, or to 1–2 if needed)
2. X cadence reduces second (from 5–7 to 4–5)
3. Newsletter, website essay, and audio cadence are the non-negotiables and are held last

The newsletter, the long-form essays, and the audio are the work's body. The X and Instagram surfaces are the work's discovery layer. When the choice has to be made, the body holds.

## Mid-Quarter Check at Day 45

Calendared in advance as a non-negotiable.

On day 45, the founder pauses and runs a one-hour check. The check is structured around five questions:

**One. Is the cadence holding?** Have the published pieces actually shipped on schedule? Where has the schedule bent, and was the bend the quality override doing its job, or was it production fatigue?

**Two. Is the quality holding?** Read the most recent two essays, the most recent four newsletter issues, the most recent ten X posts, and the most recent six Instagram pieces. Honestly assess: does the work sound like the brand at the level the v2 crystallization and the positioning passage specify? Where has the voice drifted? Where has it held?

**Three. What is the audience signal?** Newsletter open rates, growth, replies. X engagement at the meta-level (which posts have landed, which haven't — not for optimization, but for signal about what is resonating with the practitioner the work is for). Instagram follower growth and saved pieces.

**Four. Where does production cadence want to flex?** If the founder is fatiguing, the flex clause activates. Instagram first, X second, newsletter and long-form and audio last. If the founder is finding capacity exceeds the schedule, what wants to be added (e.g., a second audio piece per week, a guest essay, an interview transcript)?

**Five. Does anything need to be ejected?** Sometimes a piece in the schedule, in hindsight, was the wrong piece. The day-45 check is the moment to eject what is not serving the work and replace it with what is.

The output of the mid-quarter check is a brief written note (10–15 minutes of writing) saved as a dated entry. This note informs the second half of the quarter and the next quarter's planning.

## External Ear: The Brand Voice Check

This is an open question for the founder to resolve before day 30.

Brand interior discipline degrades silently. A trusted outside reader — not Claude, not an AI, not the founder — should read the published work periodically and flag when the voice is drifting toward the manifestation, wellness, or coaching registers the positioning passage filters out.

The check is needed because the founder is too close to their own prose to catch drift reliably, and the close-in editors (Claude) are part of the system being calibrated. An outside ear catches what the inside cannot.

The founder identifies one such person by day 30 and asks them to read what is published at month one and month two. The reader does not need to be a contemplative practitioner. The reader needs to be discerning about voice, register, and what counts as the brand's allergens.

This is a named action item with an owner: the founder. By day 30, this should be resolved.

## The Founder's Production Cadence Across 90 Days

To make this rollout real, the founder produces in this cadence:

**Weekly:**
- 1 newsletter issue (1–2 hours)
- 5–7 X posts (drawn from in-progress writing and the body of work; ~1 hour per week of dedicated time, plus ambient)
- 3–4 Instagram pieces (~1–2 hours per week including image work)
- 1 audio piece from week 6 onward (2–4 hours scripting plus 1–2 hours recording and light editing)

**Monthly:**
- 1 long-form essay (5–8 hours focused drafting session per piece, plus revision time)

**Across the quarter:**
- 1 carousel (Instagram, month 1)
- 1 short video (Instagram, month 2)
- 1 offering page (website, month 3)
- 12 aphorisms ready for ongoing X / Instagram use

Total founder time across 90 days: approximately 80–120 hours of focused work. Roughly 9–13 hours per week. The weekly audio cadence increases founder time by about 3–6 hours per week from week 6 onward.

The cadence is honest and sustainable. The quality override and the flex clause protect against the schedule capturing the work.

## What Is Open

This rollout map is v1. The pieces calibrate against:

- What actually publishes (some pieces may want longer revision than the schedule allows; the cadence flexes to quality per the override)
- What the founder discovers in practice about own production cadence
- What surfaces on each channel (some platforms may work better than expected; cadence reweights toward what works)
- What practitioner response looks like (engagement, replies, newsletter open rates) — not as growth-hacking metric, but as signal of what is landing

By day 45, the mid-quarter check informs cadence adjustments for the second half. The next 90-day map (days 91–180) is drafted around day 75, informed by the first half's data.
