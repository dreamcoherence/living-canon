## The Mark

A logarithmic spiral. Eight turns. Inward-contracting. A single point of gold at the center.

### What the mark holds

The spiral is the geometric signature of consciousness becoming form. Galaxies, hurricanes, nautilus shells, the cochlea of the inner ear, the unfurling fern, the curl of a wave. The same curve appears wherever life recognizes itself across scales.

The outer arms are the field, manifest awareness expanding into the world. The contraction toward center is the practitioner's path. The mark teaches the eye to look the way the protocol teaches the practitioner to look.

At the eye of the spiral sits a single point in gold. The bindu, the Sanskrit word for point or drop, is awareness itself. The thing the entire form is pointing toward.

### Construction

Phi-derived. Radius multiplies by phi (1.618) per full turn. Eight turns total, a Fibonacci number. Phi-per-turn (rather than the more aggressive phi-per-quarter-turn of the classical golden spiral) is the deliberate calibration that lets the mark hold many visible turns within a centered, contemplative composition.

### What the mark must never do

- Never appear in colors other than Moon (or Night on light backgrounds) for the curve.
- Never have the bindu in any color other than Gold (#C49A4F).
- Never have effects applied. No gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D.
- Never be tilted, rotated arbitrarily, or mirrored.
- Never be combined with other symbols, icons, or decorative elements.
- Never be reproduced freehand. Always use the official files.
- Never appear smaller than 24px wide (below this the bindu disappears; use the favicon-optimized variant for sizes between 16 and 32px).
- Never be cropped, truncated, or partially visible.
- Never have its growth rate or turn count modified.

## Lockups

Three configurations. Use the simplest version that does the work.

**Mark alone.** The default for established brand contexts. Favicon, avatar, watermark, footer. When the brand is already known, the mark alone is sufficient and stronger than the lockup.

**Stacked lockup.** For square applications. Spotify, Instagram avatar, book covers, album covers. The wordmark sits below the bindu at a distance equal to one and a half times the mark's radius.

**Horizontal lockup.** For headers, email signatures, video lower-thirds. The wordmark begins one mark-radius to the right of the mark's outer edge.

## Color

Three colors. Restraint as authority. Night is the field. Moon is the structural line. Gold is the bindu, a single point of light, never used in volume. The discipline of restraint is what makes the gold meaningful when it appears.

| Color | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | `#0A0E1A` | 10 14 26 | Field. Dominant background. Substrate of consciousness. |
| Moon | `#F0EFE8` | 240 239 232 | Structural line. Primary text on dark grounds. Quietude. |
| Gold | `#C49A4F` | 196 154 79 | Bindu only. Awareness recognizing itself. Scarcity makes it precious. |
| Field | `#1A2332` | 26 35 50 | Secondary tonal. Slightly lifted background for cards, panels, subtle layering. Tonal variation of Night, not a fourth brand color. |

### What never appears

- Pure black or pure white.
- Saturated colors of any kind.
- Trendy colors (sage, hot pink, terracotta, lavender).
- Gradients between brand colors.
- More than three colors in any single composition.
- Drop shadows, glows, or any color-based effects.

## Typography

Two faces. Two weights.

**EB Garamond.** Carries every emotional and contemplative surface. Display (italic for wordmark, headlines, pull-quotes). Body (regular for newsletters, books, long-form).

**Inter.** Handles every utility surface. Navigation, captions, eyebrow labels, metadata.

### Hierarchy

| Use | Face | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / cover headlines | EB Garamond Italic | 64-96px | letter-spacing +1 to +2 |
| Section headings | EB Garamond Italic | 36-48px | letter-spacing +1 |
| Subheadings | EB Garamond Italic | 24-32px | |
| Pull-quotes | EB Garamond Italic | 22-28px | |
| Newsletter body | EB Garamond Regular | 17-18px | line-height 1.7-1.8 |
| Book interior body | EB Garamond Regular | 11-12pt | line-height 1.5 |
| Captions / notes | EB Garamond Regular | 14-15px | often italic |
| Eyebrow labels | Inter | 11px | letter-spacing +3 to +4, all caps |
| Navigation | Inter Regular | 14px | |
| Buttons | Inter Medium | 14px | |
| Metadata | Inter Regular | 12px | |

### Type rules

- Two weights only. 400 (regular) and 500 (medium). No bold, no semibold, no light.
- Never use sans-serif for emotional or quotable content.
- Never center long passages of body text. Left-aligned for readability.
- Never use Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or other contemplative-brand serifs. They collapse Dream Coherence into the visual category we are differentiating from.

## Voice

Six anchors. Every line of brand copy satisfies at least three. Every line violates none.

**Calm.** Never urgent. Never breathless. Never marketing-anxious.

**Declarative.** Says what is, not what might be. Avoids hedges. Avoids the *I think* preface.

**Precise.** Each word does work. Every sentence could be stripped of styling and still carry meaning.

**Slightly oracular.** Carries weight. Sounds like it could have been written 200 years ago. Or 200 years from now.

**Quotable.** Designed for screenshot. Compressed enough to stand alone outside its original context.

**Personal but anonymous.** First and second person used freely. The *I* is universal, not biographical.

### Strong vs. weak

Strong:
- The field is already here. You did not have to make it.
- Sleep is not absence. It is rehearsal.
- Begin with the body. The mind follows.
- What you wake from is what you wake to.
- The dream is not somewhere else. You are in it now.
- Attention is the first instrument.
- The body must learn the future before life can confirm it.

Weak (rewrite):
- Unlock your highest timeline through fractal manifestation.
- Vibrate at the frequency of your dreams.
- Step into your power and become limitless.
- Hey beautiful soul, today we are going to talk about quantum consciousness.
- Don't miss out, transform your life today.
- Let me show you the ancient secret.
- You are infinite. You are eternal. You are everything.

### The brand truth test

Before publishing any line, ask: if you removed all aesthetic styling and read this in plain text, would it still be true and useful?

If the answer is *no, it sounds profound but does not actually mean anything specific*, cut it or rewrite. The voice earns its weight through truth, not through ornament.

### Words to handle carefully

These words have been claimed by the contemporary spiritual register and tend to read as filler. Use only when operationally specific. Otherwise replace.

| Word | When acceptable | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| Infinite | Mathematical contexts only | Vast, without end, or describe specifically |
| Fractal | When literally describing self-similarity across scale | Describe the actual repetition |
| Emergent | When describing genuine emergence | Arises, appears, or describe what arises |
| Vibration | Never (in physics-of-feelings sense) | Describe the actual feeling |
| Frequency | Never (in spiritual sense) | Describe the actual state |
| Energy | Sparingly | Describe what is actually happening |
| Manifest | Sparingly, only where specifically meaningful | Bring into being, produce, or be specific |
| Sacred | Never as decoration | Use only when the precise word |

### What the voice never does

- Never breezy ("hey friends!").
- Never marketing-urgent ("don't miss out").
- Never hyperbolic ("life-changing," "transformative," "revolutionary").
- Never falsely intimate ("I see you," "I feel you").
- Never aggressive ("most people are sleepwalking").
- Never makes promises it cannot keep ("guaranteed awakening").
- Never claims lineage authority Dream Coherence does not have.
- Never quotes specific traditions as if speaking from inside them.
- Never uses emojis. Rare exception: ✦ or ◯ as visual punctuation.

## The Relationship Between Brand Voice and Sovereign Voice

The Sovereign Voice (book voice, locked in `13_VOICE_AXIOMS.md`) is one register higher than the Brand Voice. It does less, with more weight. It uses the present tense more strictly, refuses em dashes, holds the no-positionality discipline of Axiom 11.

The Brand Voice is the field's working register. It honors the same discipline (calm, precise, restrained, no slop, no hyperbole) but allows more accessibility, more conventional sentence shapes, more direct addressing of the reader where the surface requires it.

A line in the Brand Voice never violates the Sovereign Voice's hard rules. A line in the Sovereign Voice may not work on social media because it is too dense.

The two registers serve each other. The Sovereign Voice carries the book. The Brand Voice carries everything else.

## Imagery

All imagery serves the same atmosphere. Contemplative, dim, dreamlike, slightly mythic. Supports the work without illustrating it.

### What imagery looks like

- Atmospheric, dreamlike, contemplative.
- Deep night skies, moonlight, water reflections, dim landscapes.
- Soft focus, slight grain, gentle depth-of-field.
- Solitary figures, silhouetted, never identifiable.
- Architectural elements. Doorways, thresholds, columns, archways.
- Symbolic objects. A hand, a single flame, a bowl of water, a stone.
- Liminal spaces. The threshold between waking and sleeping. Dawn, dusk.

### What imagery never looks like

- Bright, cheerful, optimistic Instagram aesthetic.
- Crystal-shop spirituality with rainbow chakras.
- New-age cosmic with neon colors.
- Manifestation TikTok with affirmation overlays.
- Wellness influencer with palm trees and morning light.
- Stock photography of any kind.
- Recognizable famous artworks or copyrighted visual references.
- Recognizable real people. The brand is faceless.

### Standard image generation prompt

`[subject], dim moonlight, deep blue and gold tones, atmospheric, contemplative, soft focus, film grain, mystical mood, archetypal, shot on Hasselblad, dramatic chiaroscuro, --ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6`

## Application Specifications

### Website (dreamcoherence.com)

- Hero. Full mark on Night, wordmark below in stacked configuration.
- Navigation. Inter Medium 14px, letter-spacing +2.
- Body. EB Garamond Regular 17-18px, line-height 1.7.
- Headings. EB Garamond Italic per the typography hierarchy.
- Favicon. 32px optimized variant of the mark (4 turns instead of 8).
- Footer. Small horizontal lockup centered, minimal links.

### Newsletter (Beehiiv or equivalent)

- Header. Horizontal lockup, light or dark variant per hero image.
- Issue title. EB Garamond Italic 28-32px.
- Body. EB Garamond Regular 17-18px, line-height 1.7.
- Eyebrow labels. Inter 11px, letter-spacing +3, Gold.
- Length. 600-2000 words per issue.

### Book cover (6x9in)

- Background. Night.
- Mark. Stacked lockup centered, approximately 25% of cover height.
- Title. EB Garamond Italic 36-44pt, Moon.
- Subtitle. EB Garamond Italic 14-16pt, gray.
- Top label. *Dream Coherence*, Inter 10pt, letter-spacing +4, Gold.
- Author. Inter 11-12pt, letter-spacing +4, Moon, all caps.

### Album cover (Spotify, 3000x3000)

- Background. Night, or atmospheric image with strong dark gradient.
- Mark. Stacked lockup or large mark alone.
- Album/track title. EB Garamond Italic, large, centered or bottom.
- Artist name. Inter 11pt, letter-spacing +4, all caps.
- Top label. *Dream Coherence*, Inter 10pt, letter-spacing +4, Gold.

### Instagram post (1080x1080)

- Background. Atmospheric image with dark gradient overlay if text is added.
- Text overlay. EB Garamond Italic, single phrase, upper or lower third.
- Negative space. Minimum 40% of canvas.
- Mark watermark. Lower-right corner, mark only, 30% opacity, ~60px.
- Caption. 1-3 sentences in brand voice. No CTA in caption (use bio link).

### YouTube thumbnail (1280x720)

- Background. Atmospheric image as ground.
- Title text. EB Garamond Italic, 4-6 words maximum, Moon.
- Style. Should look like a film poster or album cover.
- Never. Clickbait, faces, arrows, surprised expressions.

### Twitter/X post

- Image. Atmospheric, brand palette, square or 16:9.
- Text overlay. EB Garamond Italic, single phrase, Moon.
- Post text. 1-3 short lines, designed for quote-tweet.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

For any visual output:

- Does this use only the three brand colors (plus Field for layering)?
- Is the typography only EB Garamond and Inter?
- Are there only one or two weights of any face used?
- If the mark appears, is it correctly proportioned and not modified?
- If there is text, does it pass the brand truth test?
- Are filler words defined or replaced?
- Does it look like Dream Coherence and nothing else?

## Hard Rules

What never changes. These do not get revisited.

- The mark is a phi-derived logarithmic spiral. No other geometric form replaces it.
- Three colors only. Night, Moon, Gold. Plus Field for tonal layering. No exceptions.
- EB Garamond for emotional content. Inter for utility. No other faces.
- Two weights only across the entire system. 400 and 500.
- The brand is faceless. No real names, no real faces, no real voice attached to the brand identity. The book author name is separate from the brand identity (see `28_FOUNDER_PRESENCE_PROTOCOL.md`).
- No emojis except occasional ✦ or ◯ as visual punctuation.
- No hyperbole, no urgency theater, no false intimacy.
- No appropriated lineage names where appropriation would distort.
- No claims that cannot be honored. No guaranteed awakening. No manifestation outcomes. No medical claims.
- No advertising in or around the brand. Revenue comes from offerings the work itself produces.
