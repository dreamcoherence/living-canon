## The Ladder

Seven layers, from free transmission to the deepest container. Each layer serves a specific purpose. Each layer respects the brand discipline. No layer competes with another; they nest.

```
LAYER 6 — RETREATS AND IN-PERSON (OPTIONAL, PHASE 3+)
   $3,000-10,000 / 2-4x year / small selective gatherings
                          ▲
LAYER 5 — LIMITED EDITION ARTIFACTS
   $50-$1,000 / collectible-grade physical works
                          ▲
LAYER 4 — THE DEEP CONTAINER
   $10,000-$25,000 / 6-12 months / application-only / 20-50 practitioners
                          ▲
LAYER 3 — THE FIELD LIBRARY (MEMBERSHIP)
   $30-$50/month / recurring / accumulated body of work + monthly transmissions
                          ▲
LAYER 2 — THE FLAGSHIP COHORT
   $1,500-$2,500 / 8-12 weeks / twice yearly / 50-400 practitioners per run
                          ▲
LAYER 1 — THE FIRST THRESHOLD
   $200-$600 / 30-45 day practice container / audio + written
                          ▲
LAYER 0 — FREE TRANSMISSION (THE ENGINE)
   Website, newsletter, social, audio, poem (free or limited edition)
```

Practitioners enter at Layer 0 and rise as the work calls them. The ladder is real but not coercive; no one is required to climb. Each layer stands on its own value.

## The Coherence Arc the Ladder Serves

Beneath the seven layers runs the practitioner's interior arc: the rungs of coherence, the degrees of rising lucidity in the waking dream. The layers are what the field offers. The rungs are where the practitioner actually stands. The layers serve the rungs; they do not replace them, and a practitioner may sit at any rung within any layer.

Four rungs:

- **Noticing.** Catching that it is a dream at all. The first flickers of lucidity in waking life. Met first by Layer 0, the free transmission, where the recognition is encountered for the first time.
- **Steadying.** Holding the recognition past the flicker, into a daily, repeatable presence. Served by Layer 1, the First Threshold, the small deep practice container.
- **Acting.** Moving within the dream from presence rather than reaction, authoring rather than being run by pattern. Served by Layer 2, the flagship cohort, where the recognition becomes applied, and deepened in the direct work of Layer 4.
- **Abiding.** Coherence as a standing condition. The witness stable, lucidity become a level of being, the threshold of liberation while living. Served by Layer 4, the deep container, and held by the whole field thereafter.

The rungs are the honest form of the promise. The work never asks a practitioner to be awake. It offers to raise their coherence one degree, from wherever they stand. That is teachable, repeatable, and true, and it is what each layer of the ladder is for. The dream becomes responsive in proportion to the rung, and it answers to being, not to wishing.

## The Customer Experience Discipline (All Layers)

Before specifying each layer, the universal felt-sense discipline that holds across all offerings.

The brand's restraint extends into the texture of every interaction. The way an email arrives. The pace at which an offering is revealed. The feel of the checkout. Every touchpoint either reinforces the brand's calm or erodes it. The discipline:

**No countdown timers anywhere.** Not on enrollment pages, not in emails, not in any banner. The pressure of a ticking clock contradicts everything the work is built on.

**No fake scarcity.** The flagship cohort caps at the number it can serve well, not at an artificially small number to manufacture urgency. The deep container is capacity-bound by what the founder can hold. Capacity is honest; scarcity is not invented.

**No exit-intent popups.** No "wait, before you go" overlays. No "are you sure you want to leave" interruptions. If a visitor is leaving, they leave.

**No upsells at checkout.** What is purchased is what is purchased. No "add this for $97" surprise add-ons. No "complete your order with" cross-sells.

**Enrollment closes by clock, not by inventory** (unless inventory is genuinely capped). When the flagship cohort closes enrollment on a specific date, the date is the date. The architecture does not manipulate enrollment numbers to create artificial closings.

**Refunds without forms.** Any practitioner who completes an offering and feels they did not receive what was promised is refunded without argument and without requiring justification. The brand's discipline is its integrity.

**No urgency language in emails.** "Closing soon" is acceptable when factually true. "Last chance!" "Don't miss out!" "Final hours!" are not. The voice stays in register.

**No social proof manipulation.** Testimonials, when used at all, are real and unedited. No "300 students enrolled in the last hour" counters. No popup notifications of fake recent purchases.

**No abandoned-cart aggression.** A single follow-up email is acceptable. A sequence of 5 cart-recovery emails is not.

**Checkout pages are calm.** The brand's typography. Generous space. No "limited time" banners. No countdown timers. The checkout looks like the rest of the site.

**Welcome experiences are paced.** When a practitioner buys an offering, they are welcomed with care, not with an immediate barrage of upsells, recommendations, and additional offerings.

This discipline is enforced by the founder's review of all customer-facing experiences before launch. The discipline is enforced quarterly through customer-experience audits. Drift is corrected immediately.

The discipline is not a marketing strategy. The discipline is the brand's integrity made operational. Practitioners feel the difference even when they cannot name it.

## Layer 1. The First Threshold.

The first paid offering. A small, deep, well-built practice container.

### Working name

*The First Threshold*. May refine to a specific name when launched.

### What it is

A 30 to 45 day practice container, delivered through a sequence of audio practices, written practice materials, and email or platform-delivered guidance. The container does one specific piece of work, named at the entry: most likely either *the dream-recall threshold* or *the evening identity-alignment threshold*. ### Format

- Daily audio guidance (5-15 min each, total 30-45 audio pieces)
- Written practice notes (PDF or web-hosted)
- A single 60-90 minute orientation audio at the start
- A single closing reflection audio at the end
- Optional: a private space (Circle or similar) where participants can ask the founder one question they will receive a personal response to (high-touch but bounded)

### Price

$297 to $497 depending on what the format ends up holding. Possibly an early-access price of $197 for the first cohort to validate.

### Build timing

Built privately in Months 6-11. Beta-tested with 20-50 practitioners in Months 11-12. Public release coordinated with book availability in Months 13-15. The release is not designed to spike around book launch; the release happens when the offering is ready and the book is available.

### Safety

Per `10_QUALITY_SAFETY_AND_RED_TEAMING.md`: single screening question at enrollment, three named warning signs in welcome materials, referral path active.

### Discipline

Released only after the book is locked. The voice of the first offering must match what the book has established. The container delivers what it promises in clear, testable terms.

### Revenue expectation

Year 2: $20K-$100K. Year 3: $30K-$150K. Year 4-5 cumulative: $80K-$400K.

### What this offering is not

Not a course. Not a 90-day program. Not an introductory bundle. Not a tripwire. A single small, deep piece of work, sold at the price of doing real work.

## Layer 3. The Field Library (Membership).

Monthly recurring access to the accumulated body of work and ongoing transmissions.

### Working name

*The Field Library*. Possibly refined.

### What it is

Monthly recurring access to:

- Complete audio library (accumulated practices, atmospheric pieces, audio essays)
- Complete written archive (essays not yet released publicly, deeper transmissions, additional practice materials)
- A monthly new transmission (audio + written, sent to members only)
- An optional community layer (scoped in detail below)

### The Community Layer

A specific decision required when the membership launches. Three options under consideration:

**Option A. No community layer.** The membership is purely a library plus monthly transmissions. Members consume independently. No social space.

**Option B. The salon.** A slow-correspondence community. Quarterly written prompts the membership responds to. Monthly threads on specific recognitions. A single moderated space (Circle or similar) where members can engage in slow, considered written exchange. Not a Discord. Not a daily feed. More like a 19th-century literary salon transposed to digital. The brand's restraint is enforced through architecture (no real-time chat, no notification floods, slow defaults).

**Option C. The gathering.** A quarterly real-time gathering (online or hybrid in-person/online). Members convene for a 90-120 minute deep session held by a senior facilitator or the founder. Practitioners get to be in real-time relationship with each other and the work. Recordings available for those who cannot attend live. Outside of the gatherings, the community is a slow correspondence space (as in Option B).

Recommendation: Option C, with the understanding that the community layer is built only after the membership has 200+ founding members and there is a real population to gather. Until then, the membership is library + monthly transmissions only (Option A).

The community layer is scoped in detail in Phase 3 when the membership launches.

### Price

$30 to $50 per month. Annual option at 10-15% discount.

### Build timing

Built in Months 24-30 (designed around the body of work that has accumulated by Year 3). Launched in Month 31.

### Member growth

- Launch (Year 3 H1): 200-500 founding members
- Year 3 close: 500-1,500
- Year 4: 1,500-3,000
- Year 5: 3,000-5,000+

### Safety

Per `10_QUALITY_SAFETY_AND_RED_TEAMING.md`: standard threshold, lighter container appropriate to self-paced work, standard referral resources.

### Revenue expectation

Year 3: $200K-$700K. Year 4-5 cumulative: $1M-$4M.

### Discipline

The membership is the recurring spine. It must hold value independent of any cohort. Members are not auto-enrolled from cohort graduation; they choose. Cancellation must be easy (single click, no retention emails). Quality is held.

If the community layer fails (becomes low-signal, becomes parasocial, becomes drama-prone), it is closed. The membership reverts to library plus monthly transmissions. The community layer is dispensable; the membership is not.

## Layer 4. The Deep Container.

The founder's direct work with small numbers of advanced practitioners.

### Working name

*The Deep Container*. Refined to a final name when scoped in detail in Phase 2.

### What it is

A 6 to 12 month direct-work container. The founder is present in the container in a meaningful way. The container goes to depths the flagship cannot reach because it is small enough for individual depth.

### Format (working scope, refined in Phase 2)

- Application-only entry (per `10_QUALITY_SAFETY_AND_RED_TEAMING.md`)
- 20 to 50 practitioners in the container at any time
- Bi-weekly small group sessions with the founder (12-20 per group)
- Monthly individual check-in with the founder (30-45 min) or a senior facilitator
- A complete deep-work curriculum that goes beyond what the flagship covers
- Possibly an in-person gathering once per container (if retreats are added to the architecture)
- Access to a private community of fellow container practitioners
- Clinical consultant available for referral when needed

### What the curriculum holds (working scope, refined in Phase 2)

The deep container goes where the flagship does not. The scoping question for Phase 2: what specific work does the deep container do?

Working hypothesis (held for refinement):

The deep container holds Pillar Two work in particular depth — the dharma question, the individuation work, the specific gift the life is. The flagship teaches the protocol. The deep container helps the practitioner integrate the recognition into the specific life they are living, with attention to livelihood, relationship, body, creative expression, and the practitioner's particular dharma.

The container also serves the practitioner who has done the flagship and recognizes that the next layer of work is not more content but more direct relationship — with the work, with the founder's stewardship, with other practitioners at similar depth.

This is scaffolding. The actual scoping happens in a dedicated Phase 2 session when the architecture is asking to be built.

### Price

$10,000 to $25,000 per practitioner per year. Possibly $15,000 as the working price point for first version.

### Build timing

Scoped in detail in Months 30-36. First container launches in Month 37. Small first cohort of 15-20 selected practitioners.

### Safety

Per `10_QUALITY_SAFETY_AND_RED_TEAMING.md`: most rigorous architecture in the system. Comprehensive application, references, clinical consultant review of flagged applications, named clinical professionals available for referral, founder's active monitoring throughout the container.

### Revenue expectation

Year 3 (late): $200K-$300K (small first cohort). Year 4: $500K-$1.5M. Year 5: $750K-$2M+.

### Discipline

This layer is capacity-bound by founder energy. Cannot scale beyond ~50 practitioners at a time without diluting what the layer offers. The container is the steward layer; it is the founder's direct work.

The Phase 2 scoping session for this offering is a critical architecture decision. The container's specific transmission, curriculum, and structure are decided then, before the offering ships.

## The Free / Paid Boundary

The boundary between Layer 0 (free) and Layers 1+ (paid) is held with discipline.

What is given freely:
- The website's writing and audio
- The newsletter in full
- Social posts in full
- The poem (or a free reading of the poem with the limited edition reserved for paying)
- Audio essays (full pieces, not teasers)
- The general protocol's architecture (described in writing on the website, but the daily practices and detailed instruction live in paid offerings)

What lives behind the paid layer:
- The structured practice sequences (the first offering's specific daily audio, the flagship's full curriculum)
- Live teaching, live community, live cohort experience
- The founder's direct attention (in the deep container)
- The accumulated library at any moment in time (free pieces are released as they are produced; the membership archive holds everything)

The free layer is not a tease. The free layer is the work. Practitioners who never enter the paid layer still get the recognition transmitted. The paid layer is for those who want structured practice, live community, and the founder's direct involvement.

## Pricing Discipline

The pricing across the ladder follows specific principles.

**No artificial scarcity.** The flagship cohort caps at the number it can serve well, not at an artificial number to create urgency. The deep container is capacity-bound by what the founder can hold, not by manufactured scarcity.

**No tripwires or upsells.** A practitioner who buys the first offering is not herded toward the flagship. The next layer is named honestly and offered without pressure when the practitioner is at the next moment.

**Honest exchange.** The price of each offering reflects what the offering is worth to do, not what the marketplace would accept for similar offerings. The first offering is not $9 because that is the tripwire price; it is $297-$497 because that is the price of doing 45 days of real work.

**No discounting after launch.** Each offering has a launch price. Once launched, the price holds. No "limited time" reductions. No "scarcity" sales. Pricing changes annually if the offering's value evolves, never as marketing leverage.

**Refunds honored.** Any practitioner who completes an offering and feels they did not receive what was promised is refunded without argument. The discipline is the work's integrity.

## What This Ladder Does Not Include

Decisions made:

- **No "free 5-day challenge" or similar acquisition funnels.** The free layer is the engine of trust, not a tripwire system.

- **No affiliate program.** Practitioners can mention the work freely; the work does not pay for referrals.
