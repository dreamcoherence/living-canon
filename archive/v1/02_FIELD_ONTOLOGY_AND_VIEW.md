# Field Ontology and View

**Version 1.2. May 2026. Working operational document. The view from which the work proceeds. The position-level formulation is held in `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md`. The subtle body dimension is articulated in `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`. This file is the practical articulation of the view that ties them together.**

---

## The Foundational View

Reality is not experienced as a dead material object.

Reality is experienced as dreamlike, symbolic, responsive, participatory, consciousness-saturated, shaped by attention, interpreted through identity, stabilized through the body, and disclosed through state.

Dream Coherence begins from the view that life is not merely something happening to the practitioner. Life is something the practitioner is participating in.

---

## The Dream Principle

The waking world and the sleeping dream are not identical, but they share deep structural similarities.

Both are experienced within consciousness. Both are shaped by attention. Both are interpreted symbolically. Both are colored by emotion. Both are organized through identity. Both are responsive to expectation. Both are full of recurring motifs. Both are saturated with meaning.

The aim is not to deny waking life. The aim is to become more lucid within it.

---

## The Screen Principle

All states appear within awareness.

Waking appears. Dream appears. Sleep appears as absence remembered. Emotion appears. Thought appears. Identity appears. The world appears.

The practitioner learns to notice the screen, not only the picture.

This gives freedom inside experience.

---

## The Identity Principle

Identity selects perception.

What one assumes oneself to be determines what is noticed, what is ignored, what feels possible, what feels threatening, what action feels natural, what life appears to confirm.

Identity is not only a belief. Identity is a lived configuration of body, attention, memory, emotion, imagination, and expectation.

---

## The Attention Principle

Attention is creative because it stabilizes worlds.

What attention repeatedly touches becomes more real in experience.

Attention is therefore the spiritual instrument, the nervous system signal, the creative power, the reality filter, and the dream-steering mechanism.

Untrained attention creates drift. Trained attention creates direction.

---

## The Subconscious Principle

The subconscious is not merely hidden memory. It is the active dream-engine beneath waking life.

It appears through nighttime dreams, emotional triggers, repeating relationship patterns, money patterns, self-sabotage, unexplained resistance, compulsive fantasy, bodily contraction, and symbolic recurrence.

Dream Coherence works by bringing the subconscious into conscious relationship.

---

## The Body Principle

The body is the vessel of dream participation.

A dysregulated body cannot reliably hold subtle perception, identity shift, creativity, prosperity, or awakening.

The body must feel safe enough to receive the dream.

Body coherence is not optional. It is the ground.

---

## The Five Layers Principle

The practitioner is more than the physical body. The work operates across five integrated layers:

**Physical.** The dense body. Sensation, posture, breath, the gross anatomy.
**Etheric.** The energy body. The life-force layer that interpenetrates the physical and gives it its livingness.
**Astral.** The dream body. The emotional and imaginal layer. Where dream, vision, and disciplined imagination operate. The most distinctive working territory of Dream Coherence.
**Mental.** The thinking body. Conceptual cognition, structured thought, articulation.
**Witness.** The awareness aware of all the other layers. The presence that does not change while the lower layers move.

The layers interpenetrate. The work is integration, not ascent. The protocol's stages calibrate to the layers. See `SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md` for the full articulation.

The astral body in particular is one of Dream Coherence's most distinctive working territories. Most contemporary contemplative work touches the physical and the witness layers but leaves the middle layers — etheric, astral, mental — underdeveloped. The work claims and works with this middle territory deliberately.

---

## The Symbol Principle

Reality communicates through pattern.

Dreams speak symbolically. Life speaks symbolically. The body speaks symbolically. Synchronicity speaks symbolically.

The task is not paranoid interpretation. The task is refined listening.

---

## The Participation Principle

Conscious participation in the dream is the alignment of identity, feeling, attention, imagination, action, timing, receptivity, and nervous system capacity.

The dream responds most clearly when the dreamer becomes coherent.

This is what the book voice carries with the precision Axiom 8 requires: the recognition transforms the relationship to the dreaming such that what becomes available, in lived experience, is the alignment of state with what the field is already disclosing as possible.

---

## The Superconscious Principle

What is called the subconscious and what is called the superconscious are not separate territories.

The subconscious holds unresolved pattern. The superconscious discloses higher pattern.

The practitioner learns to receive from both: to clear the lower pattern, to open to the higher pattern, to embody the integrated whole.

---

## The Two Pillars

The view above carries the work along two pillars, as named in `00_READ_ME_FIRST.md`.

**Pillar One. The Nature of Reality.** The view itself. How the field is structured. How awareness becomes form. How the practitioner perceives and operates.

**Pillar Two. The Nature of the Self.** The dharma. The particular gift. The individuated path. How the same one consciousness recognizes itself as this specific incarnation and cultivates the capacity to live its own most enlightened expression.

The view above is the foundation for both pillars. The pillars are how the view becomes practice.

---

## The Goal

The goal is not escape.

The goal is lucid participation.

To live as if the world is real enough to love, dreamlike enough to transform, sacred enough to steward, responsive enough to engage, mysterious enough to remain humble before.

---

## Working Definition

Dream Coherence is the practice of aligning the whole field of the human being so that waking life, dream life, identity, body, desire, and destiny begin moving in one direction.

This alignment is the brand's working definition. The book's deeper articulation in `14_POSITION_AND_LINEAGE_MAP.md` (the section *Dream Coherence as the Central Frame*) holds the recognition at the sovereign register: dream coherence as the alignment of the awareness in a life with the awareness that is dreaming the life.

The two articulations are the same recognition, calibrated to two registers.
