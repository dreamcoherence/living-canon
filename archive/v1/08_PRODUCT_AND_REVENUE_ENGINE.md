# Product and Revenue Engine

## Purpose

Dream Coherence must become economically powerful without cheapening the work.

The revenue engine should emerge from the method.

Do not sell vague transformation.

Sell specific thresholds.

## Commercial Position

Dream Coherence helps people sleep, dream, re-pattern identity, and live lucidly inside the field of life.

## Revenue Philosophy

Money is not separate from the dream.

Revenue is a sign that the work:
- solves real problems
- reaches real people
- creates real transformation
- carries enough value to be exchanged for

## Product Development Rule

No product should be built unless it answers:

1. What pain does this solve?
2. What transformation does this produce?
3. How will the buyer use it?
4. What will they experience in 7 days?
5. What will they experience in 30 days?
6. How does it connect to the larger method?
7. Can Claude help produce 80% of it?

## Commercial Red Team

Before launching any product:

1. Is the promise concrete?
2. Is the outcome believable?
3. Is the product easy to use?
4. Is the aesthetic excellent?
5. Is the product too broad?
6. Would someone pay for this today?
7. Does it create a next step?
8. Does it deepen the method?
9. Is it honest?
10. Would I be proud to sell it?
