# Dream Coherence. Founder Presence Protocol.

**Version 1.2. May 2026. Working Voice register. Supersedes v1.0. The discipline of when and how the founder appears.**

This file defines the relationship between the faceless brand and the founder's selective presence. The brand is the work. The founder is the steward. The two are not the same. This file holds the discipline that keeps them clearly distinguished.

---

## The Frame

Dream Coherence as a brand is faceless. This is locked in the brand guidelines and is non-negotiable at the brand level. The mark is the brand's face. The voice is the brand's signature. The work is the brand's identity.

The founder is a real person who is named on the book, who exists in the world, and whose lived practice is the deepest validation of what the work transmits. The founder appears at high-leverage moments where presence supports the work, and is otherwise private.

Faceless brand. Named founder. Selective appearance. Stewardship model.

---

## What the Founder Is

The founder is the source of the recognition the work transmits, the lived case study the work points to, the voice that calibrates the work's register, and the steward of the architecture as it grows.

The founder is not the brand. The brand existed before the founder appeared publicly and will continue if the founder ever steps back from public presence. The work is larger than any individual.

The founder is not the personality. There is no founder content stream parallel to the brand content stream. The founder does not have a personal social media presence built off the brand.

The founder is not the marketing engine. The work markets itself. The founder appears to add depth at moments where presence carries weight.

---

## When the Founder Appears

A small set of high-leverage moments. Each is named, scoped, and held to discipline.

### One. On the book.

The founder's name appears on the book as author. This is the primary public identification of the founder with the work. The book is the foundational artifact and the convention of authored books is that the author is named. The brand carries everything else; the book carries the founder's name.

Cover treatment per the brand guidelines: author name in Inter, 11-12pt, letter-spacing +4, all caps, in Moon on the Night ground. Subordinate to the title and the brand label, not centered as the primary identifier.

### Two. Opportunistic interviews.

When podcast and interview opportunities arrive that align with the work, the founder takes them. The architecture does not concentrate these into a launch window or treat them as a critical leverage moment. The work happens regardless.

The discipline of which interviews to take is in the *Decision Standard* section below. The default is to decline. Most opportunities do not meet the standard.

Aligned venues are serious contemplative and intellectual podcasts. Examples in the work's territory: serious long-form conversation venues with editorial seriousness, contemplative-tradition podcasts, intellectual deep-dive shows. Not mass-market shows. Not wellness shows. Not motivational-speaker venues.

When interviews land, they are honored. When they do not, the work continues.

### Three. The deep container.

When the high-end container (Layer Four of the offerings ladder) is running, the founder is the practitioner the participants engage directly. This is one of the few places where the founder's direct presence is the offering.

The container is small. Twenty to fifty practitioners at any time. Application-only. Selected by the founder's reading of who is ready (calibrated against `30_PRACTITIONER_PROFILE.md` and screened per `10_QUALITY_SAFETY_AND_RED_TEAMING.md`). The founder's involvement is real and sustained throughout the container.

### Four. The deepest correspondence.

The founder personally responds to a small set of correspondence each week. Newsletter subscribers who write substantive replies. Practitioners in the high-end container. Selected inbound from people whose work has affinity with the work. This correspondence is private, never reproduced publicly without explicit permission, and is the relational ground the work maintains with its deepest practitioners.

### Five. Strategic conversations.

The founder occasionally appears in strategic settings where presence is required. Negotiation with a publisher. Partnership conversations with serious institutions. Collaborations with adjacent practitioners whose work has affinity. These are private by default.

## The Discipline of Personal Story

The founder's life — partnership, fatherhood, dharma, individuation, lived practice — is the lived ground of the work. The recognition is not theoretical; it is lived in a specific life with specific relationships and specific responsibilities.

Personal story enters the work only when the lesson translates and the teaching becomes better as a result. The discipline:

**The default is privacy.** The brand's faceless register does not invite personal disclosure. Newsletter issues, social posts, audio pieces, and essays operate without biographical specifics about the founder unless the specific story carries the teaching.

**When personal story enters, it enters lightly and in service of the lesson.** A passage may reference parenting a small child, or being in a long partnership, or a moment in lived practice, when that reference is the most precise way to transmit a recognition. The reference does not become the subject of the piece. The recognition remains the subject.

**Names of people in the founder's life are protected.** Lalita and Satya are not named in any public-facing content. References, when made, use formulations like "my partner," "my child," "the small person living in my house." This protects their privacy and keeps the writing in the territory the work occupies.

**Specific events are protected by default.** A piece may use a parenting moment to transmit a recognition about identity, but the piece does not become a parenting memoir. A piece may reference a moment of relational difficulty to transmit something about the dream of the self, but the piece does not become a relationship essay.

**The filter is: would this still be in the piece if the founder were anonymous?** If yes, the personal reference is doing real work. If no, the reference is self-indulgent and gets cut.

**Self-realization is the work, not self-expression.** The founder's lived dimension serves the recognition. The recognition does not serve the founder's self-expression. When the filter is unclear, default to leaving the personal reference out.

This discipline runs alongside the faceless brand requirement. The brand stays faceless. The founder, when occasionally referenced through the personal-as-instructive lens, is referenced lightly and only in service of the work.

## When the Founder Does Not Appear

A discipline-clarifying list of what the founder does not do.

### Personal social media.

The founder does not have a personal X, Instagram, TikTok, or LinkedIn presence built off the brand. The founder may have private accounts under different names for personal use, kept separate from the brand entirely. There is no "founder content" on the brand surfaces.

### Day-to-day brand presence.

The brand voice is the brand's voice. The founder is not the voice. Newsletter issues, social posts, and other brand outputs are not signed by the founder, do not reference the founder, and do not carry the founder's biographical details — except in the carefully filtered way described in *The Discipline of Personal Story* above.

### Mass-market press.

The founder declines mass-market press appearances. Mainstream morning television. Lifestyle magazines. Daily news circuits. These do not serve the work and dilute the founder's presence.

### Sponsored content of any kind.

The founder does not endorse products, accept sponsored content, or appear in advertising for other brands. The founder's presence is reserved for the work.

### Reactionary content.

The founder does not respond publicly to critics, controversy, or trending discourse. The work speaks. The founder does not.

### Influencer collaborations.

The founder does not appear in collaboration posts, podcast swaps that are primarily promotional, or other influencer-economy patterns.

### Photography and video for the brand.

The brand's imagery is atmospheric, symbolic, faceless per the brand guidelines. The founder is not in brand photography. The founder's face does not appear on the website (except possibly a small author photo if a publisher requires it for the book). The brand visuals never picture the founder.

## How the Founder Is Named

Naming conventions:

- On the book: real name, as agreed.
- In bylines on long-form essays the founder personally writes: real name, where the founder's voice is the point. Most essays are published unsigned under "Dream Coherence" since they emerge from the brand voice work.
- In interview attributions: real name with affiliation to Dream Coherence.
- In direct correspondence: signed personally by the founder.
- In any introduction or third-party reference: real name with affiliation.

The founder does not adopt a pseudonym for the work. The faceless brand does not require an invented name for the founder; it requires that the founder not become the primary public surface of the work.

## The Author Photo Question

If the book is published traditionally, the publisher will likely request an author photo. Discipline:

- Author photo is optional. The founder may decline if the publisher allows it.
- If required, the photo is taken in a register aligned with the brand. Atmospheric, deeply lit, contemplative. Not headshot-on-white. Not professional-author-photo. Closer to the visual register of the brand guidelines.
- The photo appears only in publisher-controlled contexts (book jacket, publisher's author page, possibly book retailers). It does not appear on the Dream Coherence website. It is not used for the brand's own marketing.

If the book is self-published, no author photo is required and none should be made.

## Biographical Notes

When the founder requires a biographical note (for podcast intros, book back-matter, interview platforms), the note is calibrated to the brand voice and stays minimal.

**Short form (for podcast intros, social bios):**
> [Founder name] is the source of Dream Coherence, a contemplative protocol for sleep, lucidity, and reality alignment.

**Medium form (for interview platforms, book back-matter):**
> [Founder name] is the source of Dream Coherence, a contemplative protocol for sleep, lucidity, and reality alignment. The work draws on the convergence of Kashmir Shaivism, Dzogchen, Daoist contemplative tradition, Kabbalah, and contemporary cognitive science, distilled into a method for waking inside the dream of life.

**Long form (for substantive interview placements):**
> The medium form, plus 2-3 sentences about the lived ground of the work. Carefully calibrated to avoid biographical specifics that pull focus from the work. The founder is described by what the founder does, not by where the founder went to school or what the founder did before this.

The biographical notes do not include credentials, certifications, lineage claims, or self-aggrandizing language. The work is the credential.

## Decisions Held for Later

Several questions are deferred to specific moments when the answer becomes obvious.

**Whether to use a single chosen full name or initials.** Decided when the book moves to production. Some authors of contemplative work use initials (J.M. Coetzee model) to support a slight distance between personal life and public work. The founder decides at the time.

**Whether the founder ever does long-form video appearances.** Held until specific opportunities arise. If a high-trust long-form conversation venue arises, the founder may appear. Defaults to no unless the venue is at the register of the work.

**Whether the founder names the lineage of their own contemplative practice publicly.** Likely held private. The work draws from multiple streams; the founder's specific teachers, retreats, and influences are personal. May change if a public articulation serves the work at a specific moment.

**Whether the founder appears in person at retreats (if retreats enter the architecture).** Decided when retreats are scoped, with the discipline that founder presence at retreats is the highest-leverage in-person work and should be reserved for the deepest practitioners.

## The Decision Standard

When in doubt about whether the founder should appear in a specific context, ask:

- Does this serve the recognition the work transmits?
- Does this serve the field's growth at depth, not just at surface?
- Is the venue at the register of the work?
- Will this presence add weight or dilute presence?
- Will appearing here mean declining a more aligned opportunity later?

If the answers are yes / yes / yes / add weight / no, appear. If any answer is no, decline.

The default is to decline. Selective presence requires saying no more than yes.

## The Closing Frame

The founder is the source of the work and the steward of its arc. The founder is not the personality the work circulates around. The brand carries the work. The founder appears when presence matters and is private when presence does not matter. Personal life enters the work only when the teaching becomes better as a result and never as self-expression.

Held with discipline, this protocol protects both the work and the founder's life. The work grows on its own legs. The founder remains free to live the practice that the book transmits, which is the deepest contribution the founder can make.

The face stays the spiral. The voice stays the brand. The founder stays the steward.
