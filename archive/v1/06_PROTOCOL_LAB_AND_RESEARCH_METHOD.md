# Protocol Lab and Research Method

## Purpose

Dream Coherence must become a real method.

Not a vibe.

Not a set of beautiful ideas.

A method.

This file defines how the method is developed.

## The Core Research Question

How can a human being align waking life, sleeping dreams, subconscious patterning, identity, nervous system, imagination, and action so that life becomes more lucid, coherent, abundant, and dharmically directed?

## The Five Research Pillars

### 1. Phenomenology
What is directly experienced?

### 2. Practice
What was done?

### 3. Mechanism
Why might it work?

### 4. Outcome
What changed?

### 5. Translation
How can others use it?

## Method Development Loop

1. Practice
2. Record
3. Observe
4. Interpret
5. Hypothesize
6. Refine
7. Test again
8. Translate into teaching
9. Convert into product

## The Initial Protocol Architecture

### Stage 1: Body Coherence
Purpose:
Stabilize the vessel.

Practices:
- sleep rhythm
- breath
- body scan
- evening downshift
- food and light awareness
- reduced stimulation

Primary question:
Can the body hold the dream?

### Stage 2: Attention Coherence
Purpose:
Train lucidity.

Practices:
- witness practice
- two-screen awareness
- reality checks
- thought observation
- attention redirection

Primary question:
Can attention remain awake inside experience?

### Stage 3: Identity Coherence
Purpose:
Repattern the assumed self.

Practices:
- I am inquiry
- assumption practice
- revision
- future-state embodiment
- self-concept audit

Primary question:
What identity is dreaming this life?

### Stage 4: Dream Coherence
Purpose:
Bring sleep dreams into the path.

Practices:
- dream journaling
- dream incubation
- reality checks
- symbol tracking
- lucid dreaming preparation
- dream dialogue

Primary question:
What is the deeper dream showing?

### Stage 5: Reality Coherence
Purpose:
Live from the integrated field.

Practices:
- synchronicity tracking
- aligned action
- decision by body and field
- daily timeline selection
- artistic transmission
- wealth embodiment

Primary question:
How does life respond when the dreamer becomes coherent?

## Protocol Versioning

Use version numbers:

- v0.1: personal experiment
- v0.2: refined from first month
- v0.3: refined from dream data
- v0.4: early public beta
- v1.0: book-ready protocol

Every version must include:
- what changed
- why it changed
- what evidence supports it
- what remains uncertain

## Red Team Questions

Before adding any practice to the method, ask:

1. Is this safe?
2. Is this grounded?
3. Is this necessary?
4. Is this testable?
5. Is this original enough?
6. Is this useful enough?
7. Could this destabilize someone?
8. Does this require caveats?
9. Does this produce a clear outcome?
10. Does it belong in Dream Coherence?
