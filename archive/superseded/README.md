## What this repository is

This repository is the living operating system for Dream Coherence.

It is not merely storage. It is the project mind: the constitutional memory, canonical seed bank, lineage map, practice architecture, voice discipline, revenue engine, content engine, AI operating system, and execution dashboard that allow the work to compound without losing itself.

## What Dream Coherence is

Dream Coherence is a living philosophical school and practice ecology dedicated to the recognition that reality has the nature of a dream, and to cultivating the faculties through which human beings consciously participate in that dreaming.

It helps people live better lives by cultivating attunement, lucidity, orientation, and conscious participation in the dream of life. It serves the Golden Thread: how reality uniquely wishes to become itself through this life.

## Primary operating law

Every output becomes input.

Every session should produce:

- a concrete deliverable,
- a repository update,
- a new recognition or refinement,
- an open question if something remains unresolved,
- a next action that reduces future founder work.

## Founder time standard

The system is designed to move toward a founder workload of 4 to 8 hours per week after the initial sprint.

The founder supplies recognition, taste, lived experience, dreams, final judgment, and strategic approval.

AI supplies synthesis, drafting, organization, multiplication, scheduling logic, funnel design, copy, review, and operational continuity.
