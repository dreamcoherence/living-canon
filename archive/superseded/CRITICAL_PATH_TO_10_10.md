# Critical Path to 10/10 and $20k/month

## Objective

Publicly launch Dream Coherence, build an owned audience, create trust, launch the Founding Cohort on 10/10, and grow into $20k+/month through repeatable and recurring offerings.

## The simplest path

1. Create public field.
2. Publish recognition content.
3. Capture email.
4. Give free practice.
5. Open waitlist.
6. Host free live activation.
7. Launch Founding Cohort on 10/10.
8. Collect testimonials.
9. Convert into evergreen First Threshold.
10. Open recurring membership.

## What must be true by launch

- Website is live and clear.
- Lead magnet is live.
- Newsletter is active.
- Offer page is ready.
- Founding Cohort curriculum is ready enough.
- Payment system works.
- Email sequence works.
- Content engine is publishing consistently.
- At least one free activation has occurred.
- Waitlist exists.

## $20k/month route

### Launch target

40 students at $497 = $19,880.

This proves the field can sell.

### Recurring target

Membership after cohort:

300 members at $67/month = $20,100/month.

Or hybrid stack:

- 150 members at $67 = $10,050.
- 20 evergreen course sales at $497 = $9,940.
- Total = $19,990.

## Founder leverage

Founder does:

- weekly recognition seed,
- one founder letter or audio,
- one live session or recorded teaching,
- final approval.

AI does:

- copy,
- content multiplication,
- email sequences,
- lesson outlines,
- landing pages,
- repurposing,
- reporting,
- repository updates.

## What to ignore until after launch

- perfect full book,
- many products,
- complex community platform,
- expensive branding overhaul,
- paid ads before organic proof,
- elaborate automation before funnel works manually.
