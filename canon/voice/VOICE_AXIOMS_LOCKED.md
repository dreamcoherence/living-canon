# Voice Axioms Locked

## Purpose

Preserve the V1 voice laws as binding standards for Sovereign Voice and deep public transmissions.

These are not style preferences. They are transmission laws.

## Axiom 1: Present tense

The recognition is occurring now.

Do not throw the reader into historical distance unless historically necessary.

## Axiom 2: No em dashes

Use periods, commas, colons, semicolons where structurally justified.

Default to periods.

## Axiom 3: Positive over negative when structurally honest

Orient toward what is.

Use negation only when intercepting a predictable misreading.

## Axiom 4: No presumed reader situation

Do not presume posture, room, page, or context unless universally present.

## Axiom 5: No materialist setup

Do not grant materialism the opening frame when dissolving that frame.

Begin from awareness.

## Axiom 6: No redundancy

No sentence restates what is already established.

## Axiom 7: Forward motion

Every sentence advances recognition, rhythm, structure, or practice.

## Axiom 8: Epistemic precision

Every definitive claim must be true from the position the work takes.

Wager what must be wagered.
Verify what can be verified.
Do not inflate claims.

## Axiom 9: Voice architecture

Default ground: Zen clarity.

Use cathedral tools sparingly:

- Mann patient sentence
- DFW recursion
- Rilke immense plain claim
- Rumi turn

## Axiom 10: Suchness addressing itself

The prose speaks from inside the recognition.

It does not explain spirituality to a reader.

It is awareness recognizing itself as language.

## Axiom 11: No positionality

Avoid speaking from one position down to another.

The pointing, pointed-at, and experience of pointing are one act.

## Axiom 12: Factual rigor

No guessing in claims that can be checked.

Translations, etymologies, dates, lineage claims, and quotations must be accurate.

## Axiom 13: Tic test

No word, phrase, image, or rhythmic move appears repeatedly unless each appearance carries distinct work.

## Axiom 14: No slop

Cut vague intensifiers, decorative phrases, and rhythmic flourishes that signal depth without carrying it.

## Test

A passage that violates an axiom is rebuilt, not defended.
