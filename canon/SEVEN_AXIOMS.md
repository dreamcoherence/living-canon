# The Seven Axioms of Dream Coherence

These are not values.

They are generative first principles. Everything downstream should be derivable from them.

## I. Reality

Reality is participatory. Experience has the nature of a dream, and every life is one way reality comes to know, express, and become itself.

## II. Perception

Perception is participation. As seeing changes, the world as it is lived changes with it.

This is stronger than saying recognition precedes transformation. It names the actual mechanism. A life is not transformed after perception changes, as if perception were a preliminary step. The transformation is already occurring in the changed perception, because the world that is lived is the world as perceived, interpreted, embodied, and acted from.

## III. The Human Faculty

Wisdom is not accumulated. It is cultivated.

Dream Coherence exists not to install beliefs, but to awaken and refine dormant faculties through which reality can be encountered directly: attunement, lucidity, imagination, discernment, intuition, beauty, authorship, surrender, and participation.

## IV. The Work

Every offering should deepen the practitioner's capacity to participate consciously in life.

Lucidity is not the whole of the work. Lucidity is the doorway. Participation is the life that opens beyond it.

## V. Stewardship

This work is not manufactured. It is stewarded.

The task is to remain faithful to the deepest recognition presently available, allowing the work to reveal its own architecture rather than forcing it into a pre-existing form.

## VI. Evolution

Everything in Dream Coherence emanates from the deepest recognition presently available.

As recognition deepens, every expression derived from it is invited to deepen in turn.

The organism remains faithful not to its previous forms, but to the living truth from which those forms arose.

## VII. Reciprocity

Reality flowers through reciprocity.

As recognition becomes participation, participation can become contribution, contribution flourishing, and flourishing the freedom for deeper recognition.

The healthiest organism continually gives rise to the conditions for its own further unfolding.

This is where prosperity belongs. Not as promise. Not as bait. As one expression of reciprocity becoming stable enough to nourish the work that nourishes others.
