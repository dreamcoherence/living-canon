# Aesthetic System

## Desired feeling

Deeply alive. Enchanted. Present. Oriented. Aligned. Attuned. Grounded. Centered. Rooted. Elevated. Infinite. Whole. Embodied. Ensouled. Integrated. Well. Flourishing. Flowing. Divine.

## Visual language

Use:

- stone
- wood
- water
- fire
- morning light
- dusk
- linen
- fog
- hands
- paths
- thresholds
- simple architecture
- temples without cliché
- dreamlike ordinary scenes
- natural texture

Avoid:

- AI slop
- generic galaxies
- neon mysticism
- cheap mandalas
- overused spiritual stock imagery
- corporate gradients
- hustle aesthetics

## Design standard

Dream Coherence should feel discovered rather than designed.

Ancient and new.

Minimal but warm.

Mystical but grounded.

Premium but not sterile.

Beautiful without trying to look expensive.
