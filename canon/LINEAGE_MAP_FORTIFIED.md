# Lineage Map Fortified

## Purpose

This file preserves the depth of V1's lineage work while giving V2 a cleaner operational map.

Dream Coherence does not claim authority from any lineage.

It reads, honors, synthesizes, translates, tests, and practices.

## The four circles

### Circle One: The Spine

Foundational ground without which Dream Coherence is not itself.

- Kashmir Shaivism: awareness as self-expression, spanda, pratyabhijna, world affirmation.
- Dzogchen and Bön: rigpa, natural light, dream yoga, deep sleep, bardo, clear light.
- Daoist internal alchemy: body as vessel, refinement, qi, jing, shen, wu wei.
- Kabbalah: emanation, Ein Sof, sefirot, tikkun, world as unfinished divine expression.
- Nahua philosophy: teotl, nepantla, flower and song, reality in motion.
- Founder lived case study: the contemporary life in which the synthesis is tested.

### Circle Two: Primary Methodological Inheritance

The practical and developmental methods that shape Dream Coherence.

- Jung: individuation, Self, dream, archetype, active imagination, shadow.
- Steiner: fourfold human, etheric/astral/I, higher cognition, discerned carefully.
- Gurdjieff: self-remembering, three centers, mechanical consciousness, Fourth Way.
- Aurobindo and the Mother: integral yoga, supramental, descent, transformation of matter.
- Hermetic tradition: correspondence, alchemy, inner operations, microcosm and macrocosm.
- Imaginal tradition: Corbin, Ibn Arabi, Hillman, mundus imaginalis, imagination as organ.
- Tibetan dream yoga: dream recall, lucidity, dream body, clear light, bardo.

### Circle Four: Background Witness

Broader traditions and philosophical ground.

- Bergson

- mythological and folkloric traditions

## Discernment discipline

What is held:

- recognitions that survive practice,
- methodologies that can be tested,
- symbolic forms that illuminate,
- lineage insights that deepen the work.

What is filtered:

- sectarian apparatus where it constrains,
- period-bound claims,
- personality cult structures,
- racial/evolutionary claims that have aged badly,
- ritual forms requiring initiation,
- false provenance,
- New Age dilution.

## Operational register

Dream Coherence should operate at the register of Jung, Steiner, Gurdjieff, Aurobindo, Dzogchen, Kashmir Shaivism, Hermeticism, and the imaginal tradition, in a contemporary form.

When work falls below this register, rebuild it.
