# Lineage Map Fortified

## Purpose

This file preserves the depth of V1's lineage work while giving V2 a cleaner operational map.

Dream Coherence does not claim authority from any lineage.

It reads, honors, synthesizes, translates, tests, and practices.

## Governing principle: convergence without equivalence

Dream Coherence learns from traditions without borrowing their authority. Convergence does not erase difference.

Traditions may illuminate similar experiential territory while differing in ontology, method, soteriology, history, sacred language, and authority. Resonance permits comparison. It does not establish identity.

Dream Coherence does not describe itself as taking "the best of every tradition." That formulation flattens traditions and implicitly places Dream Coherence above them.

Its originality lies in arrangement and synthesis: the Ground, Current, Communion framework; continuity across waking, dreaming, and sleep; the Golden Thread; personhood without enclosure; participation without omnipotence; contemporary ethical and relational safeguards; the public-to-course compression architecture; and living application to creative, relational, material, and ordinary life.

World affirmation and re-entry (the Turn) are Dream Coherence's emphasis and synthesis, not an exclusive invention. Several inherited traditions, nondual Shaivism and Aurobindo among them, are themselves world-affirming.

Lineage may remain backstage in primary prose, but the research layer preserves attribution, historical distinction, evidence register, and limits. Lineage-specific terms, quotations, and page references live in `research/SOURCE_NOTES/`.

The locked recognition "The lineages are prisms. The light is the same." is held in the poetic register. It is not an evidentiary claim of doctrinal sameness and is never cited as one.

## The four circles

Status of each entry after the 2026-10-23 integration is marked. "Active" means structurally load-bearing. "Contextual" means held but not currently load-bearing. "Source note" means a note exists or is required.

### Circle One: The Spine

Foundational ground without which Dream Coherence is not itself.

- Nondual Shaiva Tantra (Kashmir): awareness as self-expression, prakasha and vimarsha, spanda, pratyabhijna, world affirmation, contraction and particularity, iccha-jnana-kriya, grace and means. Active. Strongest current backstage grammar for The First Threshold. Whether it becomes Dream Coherence's governing ontology is OPEN and requires founder decision. Source notes: `KASHMIR_SHAIVISM_TERMS_AND_SCHOOLS.md`, `DYCZKOWSKI_DOCTRINE_OF_VIBRATION.md`.
- Dzogchen and Bön: rigpa, natural light, dream yoga, deep sleep, bardo, clear light. Active. Divergence held: the tradition recognizes the dream to wake from it; Dream Coherence recognizes it to author it on purpose. Same instrument, opposite telos. Source note required (Tenzin Wangyal synthesis exists).
- Daoist internal alchemy: body as vessel, refinement, qi, jing, shen, wu wei, timing, yielding as intelligence. Active. Source note: `DAOIST_ORIENTATION_AND_INTERNAL_ALCHEMY.md`.
- Kabbalah: emanation, Ein Sof, sefirot, tikkun, world as unfinished divine expression. Contextual. Source note required before further use.
- Nahua philosophy: teotl, nepantla, flower and song, reality in motion. Contextual pending audit. Routed through the Indigenous living-tradition audit (`research/BOUNDARIES/INDIGENOUS_WITNESS_AND_LANGUAGE.md`).
- Founder lived case study: the contemporary life in which the synthesis is tested. Active.

### Circle Two: Primary Methodological Inheritance

The practical and developmental methods that shape Dream Coherence.

- Jung: individuation, Self, dream, archetype, active imagination, shadow. Active. Divergence held: Jung's "dream" is the unconscious state one awakens from; Dream Coherence's "dream" is the affirmed fabric of experience.
- Steiner: fourfold human, etheric/astral/I, higher cognition, discerned carefully. Contextual.
- Gurdjieff: self-remembering, automaticity, integration of thought, feeling, body, and action. Active as attentional methodology, not ontology. Source note: `GURDJIEFF_SELF_REMEMBERING.md`.
- Aurobindo and the Mother: integral yoga, supramental, descent, transformation of matter. Contextual.
- Hermetic tradition, including Meditations on the Tarot: correspondence, analogy as method, symbol as operation, concentration without force, effort and grace, love above power. Active. Source note: `MEDITATIONS_ON_THE_TAROT.md`.
- Imaginal tradition: Corbin, Ibn Arabi, Hillman, mundus imaginalis, imagination as organ, relational nonduality. Active. Source note: `CORBIN_ALONE_WITH_THE_ALONE.md`.
- Tibetan dream yoga: dream recall, lucidity, dream body, clear light, bardo. Active.

## Required cautions

- In classical Advaita, the identity of Atman and Brahman is recognized as already the case. Atman does not become or attain Brahman.
- Advaita and nondual Shaivism are not collapsed. Their world, power, manifestation, and liberation grammars differ.
- Shiva, Dao, God, Brahman, rigpa, Great Spirit, and the imaginal are not interchangeable names for one generic substance.
- Generic "Great Spirit" language is not used.
- Jivanmukti varies across traditions and is never a product guarantee.
- Kashmir Shaivism is an umbrella. Spanda, Pratyabhijna, Trika, Krama, and Kaula are not interchangeable.
- Daoist, Indic, and Tibetan subtle-body maps are not anatomically identical because they resonate.
- Public language may be native to Dream Coherence while research language preserves source and limit.

## Public naming constraint

Nondual Shaiva integration carries a hard constraint: no net increase in Sanskrit terms in public prose, no increase in Shaiva naming density in the Chapter 2 prism array, and nothing extracted that cannot be rendered in the existing Lexicon. Structural sourcing only.

## Discernment discipline

What is held:

- recognitions that survive practice,
- methodologies that can be tested,
- symbolic forms that illuminate,
- lineage insights that deepen the work.

What is filtered:

- sectarian apparatus where it constrains,
- period-bound claims,
- personality cult structures,
- racial/evolutionary claims that have aged badly,
- ritual forms requiring initiation,
- false provenance,
- New Age dilution,
- divine names strung together to manufacture universality.

## Operational register

Dream Coherence should operate at the register of Jung, Steiner, Gurdjieff, Aurobindo, Dzogchen, Kashmir Shaivism, Hermeticism, and the imaginal tradition, in a contemporary form.

When work falls below this register, rebuild it.
