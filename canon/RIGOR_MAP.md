## 1. Awareness is fundamental

- **Register:** Metaphysical wager.
- **Strongest counterargument:** Every observation of awareness is made from within awareness, so its apparent primacy is a perspectival artifact. Neuroscience correlates every studied change in experience with physical change, and physicalism or neutral monism organize the same data without the extra posit.
- **Support:** Immediacy of experience; the hard problem; convergent contemplative reports; parsimony arguments (Kastrup) that are contested.
- **Where it breaks:** When presented as proven by phenomenology, by dreams, or by cross-traditional convergence.
- **Confidence:** Held as wager. Not assignable as probability.
- **Refined claim:** Experience is immediately known; that awareness is fundamental is Dream Coherence's explicit wager.
- **Ethics and safety:** Low direct risk. Indirect risk when the wager is used to dismiss medical, material, or social realities.
- **Permitted public language:** "The wager this work makes." Invitation to experiment.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Science shows consciousness is fundamental." "Your experience proves it."
- **Route:** Canon (Epistemology, Ontology), stated as wager.

## 2. Awareness is intrinsically self-knowing

- **Register:** Lineage-supported metaphysical formulation (Shaiva prakasha and vimarsha).
- **Strongest counterargument:** Reflexive awareness may be a cognitive construction; higher-order theories of consciousness explain apparent self-knowing without intrinsic reflexivity. Buddhist schools disagree among themselves on reflexive awareness (svasamvedana).
- **Support:** Phenomenological immediacy (experience is not absent to the one living it); Shaiva doctrine; some Dzogchen formulations.
- **Where it breaks:** When the minimal phenomenological point is inflated into the full metaphysical doctrine.
- **Confidence:** Moderate for the minimal phenomenological point; wager for the doctrine.
- **Refined claim:** Awareness is not only where experience appears; in this work's lineage-supported formulation, it is the living knowing of appearance.
- **Ethics and safety:** Low.
- **Permitted public language:** Direct pointing to the knownness of experience.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Awareness knows itself, therefore you are God."
- **Route:** Canon (Ground, Current, Communion), attributed in source notes.

## 3. Awareness is dynamic and self-expressive

- **Register:** Lineage-supported metaphysical wager (spanda).
- **Strongest counterargument:** Change in experience is fully explained by change in what is experienced; nothing licenses attributing dynamism to awareness itself.
- **Support:** Phenomenology of arising and subsiding; spanda doctrine; Nahua teotl as structural parallel (not equivalent).
- **Where it breaks:** When dynamism is described as frequency, vibration rank, or physical energy.
- **Confidence:** Wager.
- **Refined claim:** The Ground is not opposed to movement. Current is the Ground differentiating as this moment.
- **Ethics and safety:** Moderate. Vibration language slides toward New Thought causation and moral ranking.
- **Permitted public language:** Living movement, pulse, arising.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "High vibration," "frequency," "quantum," "energy attracts."
- **Route:** Canon (Ground, Current, Communion). Source note required.

## 4. Reality is participatory while exceeding every standpoint

- **Register:** Phenomenological finding (participation in lived experience) plus metaphysical wager (participation all the way down).
- **Strongest counterargument:** Participatory language either reduces to the trivial (actions have effects, perception is interpretive) or inflates to magical thinking. There is no stable middle.
- **Support:** Enactivism (Thompson, Varela); predictive processing; lived evidence that interpretation shapes action and relationship; Foundation's own guard ("no private conjuring").
- **Where it breaks:** When attention or identity is treated as sole cause of events.
- **Confidence:** High for the phenomenological layer. Wager for the metaphysical layer.
- **Refined claim:** Participation is agency in relation, not control. Shared reality exceeds every single standpoint.
- **Ethics and safety:** High. Without the excess clause it becomes victim-blaming.
- **Permitted public language:** "Participates in," "helps give form."
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Creates your reality," "the universe answers your thoughts."
- **Route:** Canon (Ontology, Living Pattern). Seven Axioms II amended 2026-10-23 to "the world as it is lived" (D3s); Seven Axioms VII amended to "can become" (D4s).

## 5. Waking and dreaming share structural features

- **Register:** Phenomenological finding.
- **Strongest counterargument:** The differences are decisive: waking is stable, shared, public, and corrigible; dreams are private and unstable. Shared features hold of any experience and say nothing specific.
- **Support:** Both appear in awareness, are shaped by attention and identity, carry emotional weight, and are not recognized as constructed from within. Lucid dreaming research supports trainability of recognition.
- **Where it breaks:** When the analogy is used as proof of nonduality or idealism.
- **Confidence:** High for the listed features.
- **Refined claim:** The dream supplies analogy and experimental field, not proof.
- **Ethics and safety:** Moderate. Derealization risk if waking is described as unreal.
- **Permitted public language:** "Waking arrives the way a dream arrives." Dream does not mean unreal.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "The dream proves the world is in your mind."
- **Route:** Canon (Ontology, Epistemology).

## 6. Identity participates in shaping perception

- **Register:** Phenomenological finding, partly scientifically supported.
- **Strongest counterargument:** Top-down effects of belief on perception are smaller and more contested than popular accounts claim; much perception is cognitively impenetrable.
- **Support:** Attention and expectation research; self-concept and salience research; contemplative report.
- **Where it breaks:** "Identity selects perception" overclaims. Unilateral determination is not supported.
- **Confidence:** Moderate to high for "participates." Low for "selects."
- **Refined claim:** Identity participates in shaping perception. It does not unilaterally select or determine it.
- **Ethics and safety:** Moderate. Overclaiming invites self-blame.
- **Permitted public language:** "Shapes what is noticed."
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Change your identity and the world changes."
- **Route:** Canon (Ontology, Lexicon). Replaces "identity selects perception."

## 7. Coherence may increase agency without granting control

- **Register:** Practice-testable.
- **Strongest counterargument:** "Coherence" is unmeasured; any improvement can be credited to it and any failure blamed on its lack, making the claim unfalsifiable.
- **Support:** Practitioner reports; regulation and executive-function research where analogous; founder case study.
- **Where it breaks:** When lack of outcome is read as lack of coherence.
- **Confidence:** Moderate, pending operational markers.
- **Refined claim:** Greater coherence may increase freedom of response, sensitivity to consequence, and capacity to receive correction. It does not guarantee outcomes.
- **Ethics and safety:** High. Coherence must never read illness, poverty, or trauma as incoherence.
- **Permitted public language:** "May increase," named capacities.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Coherent people attract success."
- **Route:** Canon, plus Protocol Lab: define observable markers before course launch.

## 8. Imagination can function as a faculty of participation

- **Register:** Phenomenological finding plus lineage-supported (Corbin, Jung).
- **Strongest counterargument:** Imagination is simulation; calling it disclosure licenses treating fantasy as revelation.
- **Support:** Rehearsal, mental imagery, and creative research; active imagination; the imaginal as a disciplined middle position.
- **Where it breaks:** When images are treated as evidence of external beings, events, or other minds.
- **Confidence:** Moderate.
- **Refined claim:** Imagination is a faculty of participation. Its disclosures become trustworthy through embodiment, ethics, relationship, consequence, and time.
- **Ethics and safety:** Moderate. Psychosis-adjacent risk with literalized imagery.
- **Permitted public language:** "An image can be more than decoration without becoming proof."
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Your visions are messages," "the astral shows you the truth about others."
- **Route:** Canon (Ontology), Practice, Safety.

## 9. The Golden Thread is a legitimate teleological inquiry

- **Register:** Teleological or poetic disclosure.
- **Strongest counterargument:** Asking what reality wants from a life presupposes reality has wants, invites grandiosity, and turns ordinary preference into destiny.
- **Support:** The question's orienting value; vocation literatures; the discernment tests that constrain answers.
- **Where it breaks:** When any answer is treated as certain, special, or binding on others.
- **Confidence:** High as inquiry. Not applicable as fact.
- **Refined claim:** The question is held as inquiry. Answers are tested through time, embodiment, ethics, capacity, relationship, material reality, and correction.
- **Ethics and safety:** High. Grandiosity and mania risk.
- **Permitted public language:** The central question, plainly.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Your destiny," "your highest timeline," "you were chosen."
- **Route:** Canon (Golden Thread), Passage VII, Safety.

## 10. Liberation while living is a meaningful horizon, not a promised outcome

- **Register:** Lineage-supported horizon.
- **Strongest counterargument:** Jivanmukti means different things across Advaita, Shaiva, and other traditions, and borrowing the term without its soteriology empties it. Offering it alongside a paid course implies sale.
- **Support:** Multiple traditions' accounts; the work's orientation toward integration rather than escape.
- **Where it breaks:** When implied as attainable by purchase, certifiable, or permanent.
- **Confidence:** High as horizon framing.
- **Refined claim:** Liberation is a horizon and orientation. No product certifies or confers it.
- **Ethics and safety:** High. Consumer and spiritual-authority risk.
- **Permitted public language:** Freedom that remains present through ordinary life.
- **Prohibited inflation:** "Become liberated in eight weeks."
- **Route:** Canon (Epistemology), Passage VIII, Product files.
