# Ontology

## Foundational view

Reality is not encountered as a dead material object.

Reality is encountered as experience: symbolic, responsive, participatory, consciousness-saturated, shaped by attention, interpreted through identity, stabilized through the body, and disclosed through state.

Dream Coherence begins from the view that life is not merely happening to the practitioner. Life is something the practitioner is participating in.

The register of this view: that experience is lived from within is immediate fact; that reality is consciousness-saturated is Dream Coherence's explicit metaphysical wager. See `canon/EPISTEMOLOGY_AND_RIGOR.md`.

## Dream principle

Dream does not mean unreal.

Dream means a world known from within, present as experience, patterned, state-dependent, symbolically expressive, and inseparable from the knowing through which it is lived.

The waking world and sleeping dream are not identical, but they share structural features:

- both appear within awareness,
- both are shaped by attention,
- both are interpreted symbolically,
- both are colored by emotion,
- both are organized through identity,
- both contain recurring motifs,
- both become more workable when recognized as dreamlike.

The waking world is not equated simplistically with a sleeping dream. It is shared, lawful, materially consequential, persistent, and corrigible through other people, records, resistant conditions, and consequences that private conviction cannot settle.

The dream supplies analogy and an experimental field. It does not prove waking idealism.

## Awareness principle

All states appear within awareness.

Waking appears.
Dream appears.
Sleep appears as remembered absence or clear presence.
Emotion appears.
Thought appears.
Identity appears.
World appears.

The practitioner learns to recognize the field in which the state appears, not only the state itself.

Awareness is not only where experience appears. In the work's lineage-supported formulation, it is the living knowing of appearance. See `canon/GROUND_CURRENT_COMMUNION.md`.

## The lexicon of selfhood

Four terms are kept distinct.

### Standpoint

The irreducible first-person orientation from which experience is lived. Prefer standpoint, orientation, or where experience is met. Do not describe it as an inner object, homunculus, sovereign controller, or point behind the eyes.

### Identity

The changing organization of body, memory, interpretation, expectation, affiliation, imagination, protection, and response.

### Person

The embodied, relational continuity through which experience is lived and answered for. The person acts, promises, suffers, loves, remembers, repairs, and remains responsible.

### Ground

Awareness as fundamental, held as Dream Coherence's explicit metaphysical wager.

### Formulations

- A person is a real, embodied, relational continuity through which experience is lived and answered for. Identity is the changing organization through which that person meets the world.
- A standpoint is particular. It is not the measure of everything that appears.
- The person is real and particular, but not the final boundary of experience.
- Awakening loosens enclosure, not personhood.
- Particularity is not separation. Identity becomes binding when a partial organization of experience is mistaken for the whole of what one is.

## Identity principle

Identity participates in shaping perception.

What one assumes oneself to be influences what is noticed, ignored, attempted, feared, and found natural. It does not unilaterally select or determine what appears.

Identity is not merely belief. It is a configuration of body, attention, memory, emotion, imagination, expectation, and action.

Identity is not a false mask to destroy. Within recognition it can be carried as something stewarded: exact without needing to become absolute, functional and revisable, more coherent, less defended, more reality-tested.

## Body principle

The body is the immediate condition and instrument of human participation. Embodied regulation, relationship, environment, and support materially affect how safely and sustainably subtle experience can be integrated.

Ground is reserved for awareness. The body is not called the ground, and no claim is made that a dysregulated person cannot practice or is spiritually less capable. Embodiment includes dependence, co-regulation, disability, health, social conditions, and access to support.

## Shared world and other beings

The dream is not privately mine. Other beings are not symbols produced for my development. Whatever reality's ultimate unity, no individual perspective possesses the whole. Other lives have their own inwardness, freedom, and claim upon conduct.

No individual mind privately invents the mountains, the laws, another person, illness, injustice, or the full field of events. Attention participates in lived experience. It does not command the whole.

Solipsism is a failure state, not an implication.

## Agency without omnipotence

Participation is agency in relation, not control.

Life arrives through conditions, inheritances, encounters, bodies, histories, and possibilities we did not choose. Within them, attention, imagination, skill, devotion, decision, and action participate in what becomes possible. We receive the field and help give it form.

Authorship is real when it includes inherited conditions, other agents, limitation, lawful consequences, accident, history, culture, body, power, access, and material reality.

Greater coherence may increase freedom of response, clarity of intention, sensitivity to consequence, relational skill, the ability to sustain work, discernment among impulses, strategic effectiveness, and the capacity to receive correction.

It does not imply that every event was personally chosen, that desire determines outcomes, that suffering reflects spiritual failure, that the local self possesses universal agency, that anyone can know another mind without evidence, or that correct consciousness guarantees wealth, health, safety, love, or success.

## Suffering boundary

To understand contraction as a power of consciousness is not to call every form of suffering desirable, deserved, chosen, or spiritually necessary. Sacredness does not erase tragedy, consent, responsibility, power, contingency, or the obligation to reduce harm.

## Imagination, symbol, and state

- Imagination is a faculty of participation. Its disclosures become trustworthy through embodiment, ethics, relationship, consequence, and time.
- A symbol is not evidence of an external event. It is a form in which experience gathers meaning and invites relationship, discernment, and response.
- State participates in what is noticed, interpreted, attempted, and enacted. It does not make a person the sole cause of what occurs.
- Correspondence can disclose structural resonance. It does not prove identity, causation, or prediction.

## Surrender, effortlessness, and the vessel

- Effortlessness is the fruit of attention, training, and attunement. It is not exemption from labor or consequence.
- Surrender releases exclusive authorship. It does not surrender discernment, consent, responsibility, or care.
- To become a vessel is to become more available to life and service while remaining accountable for every interpretation and act.

## Five working registers

Dream Coherence works across five lineage-informed working registers:

1. Physical: sensation, posture, breath, sleep, movement.
2. Etheric: vitality, felt aliveness.
3. Astral: dream, emotion, symbol, vision, imagination.
4. Mental: thought, meaning, belief, interpretation, articulation.
5. Witness/Causal: awareness as it knows every register.

These are working registers, not separate proven substances, a spiritual rank, or a ladder with a detached Witness above the rest. Awareness is not a detached object above the other registers. See `practice/SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`.

The astral register is one of Dream Coherence's most distinctive working territories. It is where dream, vision, emotion, symbol, and disciplined imagination operate. It does not justify privileged knowledge of other minds or external events.

## Goal

The goal is not escape.

The goal is lucid participation.

To live as if the world is real enough to love, consequential enough to answer for, dreamlike enough to transform, sacred enough to steward, responsive enough to engage, and mysterious enough to remain humble before.
