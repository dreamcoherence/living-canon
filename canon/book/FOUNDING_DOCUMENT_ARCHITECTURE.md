# Founding Document Architecture

## Status (2026-10-23)

Historical brief. The founding document it specifies was realized as Foundation (`canon/FOUNDATION_ESSAY_I_LOCKED.md`, locked). Founder confirmed 2026-10-23: the series is Foundation followed by the eight Passages; there is no separate Foundation Arc.

Foundation is not rewritten by this update. A separate Foundation alignment audit is required (see `DECISION_LOG.md`).

## Purpose

The founding document opens the field.

It does not need to contain everything.

It must make Dream Coherence unmistakable.

## Required effect

After reading, someone should feel:

- oriented,
- awake,
- curious,
- inwardly stilled,
- drawn toward practice,
- trusting of the field,
- willing to join the first threshold.

## Recommended form

Shorter than the full Path of Clear Light.

Long enough to establish:

1. Reality as dreamlike.
2. Attunement as faculty.
3. Golden Thread as central inquiry.
4. Day/night continuity.
5. Practice as threshold.
6. Participation as authorship, where authorship is agency in relation, not control.
7. Invitation into First Threshold.

## Relation to the Passages

Foundation establishes the ground. The Passages that follow build from it. They may invoke its premises, but they do not reperform its argument or reuse its signature imagery as their principal material.

## Relation to course

The founding document leads naturally into the First Threshold container.

It is the field-opening artifact.

The course is the structured crossing.
