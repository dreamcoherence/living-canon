## Nomenclature note

Series nomenclature does not rename the internal atomic recognition category. The First Threshold's works are Passages. `RECOGNITIONS.md` remains the seed bank, and "recognition" remains the inner event of seeing.

## Promotion rule

A recognition becomes canon when it survives:

- voice test,
- rigor test,
- usefulness test,
- beauty test,
- practice test,
- founder resonance test,
- epistemic-register test: its register is named and no transition is concealed,
- alterity and shared-world test: it does not reduce other beings to symbols or imply private authorship of shared reality,
- safety test,
- lineage and provenance test: inherited content is attributable,
- correction test: it names what would weaken it.

Do not promote a sentence merely because it is beautiful.

## Changes to locked text

Locked files change only by Continuity Protocol: P# · old → new · rationale, ruled by founder letter (a / r / s). A flag quotes its exact target; unflagged words in the same sentence remain locked.
