# Dream Coherence Constitution

## Purpose

The Constitution protects Dream Coherence from dilution, entropy, cheap commercialization, spiritual inflation, aesthetic drift, and operational confusion.

It defines what the project is, what it serves, what it must never become, and how it evolves.

## Definition

Dream Coherence is a living philosophical school and practice ecology dedicated to the recognition that reality has the nature of a dream, and to cultivating the faculties through which human beings consciously participate in that dreaming.

## Public aim

To help people live better lives by recognizing the dreamlike nature of experience, cultivating attunement, discerning their Golden Thread, and participating in life with greater lucidity, beauty, grace, and effectiveness.

## Internal aim

To build a noble, true, beautiful, useful, commercially powerful body of work that generates substantial recurring income while preserving the purity of the offering.

## The Golden Thread

> How does reality uniquely wish to become itself through this life?

This is the central inquiry of the work.

## What Dream Coherence must always be

- true
- wise
- useful
- beautiful
- virtuous
- effective
- distinct
- novel
- grounded
- enchanted
- nondual
- alchemical
- elegant
- alive
- commercially intelligent without becoming commercially corrupt

## What Dream Coherence must never become

- a personality cult
- guru-centered
- manipulative
- urgency-driven
- fake scarcity
- vague spirituality
- AI slop
- pseudo-scientific
- anti-scientific
- reductionistically scientific
- lineage-claiming without authority
- therapy masquerading as spiritual practice
- spiritual practice masquerading as therapy
- prosperity gospel
- manifestation influencer culture
- content farm
- brand without canon
- canon without practice
- practice without safety
- aesthetics without truth
- truth without beauty

## Public/private prosperity discipline

Prosperity is an internal strategic aim and a legitimate fruit of the work.

It is not the public trunk of the teaching.

Publicly, Dream Coherence offers recognition, practice, orientation, attunement, and better lives.

Prosperity appears as reciprocity, flourishing, freedom, and the natural exchange that occurs when the work genuinely serves.

## The founder's role

The founder is the Steward of Recognition and the Golden Thread.

The founder supplies:

- lived practice
- taste
- voice
- dreams
- discernment
- strategic judgment
- final approval
- the unique human signal AI cannot replace

The founder should not become trapped in operational maintenance.

## AI's role

AI serves as amplifier, organizer, builder, editor, strategist, and memory interface.

AI may multiply and operationalize recognition.

AI may not replace recognition.

## The repository's role

The repository is the Living Canon.

It holds memory, continuity, and operating intelligence.

Every output should feed back into it.

## Evolution law

Everything in Dream Coherence emanates from the deepest recognition presently available.

As recognition deepens, every expression derived from it is invited to deepen in turn.

The organism remains faithful not to its previous forms, but to the living truth from which those forms arose.
