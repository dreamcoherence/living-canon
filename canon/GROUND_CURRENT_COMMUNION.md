# Ground, Current, Communion

## Status

Canonical identity architecture. Adopted 2026-10-23 (First Threshold handoff, founder decision).
Authority: below Epistemology and Ontology, above Golden Thread (see `canon/CANON_OS.md`).
Definitions of individual terms are governed by the Lexicon. Where this file and the Lexicon disagree, the Lexicon governs until the conflict is logged and ruled.

## Purpose and scope

This file names the three inseparable dimensions through which Dream Coherence reads any experience, and defines coherence in relation to them.

Ground, Current, and Communion are an analytic lens, not a three-step method, not stages, and not a new vocabulary competing with Awareness, Field, or Participation. They are simultaneous in every experience.

## Ground

Awareness as intrinsically present and knowing.

Ground is the role Awareness plays when the question is what any experience is present within and known by. It is not a second term for a second thing. It is Awareness, named by function.

Ground is neither blank absence nor a detached observer standing elsewhere from life.

Epistemic status: that experience is known as it occurs is immediate fact. That awareness is fundamental is Dream Coherence's explicit metaphysical wager.

## Current

Awareness differentiating as sensation, body, image, emotion, desire, thought, speech, action, and world.

Current names intrinsic dynamism. The Ground is not opposed to movement. Current is the Ground differentiating as this living moment.

Current is not a physical frequency, a measurable vibration, an emotional positivity, or a moral rank. It is not the central channel (see `practice/SUBTLE_BODY_AND_ASTRAL.md`).

Backstage lineage grammar: Shaiva spanda. Public prose does not name it (see `research/SOURCE_NOTES/DYCZKOWSKI_DOCTRINE_OF_VIBRATION.md`).

## Communion

The reciprocal encounter of a particular life with other beings and a shared reality it neither privately invents nor controls.

Communion gives nonduality its ethical and relational completion. Whatever reality's ultimate unity, no individual perspective possesses the whole. Other lives have their own inwardness, freedom, and claim upon conduct.

Communion is also the bound on authorship. Authorship is real. The world it acts within is lawful and shared, and authorship that overrides other beings is not greater coherence.

## Coherence

### Root definition (locked)

Coherence is the standing degree of lucidity in the waking dream. (Locked recognition, Coherence Log 2026-06-09.)

### Refined definition (ratified 2026-10-23, Decision D1a)

Coherence is lucidity integrated across embodiment, identity, conduct, relationship, dreaming, and time.

In the terms of this file, that integration shows as the cultivated capacity to remain related to Ground, Current, and Communion as experience changes. Lucidity remains the root. Relatedness to the three dimensions is coherence's signature, not a replacement for its root.

Recognition may be immediate. Coherence is cultivated.

### What coherence includes

- coordinated difference;
- metabolized tension;
- appropriate boundaries;
- responsiveness to changing conditions;
- correction by consequence and by other people;
- continuity of ethical responsibility;
- the ability to act, rest, receive, revise, and remain related.

### What coherence is not

Coherence is not permanent calm, spiritual smoothness, uniformity, or the suppression of conflict. It is coordination across difference, not emotional smoothness. It includes tension, grief, uncertainty, conflict, limitation, and correction.

### The qualities it tends toward

Named in the founder's intake: spaciousness and peace, grace, tenderness without passivity, energy without agitation, determination without fear, stability, confidence, security, vitality, harmony, depth.

These are orientations and capacities. They are not permanent affective states, guaranteed outcomes, or a checklist for self-assessment.

### The shadow of coherence

Alignment can harden. When coherence becomes rigidity, or when one person's order is imposed on others, it stops being coherence and becomes force. Any increase in agency that diminishes love, freedom, humility, or answerability is not greater coherence.

## Failure modes

- Ground without Current becomes dissociation or inert witnesshood.
- Current without Ground becomes agitation, compulsion, or unconscious identification.
- Ground and Current without Communion become solipsism, inflated authorship, or spiritual narcissism.
- Communion without discernment becomes projection, submission, or inflated guidance.

## Relation to lucidity

Lucidity is the faculty by which the condition or construction of experience becomes knowable while experience continues.

Lucidity recognizes the Ground from within experience. Coherence is how much of that lucidity stands, and how far it reaches into the Current and into Communion.

## Person, world, and other beings

- The person is real, embodied, relational, and responsible. Awakening loosens enclosure, not personhood.
- A standpoint is particular. It is not the measure of everything that appears.
- The dream is not privately mine. Other beings are not symbols produced for anyone's development.
- Shared reality is lawful, persistent, materially consequential, and corrigible through other people, records, resistant conditions, and consequences.

## Practice implications

- Ground: recognition practices that include the body and the world rather than withdrawing from them (eyes closed, then eyes open).
- Current: attention to transitions, impulses, and the first movement of desire before it is reduced to explanation.
- Communion: practices that test recognition against relationship, consequence, and the freedom of others.
- Every practice reports back in all three dimensions. A practice that strengthens one by weakening another is flagged.

## Safety boundaries

- Ground language must not produce detachment, numbing, or derealization.
- Current language must not drift into frequency, vibration-as-rank, or energy escalation.
- Communion language must not license certainty about other minds, override consent, or require submission.
- Coherence must never be used to read illness, poverty, trauma, disability, or oppression as incoherence.
