# Epistemology and Rigor

## Purpose

Dream Coherence must remain mystical without becoming sloppy.

It must hold metaphysical depth while preserving epistemic honesty.

## How Dream Coherence knows

Dream Coherence works through five modes of knowing:

1. Direct phenomenology: what is immediately given in experience.
2. Contemplative lineage: what disciplined traditions have found through practice.
3. Lived case study: what changes in the founder's and practitioners' lives.
4. Practical efficacy: what consistently produces useful transformation.
5. Scientific and scholarly correspondence: where contemporary evidence supports, clarifies, or limits claims.

## Claim discipline

Every claim should be tagged implicitly or explicitly by its evidence level:

- Directly verifiable now.
- Practically testable through practice.
- Lineage-supported.
- Scientifically supported.
- Metaphysical wager.
- Poetic or symbolic articulation.

Do not inflate one category into another.

## Key rigor rules carried from V1

- Empirical findings are held at their actual evidence level.
- Cognitive science does not prove the metaphysical position.
- Lineage material is attributed precisely.
- Dream Coherence does not claim to transmit from inside any tradition.
- False lineage claims are never repeated.
- Usable phenomenology does not launder bad provenance.
- Every factual claim that can be checked should be checked.

## The Wager

Dream Coherence may unapologetically make its wager:

That awareness is fundamental and reality has the structure of dreaming.

But the public work should invite experiment rather than demand belief.

## Rigor mechanism

Load-bearing claims should be routed through the Rigor Map before they become canonical.

For each claim:

1. State the claim.
2. Name evidence level.
3. Steelman the strongest counterargument.
4. Name what the claim rests on.
5. Name where it breaks.
6. Refine.
7. Route to canon, practice, marketing, or archive.
