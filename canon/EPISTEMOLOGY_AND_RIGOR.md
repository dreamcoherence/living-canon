# Epistemology and Rigor

## Purpose

Dream Coherence must remain mystical without becoming sloppy.

It must hold metaphysical depth while preserving epistemic honesty.

## The standard of completeness

The framework cannot become perfect by becoming immune to criticism. An unassailable system has usually made itself unfalsifiable.

The mature standard: coherent within its declared scope, explicit about its wagers, safe at its boundaries, corrigible in practice, and capable of meeting its strongest counterarguments.

Five repairs are load-bearing:

1. Separate direct phenomenology from metaphysical interpretation.
2. Separate standpoint, identity, person, and Ground.
3. Affirm shared reality and the irreducibility of other beings.
4. Prevent sacredness, participation, contraction, karma, or desire from sanctifying suffering.
5. Present liberation as a horizon and orientation, never a promised product result.

## How Dream Coherence knows

Dream Coherence works through five modes of knowing:

1. Direct phenomenology: what is immediately given in experience.
2. Contemplative lineage: what disciplined traditions have found through practice.
3. Lived case study: what changes in the founder's and practitioners' lives.
4. Practical efficacy: what consistently produces useful transformation.
5. Scientific and scholarly correspondence: where contemporary evidence supports, clarifies, or limits claims.

## The four epistemic registers

The registers describe the order of inference. They clarify how one kind of claim relates to the next. They do not replace the claim tags below.

### 1. Immediate fact

What is directly available before theoretical interpretation. Experience is occurring. A sensation, thought, image, sound, or knowing is present.

### 2. Phenomenological finding

A disciplined account of how experience presents and changes. Experience is lived from a first-person standpoint and is shaped through attention, embodiment, memory, state, language, history, relationship, expectation, and interpretation.

### 3. Metaphysical wager

An organizing interpretation exceeding direct verification. Dream Coherence wagers that awareness is fundamental, reality is consciousness-saturated, and manifestation is intrinsically expressive.

### 4. Teleological or poetic disclosure

A meaning-bearing way reality may be encountered. Reality may be experienced as wishing to become itself through a particular life.

### The permanent discipline

No register proves the one that follows it.

- Experience being known does not prove that consciousness is ontologically fundamental.
- Practical transformation does not prove the explanation offered for it.
- Cognitive science does not prove idealism.
- Cross-lineage resonance does not establish doctrinal equivalence.
- A dream, symbol, synchronicity, intuition, or felt certainty does not establish an external fact, another person's interior state, or destiny.
- Public prose may move among registers, but it may not conceal the transition.
- Teleological language can be central and unapologetic while remaining honestly identified as wager, revelation, inquiry, or poetic articulation.

## Claim discipline

Every load-bearing claim is classified, internally or explicitly, as one of:

- Directly available now.
- Phenomenological finding.
- Practically testable through practice.
- Lineage-supported.
- Scientifically supported.
- Metaphysical wager.
- Symbolic or poetic articulation.

Do not inflate one category into another.

### Worked classifications

| Claim | Classification |
|---|---|
| Experience is occurring | Directly available |
| Experience is known as it occurs | Directly available, in the minimal sense that present experience is not absent to the one living it. Whether all experience is noticed is contested and is not claimed. |
| Attention moves; appearances change | Directly available |
| Human experience is embodied and relational | Phenomenological finding |
| Identity participates in shaping perception | Phenomenological finding, partly scientifically supported |
| Presence can remain available during activity | Practically testable |
| Faculties can become more integrated | Practically testable |
| Awareness is luminous and self-knowing | Lineage-supported metaphysical formulation |
| Awareness is fundamental | Metaphysical wager |
| Reality wishes to become itself through this life | Teleological or poetic disclosure |
| The world is the Beloved | Poetic or devotional articulation |

## Key rigor rules carried from V1

- Empirical findings are held at their actual evidence level.
- Cognitive science does not prove the metaphysical position.
- Lineage material is attributed precisely.
- Dream Coherence does not claim to transmit from inside any tradition.
- False lineage claims are never repeated.
- Usable phenomenology does not launder bad provenance.
- Every factual claim that can be checked should be checked.

## The Wager

Dream Coherence may unapologetically make its wager:

That awareness is fundamental and reality has the structure of dreaming.

It is the school's explicit stance, honestly named as wager rather than proof.

The public work should invite experiment rather than demand belief.

## The dream analogy

Dream experience does not demonstrate nondual metaphysics. It supplies a powerful analogy and an experimental field. The metaphysical inference remains a wager.

## Rigor mechanism

Load-bearing claims are routed through `canon/RIGOR_MAP.md` before they become canonical.

For each claim:

1. State the claim.
2. Name its register and claim class.
3. Steelman the strongest counterargument.
4. Name what the claim rests on.
5. Name where it breaks.
6. Name its confidence level.
7. Refine.
8. Name its ethical and safety consequence.
9. Name the permitted public language and the prohibited inflation.
10. Route to canon, practice, marketing, or archive.
