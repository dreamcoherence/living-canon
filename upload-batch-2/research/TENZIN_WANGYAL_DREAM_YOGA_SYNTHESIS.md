# Tenzin Wangyal Dream Yoga Synthesis

## Purpose

Preserve and operationalize the V1 synthesis of Tenzin Wangyal Rinpoche's Bön dream yoga teaching.

Tenzin's role in Dream Coherence is methodological. He supplies one of the clearest contemporary practice architectures for continuity between waking, dreaming, and sleep.

## Source placement

Tenzin Wangyal Rinpoche belongs in Circle One / Circle Two:

- Dzogchen and Bön spine source.
- Primary methodology for dream yoga, sleep practice, subtle body, and astral work.

Primary text:

- *The Tibetan Yogas of Dream and Sleep*

Primary V1 source:

- Stanford Contemplation by Design lecture: *Dream Yoga: Lucid dreaming from the Bön Buddhist tradition of Tibet*

## Core recognition

Daytime awareness and nighttime awareness are one continuous practice in two intensities.

Day quality determines night quality.
Night realization influences day realization.
The cycle is virtuous or vicious.
Practice intervenes in the cycle.

The lucid dream is the moment when awareness in dream becomes available enough to act.
The lucid waking moment is the same act in a different medium.

## The file-tag mechanism

Tenzin uses the metaphor that every waking experience creates a file.

When the experience concludes, it is saved under a name or tag: serious, personal, urgent, threatening, unfinished, sacred, beautiful, resolved.

The tag determines how the file returns later, including in dream.

Dream Coherence translation:

Every experience is encoded as it is lived. The name under which it is encoded determines how it reappears in memory, mood, dream, expectation, and identity.

This supports the daily practice of renaming charged moments before sleep.

## Four practice movements from the synthesis

### 1. During the day, recognize the dream

Use moments of intensity, beauty, rupture, desire, fear, pattern, or repetition as portals into lucidity.

Practice phrase:

This is a dream.

Not to deny reality, but to remember its participatory nature.

### 2. Transform the day before sleep

Before sleep, review charged moments.

Feel beneath the story.
Rename what can be renamed.
Set down what can be set down.
Enter sleep with clearer files.

### 3. Enter sleep with intention

Sleep is not collapse.

Sleep is entry.

The practitioner carries intention into the threshold:

May I recognize the dream as dream.
May awareness remain continuous.
May the night show what needs to be seen.

### 4. On waking, recover the thread

Before moving, speaking, or checking a device, turn toward the night.

Receive image, feeling, trace, symbol, or absence.
Write something.
Continuity is trained by returning.

## What is filtered

- strict lineage claims,
- practices requiring transmission or initiation,
- cosmological details not needed for Dream Coherence's first public layer,
- claims that imply guaranteed lucid dreams.

## Safety notes

Do not push advanced dream or sleep practices on unstable practitioners.

Begin with recall, day recognition, body grounding, and simple pre-sleep intention.

Clear light sleep and bardo-style practices belong in advanced material.
