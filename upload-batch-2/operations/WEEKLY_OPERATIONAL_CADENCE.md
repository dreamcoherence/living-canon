# Dream Coherence. Weekly Operational Cadence.

**Version 1.2. May 2026. The week-by-week rhythm of operations. The minimal viable founder time. Updated as the rhythm matures.**

This file holds the operating rhythm of the field. It names what runs when, who does what (founder or infrastructure), and how the week is structured so the field's output stays consistent across years without depending on heroics in any single week.

The cadence is designed for ~5 to 10 hours of founder operational time per week during Phase 1. This is alongside the Stream A (book) work which has its own time budget. Total founder time on Dream Coherence: roughly 20 to 30 hours per week in Phase 1.

---

## The Weekly Beat

A standard week. Days are suggested; specific days adjust to the founder's life rhythm.

### Sunday (or Founder's Reflection Day)

**Founder time: ~60-90 minutes.**

- Record seed material for the week. Voice notes, written reflections, observations from the week's lived practice. Loose, exploratory, in the founder's most lucid speaking voice. Aim for 30-60 minutes of recorded material per week.
- Optional: review the previous week's published output. Note any pieces that landed especially well or that felt off-register.
- Upload transcripts to the designated folder in Google Drive (or paste into Notion seed entry).

### Monday Morning

**Founder time: ~90-120 minutes.**

- Open the Claude project. Run Prompt 1 (Weekly Seed Material Synthesis) on the week's seed material.
- Review the proposed weekly editorial calendar. For each proposed piece, decide: approved as drafted, approved with edits, sent back for full rebuild, or held for future weeks.
- For approved pieces, edits are made inline in Notion or in Claude. The piece moves to "scheduled" status.

### Monday Afternoon (Infrastructure)

**Founder time: minimal.**

- Newsletter draft (approved Monday morning) flows to Beehiiv. Founder reviews in Beehiiv interface, sets send time for Monday evening or Tuesday morning.
- X posts (approved Monday morning) flow to Buffer or Hypefury queue, scheduled across the week per the cadence pattern (5-10 posts per week, distributed Tuesday-Saturday).
- Instagram pieces (approved Monday morning) flow to scheduler with image concepts queued for generation.

### Tuesday-Friday (Cadence Runs)

**Founder time: ~30-60 minutes total across the week.**

- Content publishes per schedule. Newsletter ships Monday or Tuesday. X and Instagram run per cadence.
- Founder spot-checks the publishing surfaces ~once per day for any urgent items (substantive replies needing personal response, technical issues, anything off).
- Founder responds personally to 2-5 newsletter subscriber replies per week (the highest signal in the field).
- Founder writes for Stream A (book) in dedicated sessions, separately.

### Friday Evening or Saturday

**Founder time: ~20-30 minutes.**

- Open Claude project. Run Prompt 10 (Weekly Strategic Review).
- Read the review. Note any adjustments for the next week.
- Capture any Coherence Log entries that surfaced from the week.

### Weekend Off-Hours

- The cadence runs without founder. The infrastructure handles published content.
- Founder rests, lives, practices.

## The Monthly Beat

**Last week of each month. Founder time: ~90-120 minutes.**

- Open Claude project. Run Prompt 11 (Monthly Field Review).
- Read the review. Decide on strategic adjustments.
- Update relevant project files if any locked decisions evolved this month.
- Pull any Coherence Log entries that need integration. Run an integration pass if more than ~20 entries have accumulated.
- Set the next month's editorial direction in Notion (theme, target output volume, key milestones).

---

## The Quarterly Beat

**End of each quarter. Founder time: ~half-day.**

- Deeper review against the phase the project is in.
- Review against `25_STRATEGIC_ARCHITECTURE.md` to see if the architecture itself needs adjustment.
- Adjust the next quarter's plan.
- Consider whether any phase-exit conditions are being met or are slipping.

---

## What the Infrastructure Handles

Without founder involvement after initial setup:

- Content scheduling across all platforms (Buffer/Hypefury for X, Instagram scheduler, Beehiiv for newsletter)
- Automated welcome email to new newsletter subscribers
- Automated standard responses to common inquiries (using the templates from Prompt 8)
- Analytics aggregation for the weekly and monthly reviews
- Image generation for atmospheric Instagram pieces (against the brand visual discipline)
- Newsletter formatting and delivery
- Subscriber list hygiene (unsubscribes, bounces)
- Cross-posting where appropriate (e.g., a newsletter issue can have a complementary X thread auto-generated)

---

## What Stays in Founder's Hands

Permanently:

- Recording seed material (the only input that cannot be automated)
- Reviewing and approving the week's content
- Responding personally to high-signal correspondence
- Stream A (book) work
- Strategic decisions
- The Coherence Log integration disposition
- Final say on any output

The founder is in the work daily. The infrastructure removes the friction of producing and distributing what the work generates.

## The Time Budget Across Phase 1

A typical week in Phase 1 looks like:

| Activity | Time | Stream |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday seed material capture | 60-90 min | Stream B |
| Monday content review and approval | 90-120 min | Stream B |
| Daily spot-checks and correspondence | 30-60 min total | Stream B |
| Friday weekly review | 20-30 min | Stream B |
| Stream A book writing | 10-15 hrs | Stream A |
| Daily practice | 30-60 min | Founder lived |
| **Total Stream B operational time** | **~3.5-5 hrs/week** | |
| **Total Stream A writing time** | **~10-15 hrs/week** | |
| **Total practice and reflection** | **~3.5-7 hrs/week** | |
| **Total founder time on Dream Coherence** | **~17-27 hrs/week** | |

Within reach of a sustainable life. Lower than the 20-30 hours per week the strategic architecture estimates if the founder is efficient. Higher in months when the book is in heavy drafting.

## What to Avoid

**Skipping the Sunday seed capture.** This is the engine. Without it, the pipeline starves.

**Skipping the Monday review.** Without it, the infrastructure outputs without calibration. Quality drifts.

**Pulling content from the unfinished book.** Stream A's draft material does not go into Stream B until each chapter is locked. Keeping the streams separate protects the book's voice integrity.

**Adding surfaces too quickly.** Phase 1 runs newsletter + X + Instagram + occasional audio + monthly long-form essay. That's enough. Adding a podcast, a YouTube channel, a Substack, or a TikTok in Phase 1 dilutes attention without adding value.

**Over-engineering the operations.** If something takes more than its time budget, simplify. The infrastructure exists to compress operations, not to add complexity.

**Treating the cadence as inflexible.** Some weeks the seed material is rich and produces more output than scheduled. Other weeks the founder is in a deep practice or in heavy book drafting and the week's output is lighter. The cadence is a baseline, not a contract.

---

## What to Adjust When Things Slip

**If newsletter open rates drop below 40%:** Run the Monthly Review with attention to whether the newsletter has drifted in voice or topic. Tighten the synthesis prompt. Re-anchor to what the deepest practitioners are responding to.

**If social engagement plateaus:** Acceptable for a contemplative brand. The work is not optimized for virality. If engagement collapses entirely, audit voice for slippage. Possibly the pieces have become predictable; reintroduce variety in form and theme.

**If founder's time budget is exceeding 30 hours per week consistently:** The pipeline is doing too much. Simplify. Cut one surface or reduce cadence on one surface.

**If founder's seed material capture is missed for more than 2 weeks:** The cadence cannot run. Pause the pipeline. Investigate what is breaking. Restore capture before resuming.

**If a piece publishes that violates the brand voice:** Pull it immediately. Note the failure in the Coherence Log. Adjust the synthesis prompt to prevent recurrence.

Held to this cadence, the field grows steadily across years. The audience builds. The brand becomes recognizable. The body of work accumulates. The founder maintains a sustainable life. The wager becomes reachable not through unsustainable bursts but through compounded consistency.

The cadence is the work. The work is the cadence.
