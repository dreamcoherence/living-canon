## Part Six. The Growth Mechanics (Internal, Not Published)

What actually compounds under the brand discipline, ranked by expected leverage:

1. **Thread architecture on X.** Threads are the highest organic-distribution format available without engagement bait. Every long-form essay should yield one thread within 48 hours of publishing. The essay-and-thread sibling pattern in the Doorway Map is the single most important habit in this system.

2. **Screenshot propagation.** The voice is engineered for quotation. Every aphorism image should be legible at feed size and beautiful enough that strangers repost it with the watermark intact. The watermark is the distribution mechanism. This is how faceless brands travel.

3. **Carousel saves.** Instagram's algorithm weights saves above likes for accounts in this category. Carousels that function as reference material (the evening sequence, the four registers) accumulate saves for months. Prioritize carousels that people return to over carousels that people merely agree with.

4. **Newsletter as the only conversion surface.** Social converts to Beehiiv; Beehiiv converts to Threshold. Never collapse the two jobs. The bio link goes to Seven Nights capture and nowhere else.

5. **Consistency over intensity.** The 36-month targets (75K X, 50K Instagram, 30K newsletter) are reached by cadence held without exception, not by any single piece performing. Two missed weeks costs more than any viral post gains.

6. **The honest ceiling.** Millions do not come from this layer. They come from the book, from YouTube long-form, and from selective podcast appearances per the Founder Presence Protocol, all of which this layer feeds and none of which it replaces. When those surfaces open, the social archive becomes the proof-of-depth that converts their audiences.
